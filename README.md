# D&D 3.5e Tactical Combat Prototype

A Unity prototype of D&D 3.5e tactical combat on a square grid (5-ft squares, PHB alternating 1-2 diagonal cost). You build a party of four, pick an encounter, and fight it turn by turn under 3.5e rules. Then you collect loot and XP, level up, rest, shop, craft, and pick the next encounter. There is no campaign, exploration map, main menu, game-over screen or save system. The loop runs until you quit, and all state is lost when Play mode stops. If the whole party dies, the turn indicator shows "DEFEAT!" and the game stops responding; there is no retry, so stop Play mode.

## Status

- Prototype. The last feature commit is 0dd8e76 (2026-06-01, metamagic and wand fixes). No feature work has been committed since.
- About 654 C# files and 292K lines under `Assets/Scripts`, all compiled into one assembly (Assembly-CSharp, no asmdefs).
- Most of the code was generated by AI coding agents. The project is now maintained by an AI coding agent working with the owner (hoyty1). Agent instructions are in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
- The grid has been square since f5e29ed (2026-04-09). Identifiers that still say "Hex" (`RangeCalculator.FeetPerHex`, `RaceData.BaseSpeedHexes`) are legacy aliases.

## Features

What follows comes from reading the code. Most of it has not been re-checked in Play mode. Open gaps are listed in [docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md](docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

**Combat.** Initiative, standard/move/full-round actions, 5-ft step and withdraw. A swift-action slot exists in `ActionEconomy`, but no action spends it (only Dimension Door sets it, as part of its action lockout); quickened spells use a separate once-per-round flag. House rules: the first attack costs a standard action, and a second attack spends the move action and turns the turn into a full attack (the per-creature attack sequence in `Combat/Core/AttackPool.cs`, run by `CharacterController.TryPayForNextAttack`/`TryCommitAttack`; trip, disarm, sunder and grapple use the same steps); luck bonuses stack (`Spell/BonusType.cs`). Melee, ranged (range increments), thrown and natural attacks; iterative attacks, two-weapon fighting and flurry; crits with confirmation; flanking, sneak attack and concealment; reach-aware attacks of opportunity. Maneuvers: grapple (full sub-menu), trip, disarm, sunder, bull rush, overrun, feint, charge, coup de grace and aid another. Turn Undead (no rebuke mode), 51 condition types (`CombatConditionType`, 48 of them with rules in `ConditionRules`), and a damage pipeline with DR, energy resistance, immunities, temporary HP and nonlethal damage. Not implemented: surprise rounds, readied actions, delay and tumbling past AoOs. Mounted combat has a rules library and tests but no way to mount in play.

**Characters and classes.** All 7 PHB races and 101 feats (`Character/Feats/FeatDefinitions.cs`). Some feats can be taken but have no runtime effect yet: Combat Expertise, Blind-Fight, Two-Weapon Defense, Quick Draw, Run, Spell Focus and Skill Focus. Characters multiclass, earn XP by CR against party level, and level up to 20. Custom-created characters are built at level 1 and then levelled to 3 through the level-up screens. 16 classes are registered in `ClassRegistry.Init`:

| Class | Status in play |
|---|---|
| Fighter, Rogue, Barbarian, Cleric, Wizard, Sorcerer | Playable. Rage, sneak attack, evasion, Turn Undead, cleric domains (22) and spontaneous cure/inflict work. Known gap: the Destruction domain smite adds no damage. |
| Monk | Playable, but features do not scale: d6 unarmed damage at every level, +10 ft fast movement from level 1, and exactly two flurry attacks. |
| Bard | Spontaneous casting works. Of 9 bardic music abilities, only Inspire Courage can be started. BAB uses the 1/2 progression instead of 3/4 (`CharacterStats.CalculateClassBaseAttackBonus`). |
| Druid | Prepared casting works, up to 2nd-level spells. As with clerics, creation has no spell step; spells are prepared in the pre-combat hub. Wild shape and the animal companion are data only. |
| Ranger, Paladin | Spell slots are shown, but there is no preparation or casting. Favored enemy, companion, Smite Evil, Lay on Hands and Divine Grace have no effect in play. |
| Adept, Aristocrat, Commoner, Expert, Warrior | Registered for NPCs. PCs cannot take them (filtered in `CharacterCreationUI` and `LevelUpUI`). Adepts cannot cast. |

**Spells.** `Spell/Database/SpellDatabase_*.cs` holds 292 spells of levels 0-9; 47 are flagged `IsPlaceholder`, with no mechanics or only generic dice. Casting is prepared (Wizard, Cleric, Druid) or spontaneous (Sorcerer, Bard). Spell effects cover persistent areas (fogs, Web, Grease, walls) and Summon Monster / Summon Nature's Ally I-IV. Caps:
- The Wizard and Druid slot tables stop at 2nd-level spells.
- Clerics get higher slots but only know spells up to 2nd level.
- Domain spells exist only for levels 1-2.

The 9 PHB metamagic feats work when applied at rest preparation, in the crafting workshop, through scrolls and wands, or in the F12 panel. On-the-fly metamagic for sorcerers and bards does not work. NPCs never cast `SpellTargetType.Area` spells and never use metamagic. Some spells have bespoke handlers that the dispatch never reaches; see [docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md](docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

**Creatures and AI.** About 290 hand-written `NPCDefinition` records are registered from `NPCDatabaseCustom.cs` (custom and test NPCs) and the Monster Manual files `NPCDatabase_A.cs` to `NPCDatabase_Y.cs`. On top of those come 60 generated dragons (10 colors x 6 age categories, Wyrmling to Adult), 8 skeleton, 7 zombie and 8 lycanthrope variants, and 6 summon aliases. That makes roughly 380-390 creature IDs (a static count, not verified at runtime); the log line `[NPCDatabase] Initialized with N NPC types` gives the exact count. Celestial and fiendish templates are applied at spawn. Mechanical special abilities include breath weapons, poison, disease, paralysis, energy drain, improved grab, pounce, rake, swarms, auras, regeneration, DR and SR. Many spell-like abilities exist as text only. The 19 AI profile classes in `AI/Profiles` drive NPC turns. NPCs flee only when a condition forces it (Frightened, Panicked, turned undead); the per-profile flee thresholds are never read. NPCs do not use consumables.

**Equipment and magic items.**

| Items | Reachable in play? |
|---|---|
| PHB armor and shields | All 12 armors and 6 shield types are defined. The store sells all of them except padded armor. |
| PHB weapons | Most simple and martial weapons are defined; the store stocks 16 of them. Exotic weapons: only spiked chain and whip. |
| +1/+2 weapons, armor and shields | Sold in the store for the stocked base items. Crafting can upgrade gear to +5. |
| Masterwork and special materials | Not sold. NPCs of CR 1+ can spawn with masterwork or special-material gear (`GameManager.NPCSetup.cs`), which you can loot. |
| Potions, scrolls, wands (generated from the spell database) | Buy, use in combat, craft. Crafted potions cannot be used. |
| Rings (54 variants), wondrous items (about 170, including 10 test items) | Sold. Passive bonuses apply when equipped. Activated abilities are unreachable. |
| Rods (33) | Sold, but have no effect. |
| Staves (20), specific named items (60) | Defined, but nothing creates them. |
| Magic items from treasure | Placeholders with a name, a guessed type and slot, a value and (for magic items) an enhancement bonus, but no mechanics. Sell them. |

**Encounters and the meta loop.** Up to 15 enemies on a 20x20 grid (there are 15 preallocated enemy slots; extra enemy IDs are dropped). Encounter selection offers:
- 87 presets, about two dozen of them test setups.
- A CR/EL random generator.
- DMG dungeon tables for levels 1-8 (`Assets/StreamingAssets/dungeon_encounters.csv`).
- A custom builder (up to 8 creatures).

Cancelling selection loads the `goblin_raiders` preset. The pre-combat hub has:
- A store: party gold starts at 1000 gp, and items sell at 50%.
- The party stash.
- Spell preparation.
- A crafting workshop with 8 item creation feats. Crafting spends each character's own `ComponentGold`, not party gold.

After a victory you get DMG treasure by EL (coins go straight to party gold; gems and art are valued with the party's best Appraise check), a loot window (treasure plus enemy equipment) into the stash, XP, any level-ups, and an automatic full rest. Planar travel, creature traps and the DMG NPC-by-level templates have code but no gameplay entry point.

## Requirements

- Unity **6000.4.0f1** exactly (`ProjectSettings/ProjectVersion.txt`). Other versions will offer to upgrade the project.
- Packages resolve from `Packages/manifest.json`: Input System 1.11.2, uGUI 2.0.0, TextMeshPro, 2D Sprite, 2D Tilemap, plus Unity AI Assistant (`com.unity.ai.assistant`, pre-release) and AI Inference (`com.unity.ai.inference`), which provide the Unity MCP bridge the maintaining agent uses. Active Input Handling is set to Both.
- No IDE package is included, so Unity generates no `.sln`. For IntelliSense, add `com.unity.ide.visualstudio` or `com.unity.ide.rider` locally.
- Git Bash (on Windows) to run `tools/compile_check.sh`.

## Getting started

1. Clone: `git clone https://github.com/hoyty1/dnd35prototype.git`. The download is large (several hundred MB), mostly the history of `.abacus.donotdelete`.
2. In Unity Hub, choose Add, select the clone folder, and open it with 6000.4.0f1. The first import takes a while.
3. Open `Assets/Scenes/MainScene.unity`. It contains only `Main Camera` and `GameBootstrap`.
4. Press Play. `SceneBootstrap.Awake` (`Assets/Scripts/_Core/SceneBootstrap.cs`) builds the grid, characters, UI and `GameManager` at runtime.
5. On the character creation screen, do one of the following:
   - **Play Now!** gives an instant level-3 Fighter/Rogue/Cleric/Wizard party.
   - **Quick Start** lets you pick pre-made characters by class.
   - Build four characters step by step.

`.meta` files are committed (since 2026-10-03), so the scene's reference to `SceneBootstrap` resolves on a fresh clone. If you add or move scripts outside Unity, open the project in Unity before committing so it creates or keeps the matching `.meta` files, and commit them with the scripts.

To update, run `git pull` and reopen Unity.

## Controls and debug tools

| Input | Effect |
|---|---|
| Left click | Select actions, cells and targets |
| Right click or Esc | Cancel targeting, movement, area or summon placement (`InputService.HandleCancelInput`) |
| C | Character sheet (stats, skills, feats, spells, and the inventory embedded in it) for the active PC on the player's turn |
| K | Skills panel, same condition |
| I | Wired to the standalone inventory, but in practice it does nothing, because `SceneBootstrap` embeds `InventoryUI` in the character sheet and `GameManager.HandleInventoryInput` returns early. Use C instead (read from the code, not checked in Play mode). |
| WASD or arrows, left-drag | Pan the camera (`Utilities/CameraController.cs`) |
| Mouse wheel, `=`/`-`, numpad +/- | Zoom. R resets the camera. |
| Enter / Esc in the loot window | Loot All / close |
| 0-9 / Esc in the bull rush prompt | 0 pushes 5 ft and stays, N moves with the target and pushes it N squares / Esc pushes 5 ft and stays |
| **F12** | Spell Testing Panel: cast spells directly with metamagic toggles, a +4 casting-stat boost and a temporary caster setup (`UI/Spells/SpellTestingPanel.cs`, `_Core/GameManager.TestPanel.cs`) |
| **Left Ctrl+H** | Cycle the level-up HP mode Roll > Average > Maximum (`Utilities/DebugCommands.cs`; legacy input only) |

Test encounter presets: in encounter selection, 22 preset IDs (for example `grapple_test` or `sleep_spell_test`; all end in `_test` except `obscuring_mist_ranged_only`) are matched in `GameManager.ApplyEncounterPreset` and run a matching `Configure*TestParty` method in `_Core/GameManager.TestConfigs.cs`. That method replaces the party with a scenario setup. Other test-like presets (such as `test_2_goblins` or `xp_levelup_test`) only change the enemies. The crafting workshop also has a DEBUG toggle that makes crafting free and instant and allows every spell.

## Testing

- **Compile check:** `bash tools/compile_check.sh` compiles Assembly-CSharp headlessly with Unity's bundled Roslyn in about 5 s. It needs Unity to have opened the project once (for `Library/Bee`) and the editor at `F:/Unity/<version>/Editor`, or `UNITY_DIR` set. At 0dd8e76 it reports 0 errors and 508 warnings.
- **Runtime suites:** `Assets/Scripts/Tests` holds 101 files: 94 static suites with `RunAll()`, the `ServiceTestRunner` aggregator (also `RunAll()`, runs 9 Services suites), 3 MonoBehaviour suites whose `Start()` calls `RunAllTests()`, and 3 utility files. There is no Unity Test Framework and nothing runs them automatically. Call one from a temporary MonoBehaviour in Play mode and filter the Console for errors; each failure is a `Debug.LogError`. Some suites are stale or contain placeholder asserts.
- Details are in [docs/TESTING.md](docs/TESTING.md).
