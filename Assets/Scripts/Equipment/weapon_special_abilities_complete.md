# D&D 3.5e — Weapon Special Abilities Complete Catalog
### Source: DMG v.3.5 pp. 223–226, SRD

---

## General Rules

- A weapon must have at least a **+1 enhancement bonus** before any special ability can be added.
- The **total effective bonus** (enhancement + all special ability equivalents) **cannot exceed +10**.
- **Market Price** = (total effective bonus)² × 2,000 gp + masterwork cost (300 gp) + base weapon cost.
- Special abilities do **not** increase the attack/damage enhancement bonus unless they explicitly say so (e.g., Bane).
- Caster Level for creation = max(3 × enhancement bonus, CL required by special ability).
- Magic ammunition is destroyed on a successful hit; 50% break chance on a miss.

## Ranged Weapon Special Abilities

### +1 Bonus Equivalent

| # | Name | Description | Mechanics | Prerequisites | Restrictions |
|---|------|-------------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| 28 | **Distance** | Double range increment | **Doubles the range increment** of the weapon. | Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *clairaudience/clairvoyance* | CL 6th. Ranged only. |
| 29 | **Returning** | Thrown weapon returns | A **thrown weapon** with this property returns to the thrower's hand at the beginning of their next turn. Only works on thrown weapons. | Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *telekinesis* | CL 7th. Thrown weapons only. |
| 30 | **Seeking** | Ignores concealment/cover | Weapon **negates miss chances** from concealment (including total concealment, blur, displacement, etc.). Target still gets AC benefit of cover. | Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *true seeing* | CL 12th. Ranged only. |

### Shared: Work on Both Melee and Ranged

The following abilities from the Melee table also appear on the Ranged table:
- **Bane** (+1) — works identically on ranged weapons/ammunition
- **Flaming** (+1), **Frost** (+1), **Shock** (+1) — elemental damage on every hit
- **Flaming Burst** (+2), **Icy Burst** (+2), **Shocking Burst** (+2) — elemental + crit burst
- **Ghost Touch** (+1) — strikes incorporeal
- **Holy** (+2), **Unholy** (+2), **Anarchic** (+2), **Axiomatic** (+2) — alignment damage
- **Merciful** (+1), **Thundering** (+1) — special damage modes
- **Speed** (+3) — extra attack on full attack
- **Brilliant Energy** (+4) — ignores armor/shields

## Implementation Priority for Prototype

### Tier 1 — Core Combat (implement first)
- **Flaming / Frost / Shock** — straightforward +1d6 elemental
- **Flaming Burst / Icy Burst / Shocking Burst** — elemental + crit bonus
- **Holy / Unholy / Anarchic / Axiomatic** — alignment damage (need alignment system)
- **Keen** — crit range doubling (already have crit system)
- **Bane** — creature type targeting

### Tier 2 — Tactical Combat
- **Speed** — extra attack (already have haste)
- **Defending** — AC transfer
- **Ghost Touch** — incorporeal interaction
- **Disruption** — undead destruction
- **Merciful** — nonlethal toggle
- **Vicious** — risk/reward damage
- **Wounding** — Con damage

### Tier 3 — Advanced/Situational
- **Vorpal** — head severing
- **Dancing** — autonomous weapon
- **Brilliant Energy** — ignore armor
- **Spell Storing** — spell discharge
- **Thundering** — sonic/deafen on crit
- **Mighty Cleaving** — extra cleave (already have cleave)

### Tier 4 — Ranged-Specific
- **Distance** — range doubling
- **Returning** — thrown weapon return
- **Seeking** — ignore concealment

### Tier 5 — Niche
- **Throwing** — melee → throwable
- **Ki Focus** — monk synergy
