# D&D 3.5e — Shield Special Abilities Complete Catalog
### Source: DMG v.3.5 pp. 220–222, SRD

---

## General Rules

- A shield must have at least a **+1 enhancement bonus** before any special ability can be added.
- The **total effective bonus** (enhancement + all bonus-equivalent special abilities) **cannot exceed +10**.
- **Market Price (bonus-based)** = (total effective bonus)² × 1,000 gp + masterwork cost (150 gp) + base shield cost.
- Shield enhancement bonuses **stack with** armor enhancement bonuses (they are different bonus types: shield vs armor).
- A shield can also be built as a **magic weapon** (separate enhancement bonus and cost for the weapon portion).
- Flat-cost abilities do **not** count toward the +10 cap.

## Shield-Only Abilities Detail

These 6 abilities are exclusive to shields and have no armor equivalent:

### Arrow Catching (+1)
- Provides +1 **enhancement bonus** to AC against ranged attacks (stacks with shield enhancement)
- Can redirect ranged attacks targeting adjacent allies to the shield bearer
- **Implementation**: Modify ranged attack AC calculation, add ally protection mechanic

### Bashing (+1)
- Shield treated as **two size categories larger** for bash damage:
  - Light shield: 1d3 → **1d6**
  - Heavy shield: 1d4 → **1d8**
- Shield's **AC enhancement bonus** also applies as **weapon enhancement bonus** on bash attacks
- Shield bash does not cause loss of shield AC bonus
- **Implementation**: Modify shield bash damage dice, apply enhancement to bash attacks

### Blinding (+1)
- **2/day** command-word activation
- All creatures within **20 feet** (not wielder) make **DC 14 Reflex save**
- **Failure**: Blinded for **1d4 rounds**
- **Implementation**: Area effect, save-based condition application

### Animated (+2)
- Command-word: shield **floats and protects** without being held
- Frees both hands for two-handed weapons, dual wielding, or spellcasting
- Still applies ACP, ASF, nonproficiency penalties
- Only **one animated shield** per character
- **Implementation**: Remove shield from hand slot, maintain AC bonus, keep penalties

### Arrow Deflection (+2)
- **Once per round** when struck by a ranged weapon
- **DC 20 Reflex save** to deflect (negate all damage)
- Must be aware of attack, not flat-footed
- Does not work against siege weapons or massive projectiles
- **Implementation**: Post-hit reaction check, save negates damage

### Reflecting (+5)
- **Once per day** can reflect a spell back at the caster
- Functions as *spell turning*
- Must be aware of the spell and not flat-footed
- Works on targeted spells only (not area effects)
- **Implementation**: Spell reaction trigger, retarget spell at caster

- **Fortification (Light/Moderate/Heavy)** — shared with armor, high combat impact
- **Bashing** — shield bash combat

### Tier 2 — Tactical Shield
- **Animated** — frees hands (major tactical value)
- **Arrow Deflection** — ranged defense
- **Arrow Catching** — party protection

### Tier 3 — Magic Defense
- **Spell Resistance (all tiers)** — shared with armor
- **Blinding** — area crowd control
- **Ghost Touch** — incorporeal defense

### Tier 4 — Advanced
- **Reflecting** — spell reflection
- **Wild** — druid compatibility
- **Energy Resistance (all)** — shared with armor
- **Undead Controlling** — necromantic
