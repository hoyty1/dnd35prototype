# D&D 3.5e — Armor Special Abilities Complete Catalog
### Source: DMG v.3.5 pp. 217–220, SRD

---

## General Rules

- Armor must have at least a **+1 enhancement bonus** before any special ability can be added.
- The **total effective bonus** (enhancement + all bonus-equivalent special abilities) **cannot exceed +10**.
- **Market Price (bonus-based)** = (total effective bonus)² × 1,000 gp + masterwork cost (150 gp) + base armor cost.
- Some abilities have a **flat GP cost** instead of a bonus equivalent — these do **not** count toward the +10 cap.
- Enhancement bonuses improve AC; special abilities do **not** improve AC (they grant other benefits).
- All magic armor is masterwork (ACP reduced by 1 already included in enhancement).
- Caster Level for creation = max(3 × enhancement bonus, CL required by special ability).

## Fortification Progression Detail

| Level | % Negate | Bonus Equiv | Effective Against |
|-------|:--------:|:-----------:|-------------------|
| Light | 25% | +1 | Critical hits and sneak attacks |
| Moderate | 75% | +3 | Critical hits and sneak attacks |
| Heavy | 100% | +5 | Critical hits and sneak attacks |

- Roll d100 each time a critical hit or sneak attack is confirmed
- If roll ≤ percentage, the attack deals **normal damage** instead (not a critical, no sneak attack dice)
- The attacker still hits, just loses the extra damage from the crit/sneak
- Fortification levels do **not** stack — only the highest applies

## Energy Resistance Progression Detail

| Tier | Resistance | Cost per Type | 5 Types Total Cost |
|------|:----------:|:-------------:|:------------------:|
| Base | 10 | 18,000 gp | 90,000 gp |
| Improved | 20 | 42,000 gp | 210,000 gp |
| Greater | 30 | 66,000 gp | 330,000 gp |

- **Energy types**: Acid, Cold, Electricity, Fire, Sonic
- Each resistance is a **separate ability** — you can have Fire Resistance and Cold Resistance on the same armor
- Resistance from armor **stacks with** resistance from other sources (spells, race, etc.) per standard D&D stacking rules
- Each instance subtracts the listed amount from damage of that energy type **per attack/effect**

## Implementation Priority for Prototype

### Tier 1 — Core Defense (implement first)
- **Fortification (Light/Moderate/Heavy)** — critical hit negation (high combat impact)
- **Energy Resistance (all 5 types, all 3 tiers)** — direct damage reduction

### Tier 2 — Spell Defense
- **Spell Resistance (13/15/17/19)** — anti-magic defense
- **Invulnerability** — DR 5/magic

### Tier 3 — Utility
- **Ghost Touch (Armor)** — incorporeal defense
- **Shadow / Silent Moves / Slick** (all tiers) — skill bonuses
- **Glamered** — cosmetic

### Tier 4 — Advanced
- **Wild** — druid wild shape compatibility
- **Etherealness** — 1/day ethereal
- **Undead Controlling** — necromantic control
