2. Mindless Undead  (INT —)

18 creatures  |  CR 1/3 – 4  |  AI Profile: UndeadMindless
No intelligence at all. Operates on pure necromantic animation. Cannot think, plan, or adapt. Attacks nearest target with no tactical awareness. Zombies are limited to single actions per round; Skeletons benefit from Improved Initiative.

🧠 AI Behavior: UndeadMindless

  • Target Selection: Attack nearest enemy — no prioritization whatsoever
  • Movement: Move directly toward closest target; no flanking, no tactical positioning
  • Special Abilities: Triggered automatically (slam attack) — no decision making
  • Retreat: Never retreats — fights until destroyed
  • Group Tactics: None — each creature acts independently
  • Zombies only: Single standard action per round (move OR attack, not both)
  • Skeletons only: Full actions; benefit from Improved Initiative (+4 to initiative rolls)

New AI Profiles Required

✓ UndeadMindless (Already Implemented)

Already correctly implemented for all zombies and skeletons. Handles single action restriction for zombies and Improved Initiative for skeletons. Attack nearest target, no retreat, no tactics.

▶ UndeadIncorporeal (New — Priority 1)

Applies to: Shadow, Greater Shadow, Allip, Wraith, Spectre, Ghost

Required Behaviors

  • Move through walls to reach isolated or unsupported targets (spellcasters behind lines)
  • Retreat into solid objects when below 40% HP to avoid damage
  • Prefer targets vulnerable to touch attacks; track magic weapon users on enemy side
  • Create Spawn awareness — avoid overkilling when undead minions are tactically valuable
  • Strength Damage users (Shadow, Greater Shadow): target STR-dependent melee fighters
  • Energy Drain users: prioritize targets with most character levels
  • Ghost special: Frightful Moan as opener; toggle Manifestation to avoid focus fire

▶ UndeadTactical (New — Priority 2)

Applies to: Ghoul, Ghast, Wight, Wight Dreadwalker, Vampire Spawn

Required Behaviors

  • Cycle paralysis attacks between multiple targets rather than focusing one enemy
  • Ghast: position for Stench aura to affect maximum enemy count simultaneously
  • Energy Drain users: concentrate negative levels on key party members (healers, casters)
  • Vampire Spawn: attempt Dominate before entering melee; target the party's strongest fighter
  • Pack coordination: one paralysis attacker locks down target, others attack the helpless enemy
  • Deliver coup de grâce against paralyzed or helpless enemies when opportunity arises

▶ UndeadBrute (New — Priority 3)

Applies to: Mummy, Bodak

Required Behaviors

  • Mummy: advance into melee range, let Despair aura trigger on clustered enemies, then slam for Mummy Rot
  • Bodak: position for Death Gaze line-of-sight to affect maximum enemies per round facing
  • Low INT limits tactical complexity — straightforward but effective melee/gaze approach
  • Neither retreats; rely on DR and Turn Resistance for passive survival
  • Bodak: avoid direct sunlight (takes 1 Con damage per round in sunlight)
