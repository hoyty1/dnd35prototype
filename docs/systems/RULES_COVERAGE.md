# Rules coverage: PHB, DMG and MM

This is the map of how close the game is to the D&D 3.5e core rules (Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual). Use it to plan work toward the project goal: battles that play by the core rules, encounters generated by the DMG random-encounter rules, and between-battle party management by PHB/DMG rules, with much deeper enemy AI. It is a summary. The detail lives in the systems docs ([AI](AI.md), [ENCOUNTERS](ENCOUNTERS.md), [PARTY_MANAGEMENT](PARTY_MANAGEMENT.md), [SPELLS_AND_METAMAGIC](SPELLS_AND_METAMAGIC.md), [CREATURES](CREATURES.md), [MAGIC_ITEMS](MAGIC_ITEMS.md)), the [architecture parts](../ARCHITECTURE.md#parts) and the [issue files](../issues/). Known defects are cited by ID (an ID such as `CMB-004` lives in `docs/issues/CMB.md`).

Everything here was established by reading code. Nothing was verified in Play mode. Rules are cited by book and chapter from memory of the 3.5 books; check the book before encoding a number.

## 1. How to read this

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Complete** | The rule is implemented, reachable in normal play, and matches the book apart from cosmetic details. |
| **Mostly** | The core of the rule works in play. The gaps are edge cases or rarely reached. |
| **Partial** | A meaningful part works, but significant parts are missing, wrong or reachable only in some paths. |
| **Minimal** | Data, helpers or scaffolding exist, but little or nothing affects play. |
| **Missing** | Nothing in code. |

**deviates** (column "Dev.") marks an area where implemented behaviour gives different results from the book in normal play. Every deviation is a bug: there are no house rules (owner directive 2026-10-09); the owner's rulings where the books are silent are listed in [section 4.1](#41-owner-interpretations-of-raw-silences).

Deviations found or re-checked while writing this doc are filed like any other defect and cited by ID; [section 4.3](#43-deviations-found-for-this-doc) gives the code-level detail for each.

Paths are relative to `Assets/Scripts/`.

**Keeping this doc current.** When a fix closes an issue cited here, update the row's status and IDs in the same commit, as the issue rules in [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) require for every doc that cites an ID. When the owner confirms a house rule, move it from [4.2](#42-unconfirmed-deviations) to [4.1](#41-deliberate-house-rules) with the date. When a new rule area is implemented, add or upgrade its row here and give the detail in the matching systems doc, not here.

## 3. Conditions (DMG ch.8)

**Model.** Conditions are `StatusEffect` instances in `CharacterStats.ActiveConditions`, typed by `CombatConditionType` (`Combat/Conditions/CombatConditionType.cs`). Their rules are `ConditionDefinition` rows in `ConditionRules` (`Combat/StatusEffects/StatusEffect.cs`): 49 definitions (including `None`) plus the aliases KnockedDown to Prone and Grappling to Grappled. Each row carries attack, AC, save, skill, ability-check and initiative modifiers, a movement multiplier, `Prevents*` flags, `DeniesDexToAc`, `GrantsCombatAdvantage` and `CoupDeGraceVulnerable`. `CharacterStats` sums them on read (`ConditionAttackPenalty`, `ConditionACPenalty`, the save sums, `ConditionMovementMultiplier`, smallest multiplier wins). Some numbers live outside the table: the +4 melee bonus against helpless targets, prone AC (`CombatCalculationService.ProneACModifier`), pinned AC, the blinded 50% miss chance, and fatigue STR/DEX penalties (`CharacterStats.StrengthConditionPenalty`, `DexterityConditionPenalty`). The apply chain and the poison and disease mapping are in [combat: conditions](../architecture/combat-and-grid.md#conditions-and-status-effects).

**Durations.** Since 2026-10-09 every condition, whatever applied it, ticks once per round at the initiative count of the creature whose turn it was when it was applied (normally the applier), so an N-round condition ends just before that count N rounds later (PHB p.138; `TurnDurations`, CMB-006; checked in Play mode by `TurnDurationRulesTests` and `rules/condition-duration-turn-relative`, batch 8, 2026-10-09). A 1-round stun or daze therefore costs the target its next turn in either initiative order.

**Cross-cutting problems.** Two `ApplyCondition` implementations differ (CMB-009): the live `ConditionManager` path never escalates Fatigued to Exhausted. Spell effects and the conditions they impose tick on the same timing but are separate, unlinked instances (CMB-034). The generic "save each turn to end it" mechanism has no producers (CMB-055). Any HP-state change removes Unconscious, Disabled, Staggered, Dying and Stable whatever their source. Poison secondary saves run in real seconds (CMB-007). Any condition that sets both `PreventsStandardActions` and `PreventsFullRoundActions` makes the creature, PC or NPC, lose its whole turn in `GameManager.ShouldSkipTurnDueToHPState` before any action or AI runs (CMB-075).

PCs and NPCs alike (delegated RAW reading 2026-10-28, batch 12 step 3) | `CharacterController.MarkStrenuousAction`, `ResolvePendingStrenuousDamage`; `ActionEconomy.NoFurtherActions`; `_Core/GameManager.cs` (`NextInitiativeTurn`), `_Core/GameManager.CombatEnd.cs` (`EvaluateCombatEnd`); `Services/MovementService.cs` (`CanTake5FootStep`); `Spell/Casting/Core/CastActionRules.cs` | PHB p.145: a disabled character that performs a standard action (or another strenuous action) takes 1 damage after completing the act and, unless the activity raised its hit points, is then at -1 and dying; PHB p.93 (Diehard) names casting a quickened spell as such an action. The book does not say when within the turn the act counts as complete. Dealing the damage at the act itself would land it before an act that provokes an attack of opportunity resolves (the AoO comes first), so the game deals it once nothing of the act is left: at the turn end, or when the act decides the fight. A creature that is dying after the act can take no actions, so the window in between allows it nothing more |

**Rules the code applies that were once labelled house rules (checked 2026-10-09):**

- **The rake** (MM p.314; CMB-144; RAW by reading, 2026-10-14, formerly the owner interpretation of 2026-10-11): the rake's claws are extra attacks (MM p.314 "extra", "additional"), and more than one attack needs a full attack (PHB p.143), so the rake needs a full attack (or a pounce, MM p.313) and makes the turn a full attack; it comes once a turn, takes no step of the BAB ladder (a creature that rakes also makes its grapple actions, a lion the rake and its one grapple action, before or after the rake), only on a turn begun grappling (a pounce excepted) and without the -4 for natural attacks in a grapple (`CharacterController.CanRakeInGrapple`, `ResolveGrappleRake`, `PerformRakeAttacks`).
- **Bonus stacking** follows PHB p.171-172 and the glossary (p.305-313; DMG p.21) for every source through `BonusStacking` and `CharacterStats.Bonuses`: luck bonuses no longer stack (the old luck house rule is gone), and the same holds for morale, enhancement and every other named type; dodge and racial bonuses stack (PHB p.171; racial: owner interpretation 2026-10-11, CHR-019); circumstance and untyped bonuses stack unless they come from the same source; the same spell never stacks with itself; penalties of one type give only the worst (PHB p.171), untyped penalties from different sources add up. The monk's fast movement is an enhancement bonus to speed (PHB p.41), so it does not stack with Haste or Longstrider; Prayer gives allies +1 luck and foes -1 on both cast paths (SPL-042); the Rage spell's +2 Strength and Constitution end with it and a second Rage adds nothing; Bane's save penalty applies only against fear (PHB p.203). Known exceptions, each a filed bug: CHR-010 (Death Knell, Divine Power, hand-summed dodge AC and item skill bonuses outside the shared code); SPL-133 (Magic Fang reaches every attack and stacks with a weapon's own enhancement and masterwork bonuses, which are summed outside the shared code); ITM-073 (the Shield spell's shield bonus is added to a held shield's); SPL-134 (a dominated copy of the same spell is dropped instead of kept, so dispelling the better copy leaves nothing).
- **The attack sequence** (`Combat/Core/AttackPool.cs`, `CharacterController.ProgressiveAttackPool`) is PHB p.143 "Deciding between an Attack or a Full Attack": the first attack (or a trip, disarm, sunder or grapple replacing it) spends the standard action, and the creature may then take its move action instead of the remaining attacks (after a 5-foot step that move action cannot move it any distance, PHB p.143-144); a second attack turns the turn into a full attack (a full-round action) and spends the move action. Refused after a move action, while Slowed or with a single action. PC and NPC trip, disarm, sunder and grapple use it (one step at that step's bonus through `CharacterController.TryCommitManeuverSubstituteStep`; `ManeuverActionCost`), and the NPC melee attack runs step by step through it, so an NPC can replace any step with a maneuver and continue with the rest (a natural-weapon disarmer is armed, MM p.312: no -4, and the weapon drops in the defender's square, PHB p.155; sunder needs a manufactured weapon); the AI re-evaluates each weapon step, limited by a per-turn AI stopgap (no maneuver after one succeeded, no retry of a failed type against the same target; owner decision 2026-10-07, AI-060; an AI choice, not a rule), and makes at most one substitute per natural sequence (CMB-102, AI-035; charge and AoO substitution remain). Bull rush is a standard action or the end of a charge for PCs and NPCs alike and never uses an attack step (PHB p.141 Table 8-2, p.154; CMB-102).

Labelled simplifications, not owner rulings (treat as backlog): the swift action is "simplified for now" (`_Core/GameManager.cs` header; CMB-029); Chaos Hammer's slow applies Staggered (`Spell/Resolution/GameManager_Spells_Shared.cs`, `AlignmentBurstSpecFor`; SPL-052); about 11 spell database entries are marked simplified (SPL-048, SPL-052); spreads and emanations are placed as bursts (from a grid intersection since batch 11 step 24; a spread does not turn corners, SPL-192) and a shapeable effect as one fixed block (Wall of Thorns' one 10-ft. cube, Persistent Image's 20-ft. cube; batch 11 step 23, SPL-188, SPL-189); Dispel Magic's area version targets every character in range (`Services/DispelMagicService.cs`); the HP-per-level mode (Roll, Average, Maximum) is a setting with no UI (CORE-007).

### 4.3 Deviations found for this doc

Each was confirmed by reading the code at this commit; none was reproduced in Play mode. Each entry is headed by its issue ID.

- **CMB-065.** Adjacent melee attacks against a paralyzed target are automatic critical hits (`autoCritOnParalyzed` in `PerformSingleAttackWithCrit`). In 3.5 only a coup de grace auto-crits.
- **CMB-066.** Fatigued and Exhausted are penalized twice: `StrengthConditionPenalty`/`DexterityConditionPenalty` lower the scores (-2 or -6), and the `ConditionRules` rows add -1 or -3 to attack, AC, skills and ability checks.
- **CMB-067.** Blinded is penalized twice: the blinded attacker gets the rules-row -2 attack on top of its 50% miss chance, and attackers get +2 against a blinded target (`blindedTargetAttackBonus` in `PerformSingleAttackWithCrit`) on top of its -2 AC. The row also sets `PreventsThreatening` and `PreventsAoO` (read by `ThreatSystem`), but a blinded creature still threatens in 3.5; its attacks just have the 50% miss chance.
- **CMB-068.** An invisible defender gets +2 AC against attackers that cannot see it (`CharacterController.GetInvisibilityArmorClassBonusAgainst`); the Invisible rules row also has +2 AC. The book gives the invisible creature +2 attack and its attackers a 50% miss chance, not an AC bonus.
- **CMB-069.** Sickened has no -2 on weapon damage, and Sickened, Shaken and Panicked lack the -2 ability-check penalty (their `ConditionRules` rows have no `AbilityCheckModifier`; `IsSickened` has no callers and nothing reads Sickened in damage code).
- **CMB-070.** Checked (-2 attack), Confused (-2 attack, -2 skills), Dazzled (-1 on all skills, not only Search and Spot), Deafened (-2 all skills) and Grappled (-2 all skills) carry modifiers that the DMG ch.8 text and the PHB ch.8 grapple rules do not give.
- **CHR-056.** `AlignmentHelper.IsAlignmentValidForClass` does not restrict Bards to nonlawful alignments.
- **CMB-071.** No massive-damage rule (50+ damage from one hit forces a DC 15 Fortitude save, PHB ch.8); no code found.
- **CHR-084.** A feat stays usable when its prerequisite is lost (PHB p.87), except a feat that rests on a ranger combat style feat (`RangerRules.LosesFeatToMissingStylePrerequisite`, CHR-020): ability damage or drain below a feat's minimum, or a lost feat prerequisite of another kind, is not checked at use time.
- **SPL-090.** Since batch 11 step 20 (2026-10-16) Evasion and Improved Evasion apply through `SpellSaveResolver.ApplyEvasion`'s rule in every area spell handler with Reflex-half damage and in `SpellCaster.Cast` (the generic area loop and single-target casts). The Javelin of Lightning's bolt still ignores it (SPL-090), breath weapons roll their own save (AI-040), and there is no check for medium or heavy armor or for helplessness (CHR-081; PHB ch.3, Rogue and Monk).
