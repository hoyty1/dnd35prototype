# Rules coverage: PHB, DMG and MM

This is the map of how close the game is to the D&D 3.5e core rules (Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual). Use it to plan work toward the project goal: battles that play by the core rules, encounters generated by the DMG random-encounter rules, and between-battle party management by PHB/DMG rules, with much deeper enemy AI. It is a summary. The detail lives in the systems docs ([AI](AI.md), [ENCOUNTERS](ENCOUNTERS.md), [PARTY_MANAGEMENT](PARTY_MANAGEMENT.md), [SPELLS_AND_METAMAGIC](SPELLS_AND_METAMAGIC.md), [CREATURES](CREATURES.md), [MAGIC_ITEMS](MAGIC_ITEMS.md)), the [architecture parts](../ARCHITECTURE.md#parts) and the [issue files](../issues/). Known defects are cited by ID (an ID such as `CMB-004` lives in `docs/issues/CMB.md`).

Everything here was established by reading code. Nothing was verified in Play mode. Rules are cited by book and chapter from memory of the 3.5 books; check the book before encoding a number.

## 1. How to read this

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Complete** | The rule is implemented, reachable in normal play, and matches the book apart from cosmetic details. |
| **Mostly** | The core of the rule works in play. The gaps are edge cases or rarely reached. |
| **Partial** | A meaningful part works, but significant parts are missing, wrong or reachable only in some paths. |
| **Minimal** | Data, helpers or scaffolding exist, but little or nothing affects play. |
| **Missing** | Nothing in code. |

**deviates** (column "Dev.") marks an area where implemented behaviour gives different results from the book in normal play. A deviation is a bug until it is listed as a house rule in [section 4](#4-house-rules-and-deviations).

Deviations found or re-checked while writing this doc are filed like any other defect and cited by ID; [section 4.3](#43-deviations-found-for-this-doc) gives the code-level detail for each.

Paths are relative to `Assets/Scripts/`.

**Keeping this doc current.** When a fix closes an issue cited here, update the row's status and IDs in the same commit, as the issue rules in [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) require for every doc that cites an ID. When the owner confirms a house rule, move it from [4.2](#42-unconfirmed-deviations) to [4.1](#41-deliberate-house-rules) with the date. When a new rule area is implemented, add or upgrade its row here and give the detail in the matching systems doc, not here.

## 3. Conditions (DMG ch.8)

**Model.** Conditions are `StatusEffect` instances in `CharacterStats.ActiveConditions`, typed by `CombatConditionType` (`Combat/Conditions/CombatConditionType.cs`). Their rules are `ConditionDefinition` rows in `ConditionRules` (`Combat/StatusEffects/StatusEffect.cs`): 49 definitions (including `None`) plus the aliases KnockedDown to Prone and Grappling to Grappled. Each row carries attack, AC, save, skill, ability-check and initiative modifiers, a movement multiplier, `Prevents*` flags, `DeniesDexToAc`, `GrantsCombatAdvantage` and `CoupDeGraceVulnerable`. `CharacterStats` sums them on read (`ConditionAttackPenalty`, `ConditionACPenalty`, the save sums, `ConditionMovementMultiplier`, smallest multiplier wins). Some numbers live outside the table: the +4 melee bonus against helpless targets, prone AC (`CombatCalculationService.ProneACModifier`), pinned AC, the blinded 50% miss chance, and fatigue STR/DEX penalties (`CharacterStats.StrengthConditionPenalty`, `DexterityConditionPenalty`). The apply chain and the poison and disease mapping are in [combat: conditions](../architecture/combat-and-grid.md#conditions-and-status-effects).

**Cross-cutting problems.** All conditions tick once per round at the round boundary (GameManager.OnNewRound), not relative to each creature's turn, so 1-round conditions can cost nothing (CMB-006). Two `ApplyCondition` implementations differ (CMB-009): the live `ConditionManager` path never escalates Fatigued to Exhausted. Spell effects and the conditions they impose have separate durations (CMB-034). The generic "save each turn to end it" mechanism has no producers (CMB-055). Any HP-state change removes Unconscious, Disabled, Staggered, Dying and Stable whatever their source. Poison secondary saves run in real seconds (CMB-007). Any condition that sets both `PreventsStandardActions` and `PreventsFullRoundActions` makes the creature, PC or NPC, lose its whole turn in `GameManager.ShouldSkipTurnDueToHPState` before any action or AI runs (CMB-075).

### 4.3 Deviations found for this doc

Each was confirmed by reading the code at this commit; none was reproduced in Play mode. Each entry is headed by its issue ID.

- **CMB-064.** Normal critical hits ignore crit immunity. `CharacterController.PerformSingleAttackWithCrit` confirms a threat with `Stats.RollCritConfirmation` and never calls `IsImmuneToCriticalHits` (undead, constructs, oozes, `Immunities.immuneToCriticalHits`); only the paralyzed auto-crit path and coup de grace check it. Also noted in [MAGIC_ITEMS 3](MAGIC_ITEMS.md#3-weapon-armor-and-shield-special-abilities).
- **CMB-065.** Adjacent melee attacks against a paralyzed target are automatic critical hits (`autoCritOnParalyzed` in `PerformSingleAttackWithCrit`). In 3.5 only a coup de grace auto-crits.
- **CMB-066.** Fatigued and Exhausted are penalized twice: `StrengthConditionPenalty`/`DexterityConditionPenalty` lower the scores (-2 or -6), and the `ConditionRules` rows add -1 or -3 to attack, AC, skills and ability checks.
- **CMB-067.** Blinded is penalized twice: the blinded attacker gets the rules-row -2 attack on top of its 50% miss chance, and attackers get +2 against a blinded target (`blindedTargetAttackBonus` in `PerformSingleAttackWithCrit`) on top of its -2 AC. The row also sets `PreventsThreatening` and `PreventsAoO` (read by `ThreatSystem`), but a blinded creature still threatens in 3.5; its attacks just have the 50% miss chance.
- **CMB-068.** An invisible defender gets +2 AC against attackers that cannot see it (`CharacterController.GetInvisibilityArmorClassBonusAgainst`); the Invisible rules row also has +2 AC. The book gives the invisible creature +2 attack and its attackers a 50% miss chance, not an AC bonus.
- **CMB-069.** Sickened has no -2 on weapon damage, and Sickened, Shaken and Panicked lack the -2 ability-check penalty (their `ConditionRules` rows have no `AbilityCheckModifier`; `IsSickened` has no callers and nothing reads Sickened in damage code).
- **CMB-070.** Checked (-2 attack), Confused (-2 attack, -2 skills), Dazzled (-1 on all skills, not only Search and Spot), Deafened (-2 all skills) and Grappled (-2 all skills) carry modifiers that the DMG ch.8 text and the PHB ch.8 grapple rules do not give.
- **CHR-056.** `AlignmentHelper.IsAlignmentValidForClass` does not restrict Bards to nonlawful alignments.
- **CMB-071.** No massive-damage rule (50+ damage from one hit forces a DC 15 Fortitude save, PHB ch.8); no code found.
- **CMB-072.** The disabled strenuous-action rule (PHB ch.8: a standard action costs 1 hp) is implemented only in `CharacterController.CommitStandardAction`, and only at exactly 0 HP (it sets `CurrentHP = -1`). Standard actions spent through `Actions.UseStandardAction` directly (`CombatFlowService`, `CharmedBehaviorController`, two `GameManager` sites) skip it, and a Diehard character disabled at negative HP never loses the hit point.
- **SPL-090.** Evasion is applied only where a resolver calls `SpellSaveResolver.ApplyEvasion`: Cone of Cold and Chain Lightning (`Spell/Resolution/GameManager_Spells_Phase1.cs`) and Sunburst (`GameManager_Spells_Phase2.cs`). `TryResolveScaledAoEDamageSpell` (Fireball, Lightning Bolt), the generic area loop, `SpellCaster.Cast` and breath weapons (AI-040) ignore it, and there is no check for medium or heavy armor or for helplessness (PHB ch.3, Rogue and Monk). [characters: classes](../architecture/characters-and-creatures.md#classes) still lists Evasion as working.

## 5. Highest-leverage fidelity gaps

Ordered by how often each gap affects play: every attack first, then every turn, every fight, every encounter, and then the between-battle loop. Each item names where to start.

Items 1-6 are also the prerequisites for deeper enemy AI: an AI that scores options needs attack, damage, condition and spell outcomes that follow the same rules for both sides, and creatures whose abilities actually execute. [AI 13](AI.md#13-toward-deeper-ai-analysis-for-the-owner-to-decide) gives the options for the decision layer itself.

1. **Weapon attack and damage math (every attack).** Crit damage and crit immunity, morale damage, typed energy riders. The attack-roll modifier is now built once (`CharacterController.BuildAttackBonus`, used by `Attack`, `FullAttack`, `DualWieldAttack` and `FlurryOfBlows`; CMB-043 and CMB-002 fixed, not verified in Play mode); rake and grapple weapon attacks still need moving onto it. Start: CMB-004, CMB-003, CMB-011, CMB-064, CMB-065, CMB-087; [combat: attack resolution](../architecture/combat-and-grid.md#attack-resolution).
2. **Rules parity on NPC paths (every enemy turn).** The turn-skip gate (movement, maneuver and casting AoOs, casting Concentration and standing up are shared with PCs). Smarter AI built on asymmetric rules optimizes against rules the player does not share. Start: [AI 13.2](AI.md#132-prerequisites-fix-before-tuning-behaviour), CMB-074, CMB-075.
3. **Turn-relative durations (every condition and buff).** Expire conditions and spell effects relative to the creature's or caster's turn; normalize the 54 legacy durations; fix the never-expiring flags. Start: CMB-006, SPL-032, SPL-002, SPL-003, CMB-009.
4. **Character math foundations (every character, every fight).** CON HP, BAB and hit dice from `ICharacterClass`, unapplied save bonuses, racial traits, typed bonus stacking. Start: CHR-001, CHR-002, CHR-018, CHR-019, CHR-010, SPL-025, SPL-026.
5. **Spawned-creature correctness (every encounter).** Alignment, class BAB and saves, single template application, weapons in MainHand/Ranged slots, Undead BAB, NPC-class proficiencies. Start: [CREATURES 8](CREATURES.md#8-gaps-and-backlog-toward-mmdmg-fidelity) item 1; CRE-002, CRE-004, CRE-001, ITM-004, CRE-009.
6. **One rules-correct spell resolution path (every spell).** Single DC function, caster level by class, damage through `ApplyIncomingDamage`, dice scaling, reachable spell branches, area spells with saves and SR. Start: SPL-054, SPL-001, SPL-017, SPL-004, SPL-005, SPL-037, SPL-027; [SPELLS 7](SPELLS_AND_METAMAGIC.md#7-gaps-and-backlog-toward-phb-fidelity).
7. **Surprise, flat-footed and encounter start (every fight).** Encounter distance, Spot/Listen awareness, a surprise round and flat-footed until first action; this also makes Uncanny Dodge and sneak attack work as written. Start: CMB-028, [ENCOUNTERS 9](ENCOUNTERS.md#9-gaps-and-backlog-toward-dmg-fidelity) item 10.
8. **Spell progression (every caster from level 4).** Wizard, Druid and Cleric spells above 2nd, Ranger and Paladin casting, Adept casting, per-class spell levels. Start: SPL-041, SPL-039, SPL-008, CHR-024.
9. **Enemy capability and decision layer (every enemy turn).** NPC area spells and metamagic, casters on melee routines, Brute and Caster profiles, ranged behaviour, breath recharge, spell-like abilities, then a unified candidate-action scorer. Start: [AI 13](AI.md#13-toward-deeper-ai-analysis-for-the-owner-to-decide), [AI 9](AI.md#9-action-coverage-matrix); AI-001, AI-002, AI-003, AI-004, CRE-015, CRE-017.
10. **DMG encounter generation (every encounter).** Spawn any group size on the grid, make every table row resolve, one EL module with the DMG doubling rule. Start: ENC-001, ENC-002, ENC-003, ENC-013; [ENCOUNTERS 9](ENCOUNTERS.md#9-gaps-and-backlog-toward-dmg-fidelity).
11. **Conditions data cleanup (most fights).** Remove the double-counted modifiers, fix the non-RAW rows, apply Flat-Footed, add Blown Away and Incorporeal or delete them, let PCs and NPCs act under Nauseated, Panicked, Turned and Pinned (the shared turn-skip gate). Start: [section 3](#3-conditions-dmg-ch8); CMB-066, CMB-067, CMB-068, CMB-069, CMB-070, CMB-072, SPL-090, CMB-054, CMB-075.
12. **Class features (every fight for the affected classes).** Paladin smite and lay on hands (Smite Evil also needs alignment on spawned NPCs, CRE-002, and per-day uses that the post-combat full rest does not reset, CORE-015), Ranger favored enemy and combat style, Druid wild shape and companion, Bard songs, Monk scaling, Turn Undead by class level with rebuke, inert feats. Start: CHR-020, CRE-002, CORE-015, CHR-026, CHR-003, CMB-016, CMB-026, CHR-021, CMB-022, CMB-023.
13. **Between-battle loop (every encounter boundary).** A campaign state with a clock, rest choices with PHB natural healing, real death and defeat, daily recovery, XP by character level. Start: [PARTY_MANAGEMENT 4](PARTY_MANAGEMENT.md#4-gaps-and-backlog) items 1-4; CORE-015, CORE-001, CHR-004.
14. **Terrain and visibility (most fights).** Difficult terrain cost, line of sight and line of effect for all attacks and spells, cover, reachable movement highlight, Tiny-creature reach, movement modes. Start: GRID-002, GRID-009, GRID-003, GRID-005, GRID-006.
15. **Magic items in play (most fights after a few levels).** Ring deflection, real treasure items, special abilities, item activation, PHB action costs for item use, and NPC consumables. Start: ITM-001, ITM-016, ITM-023, ITM-019, ITM-005, AI-009; [MAGIC_ITEMS 11](MAGIC_ITEMS.md#11-gaps-and-backlog-toward-dmg-fidelity).
