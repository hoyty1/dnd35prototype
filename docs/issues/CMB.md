# Known issues: Combat (CMB-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CMB-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Poison secondary damage is timed in real seconds.** GameManager.Update runs UpdatePoisonTimers every frame, before the menu early-return, decrementing TimeUntilSecondary by Time.deltaTime (60 s, or 600 s ingested) with no pause. RAW uses game time (1 minute = 10 rounds), so the delay depends on the player's pace and keeps running while menus are open. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:2191-2201, :2232-2268, Assets/Scripts/Effects/Poison.cs:44-55. *Fix:* track the delay in rounds and decrement it in the new-round handler.
- **CMB-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **ApplyCondition has two implementations with different rules.** The normal path via ConditionManager links Helpless to Paralyzed and Unconscious and runs hooks but never escalates Fatigued to Exhausted. CharacterStats.ApplyCondition escalates but links Helpless only for Paralyzed, and its direct callers (Sunburst, rest fatigue and others) skip hooks, source tracking and negative-level slot loss. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Conditions/ConditionManager.cs:46-101, Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:1407-1430. *Fix:* keep one implementation in ConditionManager and route every caller through CharacterController.ApplyCondition.
- **CMB-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Improved Critical applies to every weapon and stacks with Keen.** FeatManager.GetAdjustedCritThreatMin doubles every weapon's threat range on HasFeat("Improved Critical"), ignoring the weapon choice, and its exact match misses NPC feats like "Improved Critical (longsword)". Keen is already in item.CritThreatMin, so the doubling stacks with it (RAW: no stacking). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:326-335, Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:619-629. *Fix:* check the chosen weapon, take the better of the doubled base range and Keen, and normalize "(weapon)" suffixes.
- **CMB-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Weapon energy damage (Flaming, Frost, Shock) is untyped.** The enchantment damage and Burst crit dice are summed into enchantmentBonusDamage and added to the weapon DamagePacket, typed only as physical damage. Energy immunity and resistance never reduce it, while DR, which RAW never applies to energy, does. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:7240-7286, :7184-7212, :7214. *Fix:* apply energy riders as separate typed DamagePackets exempt from DR.
- **CMB-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Recasting Fire Shield stacks retribution effects.** Each cast registers a new FireShieldReactionEffect with MeleeReactionService, Unregister has no callers, and IsActiveOn checks only Stats.FireShieldActive, so after a recast each melee hit triggers retribution once per instance until combat end. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager_Spells_F.cs:185-198, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Reactions/FireShieldReactionEffect.cs:40-47. *Fix:* unregister any existing effect before registering, and call Unregister wherever FireShieldActive is set to false.
- **CMB-013** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The Snatch Arrows counter and several item and mount riders skip mitigation.** The Snatch Arrows counter-throw calls `Stats.TakeDamage`, so it ignores DR and immunity. Spirited Charge bases its extra damage on post-mitigation damage, multiplying DR (latent); it also doubles or triples `result.TotalDamage` after the attack, which already holds a critical's rolls, sneak attack and energy dice, so a critical spirited charge gives x4 weapon damage and multiplies the extra dice, where RAW (PHB p.100 Spirited Charge with p.134 multiplying damage) gives x3 with a x2 weapon and never multiplies extra dice; the fix is to pass the charge multiplier into `WeaponDamageRoll.Roll` (rolls = critical multiplier + charge multiplier - 1) and add the riders once (CMB-004, 2026-10-09). Demon armor claws and the Mace of Smiting extra damage write `CurrentHP -=` (no DR, no temporary hit points, no death handling); mounted-combat damage (unreachable, CMB-031) calls `TakeDamage`. Fire Shield retribution was fixed on 2026-10-09 (SPL-004: a typed spell packet through `GameManager.DealDamage`). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs (Snatch Arrows counter), Assets/Scripts/Combat/Maneuvers/SupportActions.cs (Spirited Charge), Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Armor/DemonArmorBehavior.cs, Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Weapons/MaceOfSmitingBehavior.cs. *Fix:* route each through `ApplyIncomingDamage` (or `GameManager.DealDamage`) with a weapon or natural packet.
- **CMB-015** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Overrun has two paths with different rules.** The opposed check is shared since 2026-10-07: the move-through overrun (`GameManager.ResolveOverrunOpposedCheck` in OverrunSystem.cs) now uses the same PHB p.157 terms as the targeted `CharacterController.ResolveOverrun` (`GetOverrunAttackerCheckModifier`: STR, special size, +4 Improved Overrun, condition modifiers; `GetTripOrOverrunDefenderCheckModifier`: better of STR or DEX, special size, stability, condition modifiers; `DoesAttackerWinOpposedCheck` for ties), and its private size table is gone. The rest still differs by side: the PC path (OverrunSystem move-through) provokes an AoO, does not knock a beaten defender prone, and an AI defender always blocks; the NPC path (`ResolveOverrun` with `defenderBlocks: true`) provokes no AoO, since `ThreatSystem.DoesManeuverProvokeAoO` has no Overrun case, and knocks the defender prone and pushes it 1 square (CMB-116). `ResolveOverrunSpecialAttack` is reachable only from `ShowOverrunResponsePrompt`, which has no callers; CMB-086 also cites it. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Maneuvers/OverrunSystem.cs (move-through loop, `ResolveOverrunOpposedCheck`, `ResolveEnemyOverrunResponse`), Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs (`ResolveOverrun`), Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs (`DoesManeuverProvokeAoO`). *Fix:* one overrun resolution for both sides: AoO for entering the defender's space, prone on success, the attacker continues through, the defender's avoid-or-block choice from the UI or the AI, and the defender's knock-prone reaction on a failed block (CMB-117). Not verified in Play mode.
- **CMB-018** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The PC and NPC charge ends are two executors, and Spirited Charge exists only on the PC side.** `SupportActions.ExecuteCharge` (PC) and `NPCExecuteCharge` (NPC) each resolve the end of a charge (pounce, rake, Improved Grab, the single attack). - **CMB-065** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Adjacent melee attacks auto-crit paralyzed targets.** autoCritOnParalyzed makes every adjacent melee hit on a Paralyzed (non-crit-immune) target an automatic confirmed critical; 3.5 gives only +4 melee against helpless targets and reserves the automatic crit for a coup de grace. - **CMB-066** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Fatigued and Exhausted penalties are counted twice.** StrengthConditionPenalty/DexterityConditionPenalty already lower STR and DEX by 2 (Fatigued) or 6 (Exhausted), and the ConditionRules rows add a further -1/-3 to attack, AC, skills and ability checks. ## Development traps

- **CMB-034** · **Low** — **Conditions and spell effects are separate, unlinked instances.** Conditions go through ConditionService to ConditionManager; spell buffs live in StatusEffectManager with per-spell clean-up in TickCharacterSpellDurations. Since 2026-10-09 (CMB-006) both tick on the same timing, at the initiative count of the creature whose turn it was when they were created (`TurnDurations`), so a spell that imposes a condition and adds an effect on its caster's turn ends both at the same count; but nothing links the two, so removing one (a dispel, Remove Fear, a save that ends the condition) leaves the other. The StatusEffect class is a condition, not a spell effect. *Evidence:* `ConditionService.ApplyCondition`, `StatusEffectManager.AddEffect`, `GameManager.TickCharacterSpellDurations` (GameManager.SpellCasting.cs). *Fix:* when a spell imposes a condition, remove it explicitly on spell expiry and dispel.
- **CMB-035** · **Low** — **CombatResult.TotalDamage can report pre-mitigation damage.** It returns FinalDamageDealt only when that is > 0 or a mitigation flag is set, else Damage + SneakAttackDamage, so a weapon hit fully absorbed by Protection from Energy or zeroed by Fire Shield reports the unmitigated number to concentration checks and logs. With Shield Other it includes the half moved to the protector. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/CombatResult.cs:170-172, Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:4681. *Fix:* return FinalDamageDealt whenever mitigation ran (for example via a MitigationResolved flag).
- **CMB-036** · **Low** — **CombatResult breakdown fields are display-only.** Fields such as CombatResult.WeaponSpecBonus are written after damage is computed and feed only the breakdown text; changing them does not change damage. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/CombatResult.cs:106, :262-270. *Fix:* change the damage computation, not these fields.
- **CMB-037** · **Low** — **Threat code compares teams with == instead of TeamUtility.IsEnemy.** ThreatSystem skips allies with Team == mover.Team, so a CharacterTeam.Neutral creature would threaten and take AoOs against both sides; latent, as nothing assigns Neutral. - **CMB-038** · **Low** — **Some combat state outlives an encounter.** The statics CharacterController._grappleLinksByCharacter and MountSystem._mountedCharacters are not cleared at combat end, and AttacksOfOpportunityUsed resets only at the character's own turn start, so an AoO spent late in one combat stays used until its first turn in the next. - **CMB-147** · **Low** — **Haste's +1 bonuses are stored twice, and `ApplyHasteEffect` alone leaves them out.** PHB p.239: a hasted creature gets +1 on attack rolls, a +1 dodge bonus to AC and +1 on Reflex saves. `CharacterController.ApplyHasteEffect` writes `HasteEffectData.AttackBonus`, `ACBonus` and `ReflexSaveBonus`, which nothing reads; the formulas read `CharacterStats.HasteAttackBonus` (`ConditionAttackPenalty`), `HasteACBonus` and `HasteReflexBonus`, which only the spell handler (`GameManager_Spells_H`, next to its `ApplyHasteEffect` call) and the mithral full plate of speed set.
