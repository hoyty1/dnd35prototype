# Known issues: Combat (CMB-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CMB-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Morale and situational damage bonuses never reach weapon damage.** Inspire Courage, Smite, Magic Fang, Prayer and spell buffs add to Stats.MoraleDamageBonus, but PerformSingleAttackWithCrit never reads it; its only reader, CharacterStats.RollDamage, serves only the Confused "attack self" result. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:612-633, :4170, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Special/TemplateSmiteSystem.cs:195-211. *Fix:* add MoraleDamageBonus in the weapon damage step and replace the temporary +=/-= used by Charge and Smite with per-attack modifiers.
- **CMB-004** · **High** · Player-visible — **Crits multiply only weapon dice; coup de grace multiplies sneak attack.** On a confirmed crit PerformSingleAttackWithCrit multiplies the weapon dice but adds ability, flat, enhancement and feat damage once; RAW multiplies all of them, but not extra dice such as sneak attack or energy bursts. ResolveCoupDeGrace does the opposite and multiplies (base + sneak attack) by CritMultiplier. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:6928-6950, :11427-11448. *Fix:* multiply the static damage bonuses by critMultiplier and exclude sneak attack from the coup de grace multiplier.
- **CMB-006** · **High** · Player-visible — **Conditions tick at the round boundary, so 1-round conditions can cost nothing.** Every condition ticks at the round boundary (GameManager.OnNewRound -> ConditionService.OnRoundEnd), not relative to any creature's turn, so a 1-round Stunned on a creature that already acted expires before its next turn (RAW: it loses that action); only ChargePenalty is turn-relative.
