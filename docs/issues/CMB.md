# Known issues: Combat (CMB-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CMB-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **Flanking is counted twice.** ConditionService.RefreshFlankedConditions gives any flanked character the Flanked condition (-2 AC and touch AC) against every attacker, ranged and non-flanking included, while the flanking attackers also get CombatUtils.FlankingAttackBonus (+2), an effective +4. RAW gives only the flanking attackers +2 to hit. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/ConditionService.cs:295-338, Assets/Scripts/Combat/StatusEffects/StatusEffect.cs:761-771. *Fix:* set Flanked's ArmorClassModifier to 0 and keep the condition as a display tag.
- **CMB-002** · **High** · Player-visible — **FullAttack uses STR for ranged attacks and drops morale bonuses.** FullAttack's iterative loop (CharacterStats.GetIterativeAttackBonuses: BAB + STR + size) swaps in DEX only for melee Weapon Finesse, so ranged full attacks add STR. Neither FullAttack branch adds Stats.MoraleAttackBonus, losing Bless, the Pounce charge +2 and other morale bonuses. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:5084, :5292-5305. *Fix:* fix the root cause (CMB-043), or add (baseAbilityMod - Stats.STRMod) and Stats.MoraleAttackBonus to both FullAttack sums.
- **CMB-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Morale and situational damage bonuses never reach weapon damage.** Inspire Courage, Smite, Magic Fang, Prayer and spell buffs add to Stats.MoraleDamageBonus, but PerformSingleAttackWithCrit never reads it; its only reader, CharacterStats.RollDamage, serves only the Confused "attack self" result. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:612-633, :4170, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Special/TemplateSmiteSystem.cs:195-211. *Fix:* add MoraleDamageBonus in the weapon damage step and replace the temporary +=/-= used by Charge and Smite with per-attack modifiers.
- **CMB-004** · **High** · Player-visible — **Crits multiply only weapon dice; coup de grace multiplies sneak attack.** On a confirmed crit PerformSingleAttackWithCrit multiplies the weapon dice but adds ability, flat, enhancement and feat damage once; RAW multiplies all of them, but not extra dice such as sneak attack or energy bursts. ResolveCoupDeGrace does the opposite and multiplies (base + sneak attack) by CritMultiplier. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:6928-6950, :11427-11448. *Fix:* multiply the static damage bonuses by critMultiplier and exclude sneak attack from the coup de grace multiplier.
- **CMB-005** · **High** · Player-visible — **Movement AoOs resolve after the mover has left the square.** The PC move coroutine moves the character into path[i] before running the AoOs tagged with that PathIndex; ThreatSystem.ExecuteAoO spends the AoO, then Attack() returns an unrolled miss if the mover is now out of reach. Stepping away from an adjacent reach-1 enemy thus wastes its AoO (charges too; Withdraw resolves AoOs first). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.CombatActions.cs:795-828, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs:509, :545. *Fix:* resolve each AoO before the step out of the threatened square, or skip the range check for AoOs.
- **CMB-006** · **High** · Player-visible — **Conditions tick at round end, so 1-round conditions can cost nothing.** GameManager.OnNewRound ticks every condition via ConditionService.OnRoundEnd, so a 1-round Stunned on a creature that already acted expires before its next turn (RAW: it loses that action); only ChargePenalty is turn-relative. ConditionService.OnTurnStart also runs twice per turn, harmless until a turn-start save is added. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:3804-3818, Assets/Scripts/Services/ConditionService.cs:197-207. *Fix:* expire short conditions relative to the applier's or target's turn, and keep one OnTurnStart call path.
- **CMB-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Poison secondary damage is timed in real seconds.** GameManager.Update runs UpdatePoisonTimers every frame, before the menu early-return, decrementing TimeUntilSecondary by Time.deltaTime (60 s, or 600 s ingested) with no pause. RAW uses game time (1 minute = 10 rounds), so the delay depends on the player's pace and keeps running while menus are open. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:2189-2199, :2232-2268, Assets/Scripts/Effects/Poison.cs:44-55. *Fix:* track the delay in rounds and decrement it in the new-round handler.
- **CMB-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Standalone off-hand attacks get full STR and main-hand crit stats.** Attack(..., isOffHandAttack: true), used by CombatFlowService.ExecuteOffHandAttack, passes isOffHand: false to PerformSingleAttackWithCrit and takes crit stats from Stats, which reflect the main-hand weapon. So any attackWeaponOverride, including off-hand AoOs, gets full STR and the main weapon's crit; DualWieldAttack is correct. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:4741-4750, :4904, :4931. *Fix:* pass isOffHandAttack through and take crit stats from the override weapon.
- **CMB-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **ApplyCondition has two implementations with different rules.** The normal path via ConditionManager links Helpless to Paralyzed and Unconscious and runs hooks but never escalates Fatigued to Exhausted. CharacterStats.ApplyCondition escalates but links Helpless only for Paralyzed, and its direct callers (Sunburst, rest fatigue and others) skip hooks, source tracking and negative-level slot loss. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Conditions/ConditionManager.cs:46-101, Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:1407-1430. *Fix:* keep one implementation in ConditionManager and route every caller through CharacterController.ApplyCondition.
- **CMB-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Improved Critical applies to every weapon and stacks with Keen.** FeatManager.GetAdjustedCritThreatMin doubles every weapon's threat range on HasFeat("Improved Critical"), ignoring the weapon choice, and its exact match misses NPC feats like "Improved Critical (longsword)". Keen is already in item.CritThreatMin, so the doubling stacks with it (RAW: no stacking). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:326-335, Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:619-629. *Fix:* check the chosen weapon, take the better of the doubled base range and Keen, and normalize "(weapon)" suffixes.
- **CMB-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Weapon energy damage (Flaming, Frost, Shock) is untyped.** The enchantment damage and Burst crit dice are summed into enchantmentBonusDamage and added to the weapon DamagePacket, typed only as physical damage. Energy immunity and resistance never reduce it, while DR, which RAW never applies to energy, does. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:7028-7074, :7184-7212, :7214. *Fix:* apply energy riders as separate typed DamagePackets exempt from DR.
- **CMB-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Recasting Fire Shield stacks retribution effects.** Each cast registers a new FireShieldReactionEffect with MeleeReactionService, Unregister has no callers, and IsActiveOn checks only Stats.FireShieldActive, so after a recast each melee hit triggers retribution once per instance until combat end. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager_Spells_F.cs:182-195, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Reactions/FireShieldReactionEffect.cs:40-47. *Fix:* unregister any existing effect before registering, and call Unregister wherever FireShieldActive is set to false.
- **CMB-013** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Fire Shield retribution and the Snatch Arrows counter skip mitigation.** Both call Stats.TakeDamage instead of ApplyIncomingDamage, so fire-immune or fire-resistant attackers take full Fire Shield damage and the counter-throw ignores DR and immunity. Spirited Charge bases its extra damage on post-mitigation damage, multiplying DR (latent); other direct TakeDamage callers are unaudited. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Reactions/FireShieldReactionEffect.cs:66-76, Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:6849-6860. *Fix:* route both through ApplyIncomingDamage (Fire Shield as typed fire or cold, Source = Spell).
- **CMB-014** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Maneuvers deviate from RAW in seven ways.** Trip has no touch attack, adds BAB both sides, uses the wrong size modifier and gives the defender its own Improved Trip +4 (Disarm too); bull rush and targeted overrun use the wrong defender ability; grapple touch ignores natural 20/1 and most touch AC; ties are inconsistent. PC trip, disarm and bull rush never provoke; Improved Grab creatures cannot grapple normally. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:10528-10535, Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.CombatActions.cs:1723. *Fix:* one opposed-maneuver helper; make those three provoke without the Improved feat.
- **CMB-015** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Overrun has two resolvers with different formulas.** Move-through uses GameManager.ResolveOverrunOpposedCheck (defender max(STR, DEX), no Improved Overrun +4 or condition penalties, ties to defender, private size table); a targeted overrun uses CharacterController.ResolveOverrunAttempt (defender STR, +4, penalties, ties to attacker). The result depends on how the overrun started. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Maneuvers/OverrunSystem.cs:408-436, Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:11274-11307. *Fix:* have move-through call ResolveOverrunAttempt with a max(STR, DEX) defender, and delete the private size table.
- **CMB-016** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Turn Undead uses total character level and the undead's Level.** The turning level is Stats.Level (paladin: Level - 3), so a Cleric 1 / Fighter 9 turns as a 10th-level cleric. Undead HD come from Stats.Level, and templated undead such as ZombieTemplate set Level = 0, so they count as 1 HD. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Special/TurnUndeadSystem.cs:418-433, :491, Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/ZombieTemplate.cs:62-63. *Fix:* use GetClassLevel("Cleric") + max(0, GetClassLevel("Paladin") - 3) and TeamUtility.GetHitDice for undead HD.
- **CMB-017** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Cleave fires only on the player's single attack, against adjacent foes.** CombatFlowService.PerformSingleAttack is the only Cleave/Great Cleave site, so full attacks, AoOs, charges, Template Smite and all NPC attacks never cleave. The follow-up target comes from FindAdjacentEnemy (SquareGridUtils.IsAdjacent), ignoring reach weapons and multi-square creatures. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/CombatFlowService.cs:347-370, :887-945. *Fix:* move Cleave into a shared post-kill hook with a per-round flag and test targets with IsTargetInCurrentWeaponRange.
- **CMB-018** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Player charges never get flanking.** The player charge calls charger.Attack(target, false, 0, ...), so it never gets the flanking bonus or flank-based sneak attack; NPC charges compute isFlankingCharge. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Maneuvers/SupportActions.cs:1665 (player), :1982 (NPC). *Fix:* compute flanking for the player charge as the NPC charge does.
- **CMB-019** · **Med** · Player-visible — **NPC attacks skip ammunition and thrown-weapon handling.** Only CombatFlowService checks and consumes ammunition (CheckAmmoAvailable, ConsumeAmmoForAttack) and drops thrown weapons (ResolveThrownWeaponAfterAttack). GameManager.NPCTurns calls npc.Attack/npc.FullAttack directly and does neither; natural attacks are unaffected. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/CombatFlowService.cs:565, :621-625, Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:1126, :1227-1233. *Fix:* move ammunition, thrown-weapon and Cleave handling into a post-attack hook shared by both pipelines.
- **CMB-020** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Fortification drops specific-item damage bonuses.** When Fortification negates a crit, PerformSingleAttackWithCrit re-rolls normal weapon damage and overwrites rawWeaponDamage, losing the SpecificItemBehavior.OnDamageRoll delta (for example Sylvan Scimitar +1d6). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:7118-7137, :7145-7182. *Fix:* keep the specific-item delta when recomputing damage.
- **CMB-021** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Template Smite does not trigger melee reactions.** TemplateSmiteSystem.ExecuteTemplateSmiteAttack never calls MeleeReactionService.TriggerReactions, so Fire Shield and similar reactions do not fire on smites. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Special/TemplateSmiteSystem.cs:164-246. *Fix:* call TriggerReactions after the smite attack, as the other melee paths do.

## Unreachable or stubbed features

- **CMB-022** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Whirlwind Attack, Manyshot and Stunning Fist cannot be used.** PerformWhirlwindAttack and PerformManyshotAttack have no callers, ManyshotActive/StunningFistActive are set true only in tests (so Stunning Fist's on-hit code never runs), and SceneBootstrap never creates their CombatUI toggles. The feats can still be taken (an archery Ranger gets Manyshot at 6) and do nothing. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/CombatFlowService.cs:1303, :1392, Assets/Scripts/UI/Combat/CombatUI.cs:824-850. *Fix:* create and wire the toggles in SceneBootstrap.CreateFeatControls, route Manyshot and add a Whirlwind action, or hide the feats.
- **CMB-023** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Spring Attack and Shot on the Run do nothing.** ThreatSystem.ExecuteAoO suppresses AoOs on Stats.IsUsingSpringAttackMovement/SpringAttackTarget, which are only ever reset, and FeatManager.CanUseSpringAttack/CanUseShotOnTheRun have no callers. - **CMB-024** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Engulf leaves a permanent Helpless condition.** AIService.TryExecuteEngulf applies Helpless with duration -1 and has no damage (TODO), engulfer tracking or escape, so the target stays Helpless even after the engulfer dies. - **CMB-025** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The incorporeal miss chance ignores ghost touch and force.** CharacterController.GetMissChance gives incorporeal creatures a flat 50% miss chance against every attack (TODO for ghost touch and force); HasGhostTouchWeapon exists and is used for Blink but not here. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:8747-8752, :1671, :1684. *Fix:* skip it when HasGhostTouchWeapon is true or a force effect applies.
- **CMB-026** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Turn Undead has no turn resistance, rebuke/command or line of effect.** No turn resistance stat exists (only descriptive NPC text), evil clerics turn like good ones, and GetTurnableUndeadInRange picks enemy undead within 12 squares with no line-of-effect check. - **CMB-027** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Tumble is not implemented.** No movement or AoO code reads Tumble, so tumbling past threats and its other uses are missing; it appears only in skill lists, default and test ranks, feat text and the armor check penalty list. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Skills/ClassSkillDefinitions.cs:46, Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:5224, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs (no reference). *Fix:* add a Tumble check (DC 15, or 25 through occupied squares) in the movement AoO path before ExecuteAoO.
- **CMB-028** · **Med** · Player-visible — **No surprise round and no flat-footed state at combat start.** CombatConditionType.FlatFooted is checked (CharacterConditions, AttackCalculator, feint, Combat Reflexes, SwordOfSubtletyBehavior) but never applied, and no surprise-round code exists, so creatures are never flat-footed before their first action. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/StatusEffects/StatusEffect.cs:393-400, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/AttackCalculator.cs:269. *Fix:* apply FlatFooted to every combatant at initiative and clear it on each one's first turn, respecting Uncanny Dodge.

## Development traps

- **CMB-034** · **Low** — **Conditions and spell effects have separate, independent durations.** Conditions go through ConditionService to ConditionManager and tick in ConditionManager.TickConditions; spell buffs (StatusEffectManager) tick separately with per-spell clean-up in TickCharacterSpellDurations, so a spell applying both can desync, for example when only one is dispelled. The StatusEffect class is a condition, not a spell effect. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/ConditionService.cs:86-104, Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:7978-8040. *Fix:* when a spell imposes a condition, remove it explicitly on spell expiry and dispel.
- **CMB-035** · **Low** — **CombatResult.TotalDamage can report pre-mitigation damage.** It returns FinalDamageDealt only when that is > 0 or a mitigation flag is set, else Damage + SneakAttackDamage, so a weapon hit fully absorbed by Protection from Energy or zeroed by Fire Shield reports the unmitigated number to concentration checks and logs. With Shield Other it includes the half moved to the protector. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/CombatResult.cs:170-172, Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:4681. *Fix:* return FinalDamageDealt whenever mitigation ran (for example via a MitigationResolved flag).
- **CMB-036** · **Low** — **CombatResult breakdown fields are display-only.** Fields such as CombatResult.WeaponSpecBonus are written after damage is computed and feed only the breakdown text; changing them does not change damage. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/CombatResult.cs:106, :262-270. *Fix:* change the damage computation, not these fields.
- **CMB-037** · **Low** — **Threat and Cleave code compare teams with == instead of TeamUtility.IsEnemy.** ThreatSystem and CombatFlowService.FindAdjacentEnemy skip allies with Team == mover.Team, so a CharacterTeam.Neutral creature would threaten and take AoOs against both sides; latent, as nothing assigns Neutral. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs:167, :200, :389, Assets/Scripts/Services/CombatFlowService.cs:347-370. *Fix:* use TeamUtility.IsEnemy before introducing a Neutral team.
- **CMB-038** · **Low** — **Some combat state outlives an encounter.** The statics CharacterController._grappleLinksByCharacter and MountSystem._mountedCharacters are not cleared at combat end, and AttacksOfOpportunityUsed resets only at the character's own turn start, so an AoO spent late in one combat stays used until its first turn in the next. Each Play session starts clean (domain reload). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:595, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Mounts/MountSystem.cs:19, Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs:301-305. *Fix:* clear grapple links and mounts at combat end, and reset AttacksOfOpportunityUsed at combat start.

## Performance

- **CMB-039** · **Low** — **Threat queries rebuild sets and log on every call.** ThreatSystem.GetThreatenedSquares rebuilds the full threatened set and writes a Debug.Log per call; GetThreateningEnemies does this for every non-ally per query, and AIService calls it for each 5-foot-step candidate. The cost is log spam and garbage. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs:85-139, :182-210, Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1109, Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:556-562. *Fix:* gate the logs behind a verbose flag and cache threatened sets per turn and position.
- **CMB-040** · **Low** — **ConditionService rebuilds a condition mirror on almost every query.** Every query (HasCondition, GetConditionDuration and others) calls SyncCharacter, which re-maps all the character's StatusEffects into a private dictionary and resolves each source by a linear CharacterName search over GetAllCharacters, which allocates a list per call. The real state is on CharacterController, so the mirror is redundant. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/ConditionService.cs:46-47, :233-264, Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:11411-11421. *Fix:* drop the mirror, use CharacterController's conditions directly, and cache the character list per turn.
