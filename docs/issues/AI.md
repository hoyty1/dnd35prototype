# Known issues: AI (AI-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **AI-002** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Casters on melee routines never cast.** Only the Healer, Dragon and RangedKiter routines call `AIService.TryExecuteSpellcastAction`, and profiled NPCs reach RangedKiter only via that `AIBehavior` or a Ranged `CombatStyle`, which the Spellcaster, Abjurer and Vampire profiles lack. So the vampire never casts its spell list; the lich casts only because `LichAIProfile` is Ranged. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:265-279, 651, 896, 970; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/VampireAIProfile.cs:35. *Fix:* call `TryExecuteSpellcastAction` first in the melee routines for NPCs with castable spells, or treat those profiles as casting-capable.
- **AI-003** · **Med** · Player-visible — **`NPCAIBehavior.Ranged` is never handled.** Without a profile `AIService.ExecuteNPCTurn` treats it as AggressiveMelee, and with one only RangedKiter or a Ranged `CombatStyle` reaches the kiter routine, so all current users (3 NPC definitions and the Skeleton Archer) close to melee instead of shooting. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:267-297; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:819-820. *Fix:* map `Ranged` to `ExecuteRangedKiterTurn` in the switch and the profile branch, or change the data to RangedKiter and remove the value.
- **AI-004** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Brute and Caster AI archetypes get no profile.** `GameManager.BuildRuntimeAIProfile` has no case for `NPCAIProfileArchetype.Brute` (29 NPC definitions) or `Caster` (2), so those NPCs run the profile-less `AIBehavior` switch without profile targeting, maneuvers, charge or spellcasting; null profiles are tolerated, so nothing crashes. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:981, 991-1049; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:794-795. *Fix:* map Brute to a melee profile (e.g. `BerserkAIProfile`) and Caster to `SpellcasterAIProfile`.
- **AI-035** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Trip-enabled profiles trip every standing target instead of attacking.** `AIProfile.GetPreferredManeuver` returns Trip whenever `AttemptTrip` is set and the target is not prone, and `ShouldUseManeuver` accepts it with no odds check (the trip size limit is `CharacterController.CanTrip` since CMB-079); null-profile NPCs use the same rule in the legacy chooser. Humanoid (goblins, orcs), Berserk and Grappler NPCs in reach of a standing PC trip instead of swinging. Since 2026-10-07 (CMB-102) the trip replaces only one attack step, and the evaluation re-runs before every step with no odds check. A per-turn stopgap (owner decision 2026-10-07, AI-060) stops the worst of it: once a maneuver succeeds the remaining steps are attacks (so a tripped target is attacked, not disarmed and grappled), and a failed maneuver type is not retried against the same target that turn. It does not add an odds check: every standing target in reach still draws a trip with the first step. Since the natural-step change of 2026-10-07 (CMB-102) a maneuver can replace any natural attack, so natural-attack creatures on the trip-enabled profiles (Grappler, Berserk, Humanoid, and null-profile NPCs through the legacy chooser; for example the owlbear, Grappler profile, claw/claw/bite with Improved Grab) would have retried a failed trip, or chained trip, disarm and grapple, with every natural attack and never rolled the claw their Improved Grab needs. Until this issue adds an odds check, `GameManager.PerformNPCMeleeAttackSequence` lets a natural-attack NPC make at most one maneuver substitute per natural sequence; its other natural attacks are rolled (an AI limit, not a rule; PCs may replace as many natural attacks as they like; weapon sequences keep the per-step evaluation, limited by the AI-060 stopgap). Animal-profile creatures are not affected: `AnimalAIProfile.GetPreferredManeuver` returns no maneuver for any specialty (its `ShouldInitiateGrapple` always returns false), so `ShouldUseManeuver` returns false and the profile's `AttemptTrip = true` is never read. Static test `TestNpcNaturalSequenceMakesOneSubstitute` (Grappler profile, the real `AIService` evaluation through the loop: one failed trip, then two natural attacks) passed in Play mode on 2026-10-07; not watched in a live fight. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AIProfile.cs:217-232, 292-319; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:2213-2272; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/HumanoidAIProfile.cs:40; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs (`PerformNPCMeleeAttackSequence`, `naturalSubstituteUsed`); Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/AnimalAIProfile.cs (`GetPreferredManeuver`). *Fix:* Choose a maneuver only when its estimated success chance and value beat a normal attack.
- **AI-037** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Dragons compare no spell with their breath or full attack.** `ExecuteDragonTurn` tries `TryExecuteSpellcastAction` before its breath weapon whenever at most 2 enemies are adjacent (`IsTooCloseForCasting`). Until 2026-10-14 the cast always failed (SPL-123), so dragons went straight to breath and melee; once SPL-123 was fixed the step won every turn with whatever scored highest, so a `dragon_red_young` (cantrips only) cast Resistance in round 1 and Read Magic in round 2 and never breathed (seen in Play mode, run 20261014-134556, `rules/dragon-ai-breath-young` seeds 1-3, before the gate), and 0-level spells were then free (SPL-138, fixed the same day: they are now spent like any spell per day, but a caster level 1 dragon still has 5). What remains: no expected-value comparison. A dragon with its breath ready still spends turns on every unused buff before breathing (the juvenile red casts Mage Armor and Shield, then breathes in round 3), casts a 1st-level attack spell at range instead of closing while the breath recharges, and the adjacency test ignores reach. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs (`ExecuteDragonTurn`, `IsDragonCastFirstCandidate`, `SelectSpell`); Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/DragonAIProfile.cs (`IsTooCloseForCasting`); Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/DragonData.cs (`GetSpellsForCasterLevel`). *Fix:* compare the best spell's expected value with the breath (expected hits times damage) and a full attack, and cast only when it wins; use the dragon's real reach for the melee test.
