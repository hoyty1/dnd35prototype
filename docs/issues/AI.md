# Known issues: AI (AI-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **AI-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **NPCs never cast area spells and never use metamagic.** `GameManager.TryNPCPerformSpellCast` rejects all 62 `TargetType == Area` spells (e.g. Fireball, Web), yet `AIService.SelectSpell` keeps them and profiles favour them, so a caster preferring one falls back to a non-spell action. NPC casts pass null metamagic; `GetMetamagicScore` scores none. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:808-809; Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:928-930. *Fix:* add an NPC area-cast path reusing the PC AoE resolution (or filter Area spells in `SelectSpell`) and choose metamagic from known feats.
- **AI-002** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Casters on melee routines never cast.** Only the Healer, Dragon and RangedKiter routines call `AIService.TryExecuteSpellcastAction`, and profiled NPCs reach RangedKiter only via that `AIBehavior` or a Ranged `CombatStyle`, which the Spellcaster, Abjurer and Vampire profiles lack. So the vampire never casts its spell list; the lich casts only because `LichAIProfile` is Ranged. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:265-279, 651, 896, 970; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/VampireAIProfile.cs:35. *Fix:* call `TryExecuteSpellcastAction` first in the melee routines for NPCs with castable spells, or treat those profiles as casting-capable.
- **AI-003** · **Med** · Player-visible — **`NPCAIBehavior.Ranged` is never handled.** Without a profile `AIService.ExecuteNPCTurn` treats it as AggressiveMelee, and with one only RangedKiter or a Ranged `CombatStyle` reaches the kiter routine, so all current users (3 NPC definitions and the Skeleton Archer) close to melee instead of shooting. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:267-297; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:819-820. *Fix:* map `Ranged` to `ExecuteRangedKiterTurn` in the switch and the profile branch, or change the data to RangedKiter and remove the value.
- **AI-004** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Brute and Caster AI archetypes get no profile.** `GameManager.BuildRuntimeAIProfile` has no case for `NPCAIProfileArchetype.Brute` (29 NPC definitions) or `Caster` (2), so those NPCs run the profile-less `AIBehavior` switch without profile targeting, maneuvers, charge or spellcasting; null profiles are tolerated, so nothing crashes. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:748, 758-815; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:794-795. *Fix:* map Brute to a melee profile (e.g. `BerserkAIProfile`) and Caster to `SpellcasterAIProfile`.
- **AI-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Sanctuary and Hide from Undead saves re-rolled per selection; NPC spells ignore both.** `AIService.SelectBestTarget` rolls and logs a fresh ward save on every run (several per turn), and a successful Hide from Undead save ends the spell; RAW, one save per casting frees only that creature. `AISpellcastingStrategist.SelectBestEnemyTarget` checks no ward, so NPC spells target a Sanctuaried PC freely. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1471-1515, 1684-1764; Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:339-385. *Fix:* cache the save per (attacker, warded target, casting) and reuse the exclusion in the strategist. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: the PC side (PCs never save to attack or target a warded creature) is filed separately as CMB-093.
- **AI-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Monster special attacks bypass the attack and damage pipeline.** `AIService.RollRangedTouchAttack` is d20 + BAB + Dex vs `SimpleTouchAC` with no natural 1/20, no size, range, condition or concealment modifiers and no crit roll. Special-attack and swarm damage go straight to `CharacterStats.TakeDamage`, so e.g. the Gibbering Mouther's acid spittle ignores resistance and immunity. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:3167-3179, 3414-3428. *Fix:* use the standard attack calculator for the touch roll and route damage through `Stats.ApplyIncomingDamage` with a typed `DamagePacket`. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: ranged-special and swarm damage (AIService.cs:3208, 1383) also skip CheckConcentrationOnDamage, unlike every other damage source.
- **AI-007** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Listen checks to pinpoint concealed targets roll several times per round.** `LastKnownPositionTracker.AttemptListenChecks`, documented as one per round, clears `_pinpointedThisRound` and rolls a new DC 20 check on every call (turn start, `AIService.SelectBestTarget` with no visible target, `ExecuteRangedKiterTurn`). NPCs get extra pinpoint chances and repeated log lines; RAW, a retry is a move action. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/LastKnownPositionTracker.cs:128-135; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:865, 1536, 2968. *Fix:* record the round of the last check and return early if it already rolled.
- **AI-032** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Breath weapons and ranged specials never recharge.** `UseBreathWeapon` and `ApplyRangedSpecialAttackCooldown` set cooldowns, but `TickBreathWeaponCooldown`, `TickRangedSpecialAttackCooldown` and `TickTerrainManipulationDuration` have no callers. A dragon or hell hound breathes once per spawn, and cooldown-limited ranged specials fire once. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3802-3842; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:1441; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:10930-10943. *Fix:* Call the three tick methods from `BeginNPCTurnForAI` (and the PC turn start for consistency).
- **AI-035** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Trip-enabled profiles trip every standing target instead of attacking.** `AIProfile.GetPreferredManeuver` returns Trip whenever `AttemptTrip` is set and the target is not prone, and `ShouldUseManeuver` accepts it with no odds or size check; null-profile NPCs use the same rule in the legacy chooser. Humanoid (goblins, orcs), Berserk and Grappler NPCs in reach of a standing PC trip instead of swinging. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AIProfile.cs:217-232, 292-319; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:2213-2272; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/HumanoidAIProfile.cs:40. *Fix:* Choose a maneuver only when its estimated success chance and value beat a normal attack, and enforce the trip size limit.
- **AI-036** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Mirror Image priority target overrides all AI targeting map-wide.** `SelectBestTarget` returns `GetMirrorImagePriorityTargetForAI` before any charm, fear, ward, visibility or range filter and ignores the candidate list it was given, so every enemy anywhere on the map chases the nearest image or caster. Callers that pass a reach-filtered list (around line 3064) still get an out-of-range image. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1471-1483, 3064; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Special/GameManager_MirrorImage.cs:781-826. *Fix:* Apply the mirror-image preference only among candidates that pass the normal filters and the caller's list.
- **AI-037** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Dragons cast any spell before breathing, with no score floor.** `ExecuteDragonTurn` calls `TryExecuteSpellcastAction` before considering its breath weapon whenever it is not in melee, and `SelectSpell` returns the highest-scoring castable spell however low the score. A weak spell or cantrip can pre-empt the breath every turn (the Detect Magic case is inferred, not verified). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:649-657, 2492-2576; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/DragonData.cs:1338. *Fix:* Compare the best spell's expected value with the breath and a full attack, and add a minimum score for casting.
- **AI-038** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Spellcaster-profile monsters on melee routines hold at six squares.** `SpellcasterAIProfile` sets `PreferredRangeSquares = 6` but leaves `CombatStyle` Melee, so on AggressiveMelee `EvaluateMovementOptions` parks it about 6 squares from the target where it can neither attack nor cast. Vampire and Dragon profiles use 2, which ties an adjacent cell (+2 threat) with a 2-square cell, so approach is decided by cell order. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/SpellcasterAIProfile.cs:20-38; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:2074-2124; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/VampireAIProfile.cs:45; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/DragonAIProfile.cs:79. *Fix:* Use the preferred range only on routines that attack or cast at range, and break the threaten tie explicitly.
- **AI-039** · **Low** · Player-visible — **RangedKiter retreats even when the NPC has no ranged weapon.** `shouldRetreatForDistance` (enemy within 2 squares) is not gated on `IsEquippedWeaponRanged`, so a kiter without a bow (common because of ITM-004) backs off, is then out of melee reach, and approaches again next turn. Weaponless ranged-profile monsters oscillate without attacking. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:929-1006; docs/issues/ITM.md ITM-004. *Fix:* Retreat only when the NPC has a ranged attack or castable ranged spell, otherwise fall back to the melee routine.
- **AI-040** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Breath weapon resolution skips save and damage rules and costs no action.** `NPCExecuteBreathWeaponForAI` rolls damage with `UnityEngine.Random`, saves with raw d20 + save (no natural 1/20, no Evasion or situational bonuses), applies only the first matching resistance and calls `Stats.TakeDamage` directly, bypassing `ApplyIncomingDamage`. It never spends a standard action. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:1430-1556. *Fix:* Resolve the save through `SavingThrowResolver` (with Evasion), damage through `ApplyIncomingDamage` with a typed `DamagePacket`, and commit a standard action.
- **AI-041** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Frightful presence fires once, skips charges and ignores HD rules.** `ResolveFrightfulPresence` is triggered only from `NPCPerformAttack` and the breath weapon (not charges) and marks itself used for the rest of combat. It measures Manhattan distance, never exempts creatures with HD at or above the dragon's, compares teams with `==` and rolls a raw Will save with no natural 1/20. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:1081-1083, 1436-1439, 1570-1641. *Fix:* Trigger it on every attack or charge, track per-target immunity after a save, use grid distance and the HD exemption, and roll through `SavingThrowResolver`.
- **AI-042** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Charmed NPCs attack their own allies.** `CharmedBehaviorController.ExecuteDecision` attacks the nearest enemy of the charmer, i.e. the charmed creature's own allies, which Charm Person does not compel. (Its moves are now awaited.) *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Behaviors/CharmedBehaviorController.cs:124-143, 374-401. *Fix:* Have charmed creatures act as non-hostile to the charmer (or follow explicit commands).
- **AI-043** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Confusion: self-damage on 01-10, no retaliation, single-move flee.** On 01-10 a confused creature that cannot reach the caster damages itself (`ResolveSelfAttack`) instead of closing with the caster as the 3.5 table says, and nothing makes a confused creature attack whoever attacked it. The 51-70 flee uses one move action rather than top speed. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Behaviors/ConfusedBehaviorController.cs:85-97, 99-116, 299-324. *Fix:* Replace self-attack with moving toward the caster, add the attacked-retaliates rule, and flee with a double move or run.
- **AI-044** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Frightened run is 1x speed; cornered fear handled wrongly.** The run branch moves via `MoveCharacterAlongComputedPath`, which provokes AoOs like any move but covers only 1x speed. A cornered Frightened creature only sets fighting defensively and never attacks, and a cornered Panicked one logs cowering but gets no Cowering condition (Panicked turns are skipped anyway, CMB-075). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Behaviors/FrightenedBehaviorController.cs:100-147, 152-156. *Fix:* Run at 4x speed, let cornered Frightened creatures attack, and apply Cowering to cornered Panicked ones.
- **AI-045** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Kiter approach move ends the turn with the standard action unused.** When the target is beyond `maxRange`, `ExecuteRangedKiterTurn` moves closer and then ends without attacking or casting, even if the move brought the target into range. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:991-1001. *Fix:* After the approach move, re-check range and attack or cast with the remaining standard action.
- **AI-046** · **Low** — **Strategist spell-resistance chance formula is off by 20.** The strategist estimates SR success as `(CL + 1 - SR) / 20`, but a caster level check d20 + CL >= SR succeeds with probability `(21 + CL - SR) / 20`. NPC casters badly underrate SR-allowing spells against resistant targets. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:431-440. *Fix:* Use `(21 + effectiveCL - SR) / 20f`, clamped to 0-1.
- **AI-047** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Healer heals itself, never moves to touch, may target ally offensively.** `SelectBestAllyTarget` falls back to the caster when no ally is within touch range and the Healer branch never moves toward the wounded ally. When healing fails it casts with the ally as fallback target, and since `IsValidTargetForSpell` lets any spell target any character, an offensive spell can be aimed at the ally. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:246-279, 339-385; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:213-228; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:1286-1290. *Fix:* Move adjacent before touch healing, never fall back to self unless self is the most wounded, and never pass an ally as an offensive spell's fallback target.
- **AI-048** · **Low** — **Domain spell bonus applies to any spell on any domain list.** `GetDomainSpellScore` adds `DOMAIN_SPELL_BONUS` whenever the spell appears on any domain in `AvailableFor`, regardless of the caster's class or chosen domains. Wizards and sorcerers get the bonus for spells such as Fireball. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:1014-1030, 1175. *Fix:* Award the bonus only when the caster has that domain and the spell is in a domain slot.
- **AI-049** · **Low** · Player-visible — **DefensiveMelee targets lowest-HP enemy without ward, charm or visibility filters.** `ExecuteDefensiveMeleeTurn` overrides the selected target with `SelectLowestHPEnemy`, which checks only team and death, so it charges or walks toward Sanctuaried, hidden, fear-source or charmer targets and prefers dying PCs. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1140-1160, 2612-2631. *Fix:* Pick the lowest-HP target from the candidates `SelectBestTarget` already filtered.
- **AI-050** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Summon, swarm, charmed and confused targeting ignore wards and visibility.** `SelectSummonTarget`, the swarm candidate list and the charmed/confused controllers pick the nearest enemy by distance only, with no Sanctuary, Hide from Undead, charm or concealment checks. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1437-1461, 2866-2895; Assets/Scripts/Combat/Behaviors/CharmedBehaviorController.cs:342-369; Assets/Scripts/Combat/Behaviors/ConfusedBehaviorController.cs:50-53. *Fix:* Route these selectors through one shared target filter (attacker, target, action).
- **AI-051** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Melee routines path to the true square of unseen targets.** When `SelectBestTarget` returns a concealed tracked target, the melee and kiter movement calls use `target.GridPosition` (its real square) rather than the last-known position, so NPCs home in on invisible creatures. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:531, 882, 1188. *Fix:* Use the tracker's last-known or pinpointed position for movement toward unseen targets.
- **AI-052** · **Low** · Player-visible — **AI movement scoring accepts A* partial fallback paths.** When A* fails, `SquareGrid` returns a trimmed straight-line fallback path, and `EvaluateMovementOptions` rejects only a null path. Unreachable cells are scored as if reached and the NPC stops short somewhere else. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:2093-2095; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:382-445. *Fix:* Mark fallback results as incomplete and skip cells whose path does not end at the cell.
- **AI-056** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Summoned creatures smite with a made-up formula.** `TryExecuteSummonSmiteAttack` adds +max(0, Cha modifier + 2) to attack and +max(1, Level + 2) to damage. The celestial and fiendish templates give no attack bonus, only extra damage equal to HD (maximum +20) against an evil or good foe (MM p.31 and p.107), and `TemplateSmiteSystem` uses its own formula, so the two smite paths disagree. The damage goes into `MoraleDamageBonus`, which weapon damage ignores (CMB-003). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:192-215. *Fix:* route summon smite through `TemplateSmiteSystem` with the RAW numbers. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: the PC button formula (TemplateSmiteSystem.cs:191-193: +CHA to hit, +HD damage) is not RAW either: MM p.31/p.107 give no attack bonus and +HD (max 20) damage. Templated enemies that are not summons have no smite path at all, because TryExecuteSummonSmiteAttack requires an ActiveSummonInstance (NPCTurns.cs:212-213).
- **AI-057** · **Low** · Player-visible — **NPCs cannot reload a light or hand crossbow and fire in the same turn.** `NPCPerformAttack` reloads through the shared `ExecuteReload` and then `yield break`s, so an NPC never fires in a turn it reloads. A PC reloads with its move action and still attacks with its standard action. RAW: PHB p.116: loading a hand or light crossbow is a move action. Not verified in Play mode. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:1142-1150; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:7464-7521. *Fix:* after a move-action reload, continue into the attack when a standard action remains. Found by the PC/NPC parity audit (docs/systems/PC_NPC_PARITY.md); not verified in Play mode.

- **AI-015** · **Med** — **NPC AI behavior lives in an index-parallel list.** `GameManager.GetNPCBehaviorForAI` reads `_npcAIBehaviors` by `NPCs.IndexOf(npc)`, but `SetupEnemyEncounter` skips the `Add` for an unknown enemy ID (misaligning later NPCs), summons append and `RemoveAt`, and the `LionsShieldBehavior` lion gets no entry. - **AI-054** · **Med** — **PC action executors are bound to the PC turn flow.** `ExecuteAidAnother` calls `ShowActionChoices` and `AfterAttackDelay`, `TryUseConsumableFromInventory` requires `IsPlayerTurn && ActivePC == actor` and its AoO coroutine ends with `EndActivePCTurn`, `PerformAoESpellCast` reads `_pendingSpell`/`_pendingMetamagic` and sets `CurrentSubPhase`, and `ExecuteTurnUndead` drives a PC menu. The NPC Wall of Ice wrappers (CombatActions.cs:2758-2785) reuse PC executors that schedule AfterAttackDelay, which can call EndActivePCTurn -> NextInitiativeTurn during the NPC coroutine (CombatActions.cs:2228, 2343, 2634-2722; CORE-012).
