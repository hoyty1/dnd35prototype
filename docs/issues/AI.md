# Known issues: AI (AI-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **AI-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **NPCs never cast area spells and never use metamagic.** `GameManager.TryNPCPerformSpellCast` rejects all 62 `TargetType == Area` spells (e.g. Fireball, Web), yet `AIService.SelectSpell` keeps them and profiles favour them, so a caster preferring one falls back to a non-spell action. NPC casts pass null metamagic; `GetMetamagicScore` scores none (AoO and Concentration gaps: SPL-006). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:787-788; Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:914-916. *Fix:* add an NPC area-cast path reusing the PC AoE resolution (or filter Area spells in `SelectSpell`) and choose metamagic from known feats.
- **AI-002** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Casters on melee routines never cast.** Only the Healer, Dragon and RangedKiter routines call `AIService.TryExecuteSpellcastAction`, and profiled NPCs reach RangedKiter only via that `AIBehavior` or a Ranged `CombatStyle`, which the Spellcaster, Abjurer and Vampire profiles lack. So the vampire never casts its spell list; the lich casts only because `LichAIProfile` is Ranged. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:265-279, 651, 896, 970; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/VampireAIProfile.cs:35. *Fix:* call `TryExecuteSpellcastAction` first in the melee routines for NPCs with castable spells, or treat those profiles as casting-capable.
- **AI-003** · **Med** · Player-visible — **`NPCAIBehavior.Ranged` is never handled.** Without a profile `AIService.ExecuteNPCTurn` treats it as AggressiveMelee, and with one only RangedKiter or a Ranged `CombatStyle` reaches the kiter routine, so all current users (3 NPC definitions and the Skeleton Archer) close to melee instead of shooting. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:267-297; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:819-820. *Fix:* map `Ranged` to `ExecuteRangedKiterTurn` in the switch and the profile branch, or change the data to RangedKiter and remove the value.
- **AI-004** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Brute and Caster AI archetypes get no profile.** `GameManager.BuildRuntimeAIProfile` has no case for `NPCAIProfileArchetype.Brute` (29 NPC definitions) or `Caster` (2), so those NPCs run the profile-less `AIBehavior` switch without profile targeting, maneuvers, charge or spellcasting; null profiles are tolerated, so nothing crashes. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:748, 758-815; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:794-795. *Fix:* map Brute to a melee profile (e.g. `BerserkAIProfile`) and Caster to `SpellcasterAIProfile`.
- **AI-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Sanctuary and Hide from Undead saves re-rolled per selection; NPC spells ignore both.** `AIService.SelectBestTarget` rolls and logs a fresh ward save on every run (several per turn), and a successful Hide from Undead save ends the spell; RAW, one save per casting frees only that creature. `AISpellcastingStrategist.SelectBestEnemyTarget` checks no ward, so NPC spells target a Sanctuaried PC freely. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1471-1515, 1684-1764; Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:339-385. *Fix:* cache the save per (attacker, warded target, casting) and reuse the exclusion in the strategist.
- **AI-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Monster special attacks bypass the attack and damage pipeline.** `AIService.RollRangedTouchAttack` is d20 + BAB + Dex vs `SimpleTouchAC` with no natural 1/20, no size, range, condition or concealment modifiers and no crit roll. Special-attack and swarm damage go straight to `CharacterStats.TakeDamage`, so e.g. the Gibbering Mouther's acid spittle ignores resistance and immunity. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:3167-3179, 3414-3428. *Fix:* use the standard attack calculator for the touch roll and route damage through `Stats.ApplyIncomingDamage` with a typed `DamagePacket`.
- **AI-007** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Listen checks to pinpoint concealed targets roll several times per round.** `LastKnownPositionTracker.AttemptListenChecks`, documented as one per round, clears `_pinpointedThisRound` and rolls a new DC 20 check on every call (turn start, `AIService.SelectBestTarget` with no visible target, `ExecuteRangedKiterTurn`). NPCs get extra pinpoint chances and repeated log lines; RAW, a retry is a move action. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/LastKnownPositionTracker.cs:128-135; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:865, 1536, 2968. *Fix:* record the round of the last check and return early if it already rolled.

## Unreachable or stubbed features

- **AI-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Metallic dragons' secondary breath weapons are never used.** `DragonAIProfile.EvaluateSecondaryBreathWeapon` has no callers, `WantsToUseSecondaryBreath` is never read and `CharacterController.UseSecondaryBreathWeapon` is never called, so the gases configured at spawn (WeakeningGas, ParalysisGas and others) have no NPC or player execution path. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/DragonAIProfile.cs:58, 234-264; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3754. *Fix:* call `EvaluateSecondaryBreathWeapon` in the dragon turn and add an executor that applies the gas (save, then condition).
- **AI-009** · **Med** — **NPCs never use consumables.** `AIConsumableManager` is added only by `NPCTemplateSystemTests` and `AIService` has no potion, scroll or wand logic, though `QuickSpawnSystem` NPCs carry such items; `NPCTemplateAIConfigurator.ConfigureRuntime` (runtime spellcasting setup) has no callers. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AIConsumableManager.cs:16; Assets/Scripts/AI/NPCTemplateAIConfigurator.cs:23, 54; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:670-675. *Fix:* attach `AIConsumableManager` in NPC setup, query it from the AI turn and call `ConfigureRuntime` for template NPCs, or delete both.
- **AI-010** · **Low** — **Per-profile flee thresholds and the lich fear aura do nothing.** `SpellcasterAIProfile.FleeHealthThreshold` is set (Lich, Vampire) but never read, with no gaseous-form escape; the only retreats are hard-coded (DefensiveMelee below 30% HP, animal grapple escape below 25%). The lich `NPCDefinition` has no `AuraAbility`, so its promised fear aura never fires. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/SpellcasterAIProfile.cs:23; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1155-1167; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_L.cs:273. *Fix:* read `FleeHealthThreshold` instead of 0.30, add the gaseous-form escape and give the lich an `AuraAbilityDefinition`.
- **AI-011** · **Low** — **Strategist multi-round planning and pattern learning are half-built.** `AISpellcastingStrategist.RegisterPlan`, `RecordIneffectiveDamage` and `RecordSpellSaveSuccess` have no callers, so the plan, ineffective-damage and failed-spell stores stay empty and their readers never fire; `GetMultiRoundPlanScore` still gives Buff spells a flat `SETUP_SPELL_BONUS` in rounds 1-2. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:94-131, 415-417, 616, 778-823. *Fix:* call the Record* methods from `TryNPCPerformSpellCast` and `RegisterPlan` when a setup spell is cast, or delete the scaffolding.
- **AI-012** · **Low** — **The AI difficulty setting does nothing.** `AIService` declares an `AIDifficultyLevel` enum (Easy/Normal/Hard) and a serialized `_difficulty` field that nothing reads, so changing it in the Inspector has no effect. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:17-22, 36. *Fix:* wire `_difficulty` into scoring (e.g. deliberate noise at Easy) or remove both.
- **AI-013** · **Low** — **Special-attack status and aura mappings are partial.** `AIService.ApplyStatusEffectFromSpecialAttack` handles only "blinded" and "sickened" (a warning otherwise), and `AuraEffectToCondition` maps unhandled `AuraEffectType` values (today only `Wisdom_Drain`) to Confused; current creature data reaches neither fallback. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:3396-3407, 3484-3501. *Fix:* extend the status switch (or route it through `ConditionService`) and map `Wisdom_Drain` explicitly.
- **AI-014** · **Low** — **Bull rush and overrun target checks are placeholders.** `AIProfile.IsValidBullRushTarget` and `IsValidOverrunTarget` return true for any non-null target and self, with no size or path rules; unreachable today because no profile or creature sets `Maneuvers.AttemptBullRush` or `AttemptOverrun`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AIProfile.cs:276-290, 311-315; Assets/Scripts/AI/AIBehaviorData.cs:62-64. *Fix:* before enabling either flag, add size checks and reuse `OverrunSystem.IsValidOverrunTarget`.

## Development traps

- **AI-015** · **Med** — **NPC AI behavior lives in an index-parallel list.** `GameManager.GetNPCBehaviorForAI` reads `_npcAIBehaviors` by `NPCs.IndexOf(npc)`, but `SetupEnemyEncounter` skips the `Add` for an unknown enemy ID (misaligning later NPCs), summons append and `RemoveAt`, and the `LionsShieldBehavior` lion gets no entry. Code that adds or removes NPCs must keep both lists in step. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:10922-10928; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:46-63, 179; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Armor/LionsShieldBehavior.cs:286. *Fix:* store `NPCAIBehavior` on `CharacterController` and remove the parallel list.
- **AI-016** · **Low** — **AI team checks bypass `TeamUtility`.** `AISpellcastingStrategist` (about 13 sites), `HealerAIProfile.IsAliveAlly` and `AIService.TryAIReadyCounterspell` compare `Team` directly, so a Neutral creature would be a spell target but not a melee target, and Neutral casters would treat other Neutrals as allies. Latent: nothing assigns `CharacterTeam.Neutral`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/TeamUtility.cs:37-54; Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:169, 265, 370. *Fix:* route all AI ally/enemy checks through `TeamUtility.IsEnemy`/`IsAlly` before adding a Neutral faction.
- **AI-017** · **Low** — **Runtime AI profiles leak, and their `OnEnable` overwrites tuning.** Every NPC init, some spawn overrides and swarm summons call `ScriptableObject.CreateInstance` and nothing destroys the instances; `OnEnable` hard-sets tuning fields, so future `.asset` or Inspector values would be lost. `DragonAIProfile` holds per-turn state, so never share an instance between NPCs. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:748; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/SpellcasterAIProfile.cs:25-38; Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/DragonAIProfile.cs:55-93. *Fix:* destroy the profile with its NPC, set defaults only when unset and move per-turn dragon state to a per-NPC context.
- **AI-018** · **Low** — **`AIService` dereferences `CombatUI` without a null check in 7 places.** Two are at the start of `ExecuteNPCTurn`, so any NPC turn without a `CombatUI` (headless test, bare scene) throws `NullReferenceException`; the other 54 uses are null-conditional and the normal scene is unaffected. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:59, 60, 551, 951, 953, 999, 1202. *Fix:* change the 7 `CombatUI.` uses to `CombatUI?.`.

## Performance

- **AI-019** · **Low** — **AI movement scoring runs A* once per candidate cell.** `AIService.EvaluateMovementOptions` and `TryFindSearchDestination` call `GameManager.FindPath` for every reachable cell and allocate new lists, and `EvaluateMovementOptions` has about 10 call sites, some hit several times per turn, so turn cost grows with move range times path cost (not profiled). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:333-418, 2059-2200. *Fix:* run one Dijkstra flood from the mover and score candidates from that cost map.

## Dead code, duplication and tech debt

- **AI-020** · **Med** — **`AIService` is a 3,504-line god class with copy-pasted turn routines.** The Aggressive, Dragon, RangedKiter and Defensive routines repeat the same move, death check, re-target, maneuver-or-attack and `TryAIWallInteraction` sequence, and the dragon's post-breath retreat is copied into both breath branches, so a fix in one must be repeated in the others. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:477, 631, 710-731, 753-773, 835, 1140. *Fix:* extract shared `MoveThenAttack` and `PostBreathRetreat` coroutines and keep only per-archetype decisions in each routine.
- **AI-021** · **Low** — **Two last-known-position stores, never cleared.** `LastKnownPositionTracker` and `CharacterController._lastKnownTargetPositions` both hold positions, and `ClearAllPositions`, `ClearRoundPinpointData`, `ClearLastKnownPosition` and `IsAtLastKnownPosition` have no callers, so pooled NPCs may carry stale PC positions into the next encounter (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/LastKnownPositionTracker.cs:66, 123, 272; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:546, 8796-8822. *Fix:* keep only the tracker and clear it for every pooled NPC in `SetupEnemyEncounter`.
- **AI-022** · **Low** · Player-visible — **AI spell-score terms use mismatched scales.** `SpellcasterAIProfile.ScoreSpell` adds `GetSchoolPriority * 10`, a +100 vs +10 spread for Evoker- and Abjurer-style profiles that swamps the strategist's single- and low-double-digit terms. A Buff spell in rounds 1-2 also gets two near-identical bonuses (`GetPreBuffBonus` up to +20 and `SETUP_SPELL_BONUS` +12). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/Profiles/SpellcasterAIProfile.cs:40-63, 133-136; Assets/Scripts/AI/AISpellcastingStrategist.cs:800-809. *Fix:* scale school priority to the strategist's range and drop `SETUP_SPELL_BONUS` for plain buffs.
- **AI-023** · **Low** · Player-visible — **AI pacing uses hard-coded delays.** `AIService` has 41 `new WaitForSeconds(x)` literals of 0.3-0.8 s (92 across Assets/Scripts) and there is no combat or AI speed setting; only per-step movement speed is centralised (`GetPlayerMoveSecondsPerStepForAI`). *Evidence:* `grep -c 'new WaitForSeconds(' Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs`. *Fix:* route AI delays through one helper scaled by a configurable combat speed.
- **AI-024** · **Low** — **`AIService` and `GameManager` are coupled both ways.** GameManager exposes 40 public `*ForAI` wrappers (34 in `GameManager.cs`), and six services (AIService, CombatFlowService, EconomyService and 3 more) hold a `GameManager _gameManager` set by `Initialize`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:10314, 10879-11036; Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:34. *Fix:* introduce a narrow combat-context interface for AI and service access.
- **AI-025** · **Low** — **NPC maneuver choice is duplicated.** `GameManager.TryNPCSpecialAttackIfBeneficial` makes the real choice; `AIService.ShouldUseManeuver`'s no-profile fallback is a separate gate that does not exclude Improved Grab creatures from grapple, `EvaluateBestManeuver` has no callers, and coup de grace eligibility is computed twice. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:2213-2316, 3094-3115; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:289-343. *Fix:* delete `EvaluateBestManeuver` and make the fallback call the same chooser.
- **AI-026** · **Low** — **11 AI profile settings are never read.** `AIProfile.Aggression`, `MovementPreferences.UseCover`, `DragonAIProfile.PreferBreathThreshold` and 8 more have no readers, so tuning them has no effect. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/AI/AIProfile.cs:28, 36, 61; Assets/Scripts/AI/AIBehaviorData.cs:66-68, 85, 91. *Fix:* wire them into target, movement and maneuver selection, or delete them.
- **AI-027** · **Low** — **`EvaluateAttackOptions` is unused and `SelectBestAction` is only a charge test.** `AIService.EvaluateAttackOptions` has no callers, and `SelectBestAction` returns six `AIActionType` values but its three callers only compare against `Charge`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:2192, 2579-2609. *Fix:* delete `EvaluateAttackOptions` and reduce `SelectBestAction` to a `bool ShouldCharge`.
