# Known issues: Items, store, crafting and treasure (ITM-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **ITM-002** · **High** · Player-visible — **Brewed potions cannot be drunk.** `CraftingExecutor.CreatePotion` leaves `ConsumableEffect` at None, so `GameManager.ApplyConsumableEffectAndConsume` hits its default case and reports "has no implemented consumable effect yet". Crafted scrolls and wands set `ConsumableEffect = SpellEffect` and work. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Crafting/CraftingExecutor.cs:279-297; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:5578-5592. *Fix:* build crafted potions through PotionFactory, or set `ConsumableEffect`, `HealAmount` and `IsStackable` in CreatePotion.
- **ITM-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Selling a potion or scroll stack pays for one and removes all.** StoreUI counts Quantity in ItemData objects, but potions and scrolls merge into one ItemData (`StackCount` up to 20), and `Inventory.RemoveItem` deletes the whole slot. Sell stacks are keyed by name and priced from the first item, so same-named wands all sell at the first one's charge-prorated price. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Store/StoreUI.cs:19-35, 1508-1544; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:179-212. *Fix:* sum StackCount into Quantity, decrement it on partial sells, and price wands per source.
- **ITM-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Using any stored item costs a full-round action.** `GameManager.GetItemUseActionType` always returns "FullRound" (TODO), and using a consumable, picking up an item and casting from a scroll or wand all go through it. RAW: retrieving or picking up an item is a move action; drinking a potion or using a scroll or wand is a standard action. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:4414-4424, 4427-4477. *Fix:* return Move + Standard (Standard if already in hand) per item type, and Move for pickup.
- **ITM-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Item spell resistance and Freedom of Movement stay after unequip.** Inventory sets `SpellResistance = Max(SpellResistance, WondrousGrantedSR)` and never restores the base, so a Mantle of Spell Resistance's SR persists and blocks allies' SR-allowing buffs. `ResetRingBonuses` also never clears `FreedomOfMovementActive`, which is mostly cosmetic. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:898-919 (`ResetRingBonuses`), 983-987, 1122-1125. *Fix:* compute effective SR as max(base, item) at read time and derive FoM-active from `RingGrantsFreedomOfMovement`.
- **ITM-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Sell prices of enhanced weapons and armor are wrong.** `StoreInventory.ResolveBaseValue` prefers the store price for the Id, and crafted upgrades keep the mundane Id, so a crafted +3 longsword sells for 7 gp. Otherwise it adds the enhancement price to a `BasePriceGp` that already includes it, counting it twice. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Store/StoreInventory.cs:410-440; Assets/Scripts/Crafting/CraftingExecutor.cs:355-388. *Fix:* store the mundane base price apart from the market price and use the Id lookup only for unmodified items.
- **ITM-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Scroll save DCs use a different formula from wand save DCs.** Store and loot scrolls fall back to 10 + spell level, wands use 10 + SL + floor(SL/2), and crafted metamagic scrolls bake 10 + level + the crafter's casting modifier. RAW: 10 + spell level + the minimum casting ability's modifier (the wand formula). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Items/ScrollData.cs:138; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/WandFactory.cs:133-137; Assets/Scripts/UI/Crafting/CraftingWorkshopUI.cs:1012-1014. *Fix:* use one magic-item DC helper for scrolls, wands and crafting.
- **ITM-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Metamagic in the Crafting Workshop drops the scroll-substitution cost.** Both the wand and scroll branches overwrite `project.GoldCost` with a recomputed cost, discarding the `ScrollCostGp` that CraftingValidator added, while the cost line still says the scroll cost is included. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/UI/Crafting/CraftingWorkshopUI.cs:986-1028, 1087-1088; Assets/Scripts/Crafting/CraftingValidator.cs:266-269. *Fix:* add `project.ScrollCostGp` back after recomputing GoldCost.
- **ITM-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Armor never reduces speed.** `CharacterStats.EffectiveSpeedFeet` applies only weight-based encumbrance, with no armor-category term, so mithral's lighter category matters only for proficiency and Barbarian fast movement. RAW: medium and heavy armor reduce speed (30 ft to 20 ft, 20 ft to 15 ft). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:1231-1247; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Materials/MaterialProperties.cs:198-210. *Fix:* apply the reduction in EffectiveSpeedFeet using `MaterialProperties.GetEffectiveArmorCategory`. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: nine NPC entries bake the armor speed reduction into BaseSpeed (NPCDatabase_P.cs:207, 259, 315; NPCDatabaseCustom.cs:523; NPCDatabase_O.cs:155; H.cs:417; S.cs:550; J.cs:31; F.cs:323), so an NPC paladin in full plate moves 20 ft while a PC paladin moves 30 ft. Restore unarmored speeds in the data when this is fixed.
- **ITM-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Robes fit over body armor, and Monk's Belt uses the wrong slot.** Wondrous robes (Archmagi, Eyes, Blending and others) use `EquipSlot.Torso`, separate from `ArmorRobe`, so a robe and body armor can be worn together; Monk's Belt is slotted Torso instead of Waist. RAW: a robe shares the body slot with armor. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Wondrous/WondrousItemFactory.cs:346, 1328-1460, 1678. *Fix:* move robes to ArmorRobe (or block Torso robe plus armor) and Monk's Belt to Waist.
- **ITM-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The halberd is a reach weapon, and a test locks that in.** `ItemDatabase` registers it with `IsReachWeapon = true`, `ReachSquares = 2` and `CanAttackAdjacent = false`, and `ReachWeaponRulesTests` asserts this. RAW (PHB): the halberd is not a reach weapon; the glaive, guisarme, longspear and ranseur are. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Items/ItemDatabase.cs:614-625; Assets/Scripts/Tests/Combat/ReachWeaponRulesTests.cs:26. *Fix:* make it non-reach (reach fields, `CanAttackAdjacent`, description) and change the test in the same commit.
- **ITM-013** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Carried weight and item removal ignore stacks and slotless items.** `Inventory.GetTotalCarriedWeightLbs` ignores `StackCount` (N potions weigh as one) and ammunition always weighs a full bundle; `Inventory.RemoveItem` never searches `SlotlessItems`. `CharacterInventory` helpers skip SlotlessItems too, but their only callers are never called. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:644-686, 179-225; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/CharacterInventory.cs:157-203. *Fix:* weight by StackCount and remaining ammo; add a SlotlessItems pass to RemoveItem.
- **ITM-014** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Test wondrous items are sold in the store and offered for crafting.** `WondrousItemDatabase.Init` registers 10 "Test ... - **ITM-064** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Ring of the Ram regains 1d10 charges on every rest.** RingFactory gives the Ring of the Ram RingChargesPerDay = 10 and RingActivationManager calls RingChargeManager.RegenerateCharges on each post-combat rest, adding 1d10 charges; the 3.5 DMG ring has 50 charges and does not recharge, so the comment citing 'DMG p.233' is wrong and the ring is effectively unlimited. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Rings/RingFactory.cs:594-596; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Rings/RingChargeManager.cs:38-56; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Rings/RingActivationManager.cs:839-841. *Fix:* Set RingChargesPerDay = 0 for the Ring of the Ram (non-recharging) and correct the RegenerateCharges comment.
- **ITM-066** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Craft Magic Arms and Armor can never succeed.** All three CraftableItemRegistry entries for Craft Magic Arms and Armor have IsUpgrade = true, CraftingWorkshopUI._upgradeTarget is only ever assigned null, and CraftingValidator rejects any upgrade without a target ('Enhancement upgrade requires selecting a target item from inventory'). - **ITM-067** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Vorpal decapitation resolves before Fortification can negate the crit.** The Vorpal check kills the target (TakeDamage(CurrentHP+100)) inside the enchantment block before the defender's Fortification roll runs, so a Fortification success that negates the crit cannot stop the decapitation; Vorpal also inherits the missing crit-immunity check (CMB-064). - **ITM-068** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Ring energy resistance overwrites and outlives a Resist Energy spell entry.** ApplyRingBonuses passes a 999999-round effect to SetResistEnergyEffect, which for an existing same-type entry mutates that entry (amount, duration, Caster) instead of adding the ring's object, yet the ring tracks its own unadded object; RemoveRingEnergyResistances then removes nothing, so the spell entry keeps the ring's value permanently after unequip. - **ITM-071** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Weapons have no size of their own: every weapon fits its current wielder.** `ItemData.DesignedForSize` is Medium for every item (nothing sets another value), and `WeaponDamageScaler` treats it as the Medium reference: `Inventory.ApplyWeaponStats` and `CharacterController.GetScaledWeaponDamageDice` resize every weapon to the wielder's current size (DMG Tables 2-2 and 2-3, p.28). ## Development traps

- **ITM-029** · **Med** · Player-visible — **There are two unconnected gold pools.** The store, loot and party gold use `EconomyService.PartyGold`, while crafting and costly material components spend per-character `CharacterStats.ComponentGold`, which starts at 1000 and refills only from crafting-failure refunds. Nothing moves gold between them. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:196, 3492-3504; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Casting/SpellComponentSystem.cs:434-441. *Fix:* spend crafting and component costs from EconomyService, or add a transfer UI.
- **ITM-030** · **Med** — **`Inventory.AddItem` drops per-instance state on stackable items.** Stackables merge by Id alone and the incoming instance is discarded, and any remainder becomes new stacks cloned from the ItemDatabase template, so a custom name, charges, baked DC or metamagic is lost.
