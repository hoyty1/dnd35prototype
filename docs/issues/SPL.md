# Known issues: Spells and metamagic (SPL-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **SPL-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **Save DC formula depends on the code path; Sorcerer and Bard DCs use WIS.** SpellCaster.Cast uses `IsWizard ? INTMod : WISMod` plus Spell Focus and Heighten; GetSpellSaveDC (Fireball) and CombatCalculationService.SpellSaveDC (Sleep) use the right ability but no Spell Focus or Heighten. Save-modifier and SR helpers also exist three times each. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Casting/SpellCaster.cs:370-382`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:5018-5030`. *Fix:* one DC function (class ability, Spell Focus, Heighten level) for every path, plus merged save and SR helpers.
- **SPL-002** · **High** · Player-visible — **Spells that set only BuffDurationRounds get a 0-round duration.** 54 spells (Aid, Doom, Silence and others) leave DurationType at its Instantaneous default, so effects added through StatusEffectManager.AddEffect start at 0 rounds and expire on the first tick (not verified in Play mode). MinutesPerLevel has no case, so Invisibility Purge gets a fixed 50 rounds. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/StatusEffects/ActiveSpellEffect.cs:133-181,187-194`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Data/SpellData.cs:375,400`. *Fix:* normalize at registration (rounds > 0 to Rounds, -1 to Hours per level) and add a MinutesPerLevel case.
- **SPL-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Silence, Death Knell and Align Weapon never expire.** `TickClericSpell2Durations`, the only code that clears their flags, has no callers, and `RestorePartyAfterCombat` does not reset them. A silenced character never casts verbal spells again, and the Death Knell STR bonus is never reversed. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Effects/EffectService.cs:278-334`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:8147-8148`. *Fix:* call it next to the level 3/4 tick calls, and clear the flags (reversing Death Knell STR) in `RestorePartyAfterCombat`.
- **SPL-004** · **High** · Player-visible — **Custom spell damage bypasses energy resistance, immunity and DR.** 22 call sites in 11 spell files use `Stats.TakeDamage(int)` instead of `ApplyIncomingDamage`, so Fireball, Cone of Cold, Wall of Fire and others ignore resistance, immunity and DR. Spike Stones, Black Tentacles and two domain handlers write `CurrentHP -=`, also skipping temp HP and death handling. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager_Spells_Shared.cs:263`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/AreaEffects/SpikeStoneAreaEffect.cs:55`. *Fix:* send spell damage through one helper that builds a typed `DamagePacket` for `ApplyIncomingDamage`.
- **SPL-005** · **High** · Player-visible — **Generic spell dice ignore caster level; Cure and Inflict ignore undead.** `SpellCaster.Cast` uses the registered dice unchanged, so Burning Hands is always 3d4 and Cure Light Wounds 1d8+3 (there only Magic Missile's count scales). Its generic branches let Cure heal undead and Inflict harm them; Heal's undead branch is unreachable (SPL-037). *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Casting/SpellCaster.cs:455-470,517-557`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Database/SpellDatabase_B.cs:407`. *Fix:* add capped per-CL dice and bonus fields to SpellData, and invert positive and negative energy on undead.
- **SPL-006** · **High** · Player-visible — **AoE, NPC, summon and Grease casts skip pre-cast checks.** `PerformAoESpellCast` skips the entangled concentration check, Blink's 20% failure and the Ring of Counterspells. `TryNPCPerformSpellCast` never provokes AoOs, rolls defensive Concentration or checks the Ring, and summon and Grease casts never call `TryResolveCounterspell`. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:1782,1878,3567-4843`, `Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:758-958`. *Fix:* extract the shared pre-cast steps (concentration, Blink, ASF, AoO, counterspell, Ring) into one method all four pipelines call.
- **SPL-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Per-class spell levels are ignored when casting, preparing and computing DCs.** About 250 non-test reads use `SpellData.SpellLevel` instead of `GetSpellLevelFor(class)`, so a Bard with See Invisibility (Bard 3) or a Sorcerer with Bestow Curse (Sor/Wiz 4) can never cast it, and InitWizard drops Bestow Curse and Contagion. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Data/SpellData.cs:51-134`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SpellcastingComponent.cs:540-543,2120,2617`. *Fix:* resolve the casting class per cast and use `GetSpellLevelFor(class)` for slots, CanCast/SpendSlot, the spellbook filter, DC and concentration.
- **SPL-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **School strings with a subschool or descriptor break every school comparison.** 40 spells store text such as "Evocation [Fire]" in `School`, which exact compares miss: Spell Focus never applies, an Evocation specialist cannot put Fireball in the specialist slot, and prohibited-school checks never block them. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:450-465`, `Assets/Scripts/Character/Specialization/WizardSpecialization.cs:97-111`. *Fix:* compare with `SpellSchoolUtils.Parse(spell.School)` (now only the AI calls it), or split School into an enum plus subschool and descriptor fields.
- **SPL-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Enlarge Spell does not extend standard ranges, and it doubles area.** It doubles `RangeSquares`, which `GetRangeSquaresForCasterLevel` ignores except for Custom range, so 116 Close/Medium/Long and 92 Touch spells pay +1 level for nothing. It also doubles `AoESizeSquares`/`AreaRadius` (quadrupled with Widen), and its test checks only `RangeSquares`. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Casting/SpellCaster.cs:786-805,837-851`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Data/SpellRanges.cs:27-57`. *Fix:* add a range multiplier that GetRangeSquaresForCasterLevel honors, limit CanEnlarge to Close/Medium/Long, and drop the area doubling.
- **SPL-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Multiclass characters with a spontaneous class cannot cast their prepared spells.** CanCastSpell, GetCastablePreparedSpells and CastSpellFromSlot return from the IsSpontaneousCaster branch, and RestoreAllSlots returns early for spontaneous and partial casters, so a Wizard/Sorcerer never casts prepared spells and a Cleric/Paladin never regains them. Only one spontaneous class is supported (Sorcerer wins over Bard). *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SpellcastingComponent.cs:411-437,2117-2140,3204-3222`. *Fix:* dispatch on the chosen spell's casting class, and restore every subsystem in RestoreAllSlots.
- **SPL-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Plain casts can spend locked, disabled or metamagic slots.** CastSpellFromSlot takes the first matching `!IsUsed` slot, ignoring DisabledByNegativeLevel, LockedByImbue and HasMetamagic; the CastWizardSpellWithMetamagic lookups ignore locks too, and its last resort spends any unused slot at the effective level. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SpellcastingComponent.cs:2163-2166,2374-2391`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Data/SpellSlot.cs:116`. *Fix:* use `s.CanCast` and prefer non-metamagic slots, and limit the metamagic fallbacks to CanCast slots of the same spell and class.
- **SPL-013** · **Med** · Player-visible — **RefreshSpellSlots drops metamagic, BaseSpellLevel and imbue locks.** It restores only PreparedSpell, IsUsed and DisabledByNegativeLevel from SpellSlotSnapshot, so any refresh (level-up, Ring of Wizardry equip or unequip, character creation) leaves the plain base spell in a metamagic slot and unlocks imbue-locked slots. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SpellcastingComponent.cs:827-916,1152-1183`, `Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Rings/RingActivationManager.cs:934,975`. *Fix:* add AppliedMetamagic (cloned), BaseSpellLevel and LockedByImbue to SpellSlotSnapshot and restore them.
- **SPL-014** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Manual preparation rejects domain-only spells in domain slots.** IsValidSpellForSlot and PrepareSpellInSlot require IsSpellKnownByClass, and the Cleric known list omits domain spells, so Enlarge Person (Strength 1) reaches a domain slot only through AutoPrepareClericSlots. Calm Animals is also registered as Cleric 1 (PHB: Animal 1, Druid 1, Ranger 1). *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SpellcastingComponent.cs:1375-1385,1531-1546`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Database/SpellDatabase_C.cs:28-33`. *Fix:* skip the known-spell check for domain slots, or add domain spells to the known list.
- **SPL-015** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Character-creation prepared spells are matched to slots by position.** ApplyPreparedSpellSlotIds assigns by index, so any difference in bonus or domain slots shifts spells into wrong slots, where they are rejected and stay empty (by static reading, Shield of Faith and Flame Strike in the L3 Cleric quickstart). - **SPL-016** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Scroll and wand casts use the user's caster level, not the item's.** The scroll and wand pipelines compute the item CL but only log it. Damage dice, duration, range and the SR check use the user's caster level, while the item's save DC is applied. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:5983-5995,6106-6110`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Casting/SpellCastingHelper.cs:103-109`, `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Casting/SpellCaster.cs:906-920`. *Fix:* add a pending caster-level override (like the SaveDC override) that GetEffectiveCasterLevel and the SR check honor during scroll and wand casts.
- **SPL-017** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Caster level is computed inconsistently.** The range highlight, generic buff path and some summon and duration helpers use character level (`Stats.Level`); SpellCaster.Cast and the AoE and NPC paths use GetCasterLevel(), the highest casting class level when no class is given.
