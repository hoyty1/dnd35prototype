# Known issues: Spells and metamagic (SPL-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **SPL-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **AoE, summon, Grease and held-charge casts skip some pre-cast checks.** `PerformAoESpellCast` skips the entangled check (itself wrong, SPL-145), Blink's 20% failure and the Ring of Counterspells.
