# Known issues: Characters, feats and classes (CHR-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CHR-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **Created PCs get CON hit points twice, clamped to +1 per level.** ComputeFinalStats already adds conMod*level and the CharacterStats constructor adds max(1, CON mod)*level again, which also gives CON 10-11, NO_SCORE and negative-CON creatures +1 HP per level (RAW: CON mod per Hit Die, minimum 1 HP per die). MaxHP is never recomputed later. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterCreation/CharacterCreationData.cs:107-111; Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3430-3435. *Fix:* pass CON-free hit-die HP and add GetModifier(CON) per level (negative allowed, 0 for NO_SCORE), floored at 1 HP per level. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: the same constructor term also double-counts CON (and Toughness via TotalMaxHP) for NPCs whose BaseHitDieHP is an MM total; see CRE-041.
- **CHR-002** · **High** · Player-visible — **Hard-coded BAB and hit-die tables contradict the class definitions and RAW.** Three switch tables give Bard 1/2 BAB (BardClass.BABAtLevel3 says 3/4, as RAW), and the live BaseAttackBonus getter sums one of them for every PC; level-up rolls Rogue and Bard on d8 and Ranger on d10 (RAW: d6, d6, d8). *Evidence:* CharacterStats.CalculateClassBaseAttackBonus, CharacterStats.GetClassHitDieSize, LevelUpCalculator.CalculateClassBab, CharacterCreationData.CalculateClassBab. *Fix:* derive BAB and hit die from ClassRegistry.GetClass(name), as CreatureClassEngine does, and delete the tables.
- **CHR-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Monk features are fixed at low-level values, and the flurry penalty is wrong.** GetFlurryOfBlowsBonuses uses -2/-1/0 at monk levels 1/2/3+ and exactly two attacks (RAW: -2 to level 4, -1 at 5-8, plus iteratives), so the default L3 monk flurries at +4/+4, not +2/+2. Unarmed die, fast movement, monk AC and Stunning Fist uses ignore monk level, and Bracers of Armor switch off monk AC and speed. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:883-913, 940-953, 2204-2212; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:1110-1117. *Fix:* scale these by monk level per the PHB, add extra flurry attacks and track bracers separately.
- **CHR-004** · **High** · Player-visible — **Combat XP does not scale with party level.** ExperienceCalculator.GetXPForCR keys a flat table only by CR - APL, so a CR = APL creature is worth 300 XP total at any level, split across the party (DMG: 300 x level, 1,500 at level 5). Level thresholds follow 3.5e, so progression slows sharply after level 1. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Progression/ExperienceCalculator.cs:28-51, 222-233. *Fix:* index the award by (party level, CR) as in the DMG, or multiply the delta-table value by APL.
- **CHR-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Destruction domain smite never adds its damage.** PerformSingleAttackWithCrit calls ConsumeDestructionSmite right after the attack roll, so GetDestructionSmiteDamageBonus later sees the cleared flag and returns 0, and the crit branch never adds it at all; only the +4 attack bonus applies. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:6653, 6869, 7141-7169; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Domain/GameManager_DomainPowers.cs:440-465. *Fix:* read the damage bonus together with the attack bonus and add it in both damage branches.
- **CHR-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Level-up feat selection drops Weapon Focus weapon choices.** CharacterCreationManager.ApplySelectedFeats (also used by LevelUpUI) adds only feat names and never copies FeatSelectionUI.GetAllSelectedWeapons() into stats.WeaponFocusWeapons, so a first Weapon Focus taken at level-up gives +0 and a second one for a new weapon is lost. Creation copies the weapons correctly. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterCreation/CharacterCreationManager.cs:221-237; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:168-171. *Fix:* add each selected weapon to stats.WeaponFocusWeapons in the onConfirmed callbacks.
- **CHR-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Class auto-granted feats are applied only at construction.** CharacterStats.InitFeats (which starts with Feats.Clear()) runs only from the constructor and test configs, so multiclassing into Monk or Wizard grants no Improved Unarmed Strike or Scribe Scroll, and a Ranger reaching level 3 by level-up never gets Endurance. Calling it later would wipe selected feats. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2084-2100, 3442. *Fix:* add an additive per-class-level hook (for example ICharacterClass.GrantLevelFeatures) called from ApplyPendingLevelUp.
- **CHR-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Feats are bare names: no per-weapon choice, no stacking, inert NPC parameters.** Feats is a `HashSet<string>`, so Toughness and Extra Turning cannot stack, Weapon Specialization and the Greater feats cover every Weapon Focus weapon, and Improved Critical all weapons. NPC entries such as 'Weapon Focus (longsword)' or 3.0 'Expertise' are copied verbatim and do nothing (Multiattack is read by its bare name since 2026-10-07: `CharacterStats.GetNaturalAttackSequencePenalty`, CMB-102; the animal companion's `AnimalCompanionData.HasMultiattack` (effective druid level 9+) is set but read by nothing and never becomes that feat, beyond the level 1-8 scope). Weapon Focus never applies to a natural attack: the natural sequence in `CharacterController.FullAttack` builds its modifier with no weapon, so `FeatManager.GetWeaponFocusBonus(stats, null)` looks up "Unarmed Strike", and the wolf's bare 'Weapon Focus' with `WeaponFocusChoice` "Bite" does nothing: in Play mode (scenario `rules/maneuver-freetrip`, 2026-10-07) the MM wolf bites at +2, not the MM's +3. The CRE-045 audit (2026-10-08) wrote the MM's natural-weapon Weapon Focus and Improved Natural Attack entries in the 'Name (param)' form, inert until this is fixed. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:136-160, 326-335; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:603-609. *Fix:* model feats as instances with a parameter and count, parse 'Name (param)' for NPCs, and tie weapon feats to one weapon.
- **CHR-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Augment Summoning boosts fields nothing reads.** FeatManager.ApplyAugmentSummoningBonuses adds +4 to BaseSTR and BaseCON, pre-racial creation inputs that STRMod and CONMod ignore, so summons get only the +2 HP per HD. Phase2SpecializedTacticsTests asserts on BaseSTR/BaseCON and enshrines the bug. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:866-876; Assets/Scripts/Tests/Feats/Phase2SpecializedTacticsTests.cs:251-270. *Fix:* apply the +4 to the effective STR and CON and assert STRMod/CONMod in the test.
- **CHR-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Bonus stacking is ad hoc outside spell effects.** Only spell effects get same-type stacking; other bonuses are hand-summed, so Ring of Protection stacks with Shield of Faith (whose expiry subtracts twice), item STR enhancement with Bull's Strength, and two enhancement speed bonuses with each other. Dodge and Haste AC stay when DEX is denied, and touch AC omits WondrousInsightACBonus. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/StatusEffectManager.cs:46-123, 312-316, 597-599; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Inventory/Inventory.cs:928-937. *Fix:* total typed per-source bonuses with BonusTypeHelper.DoesStack and fix the dodge and touch-AC sums.
- **CHR-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Healing goes to nonlethal damage first; stabilization uses a d20 check.** CharacterStats.HealDamage (16 call sites) removes nonlethal damage first and heals lethal damage only with the remainder (RAW: both by the full amount). Dying stabilization is d20 + CON mod vs DC 10 (RAW: 10% per round). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:4743-4775; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:4780-4803. *Fix:* heal nonlethal and lethal damage independently by the full amount, and stabilize on d100 <= 10 (or document a house rule).
- **CHR-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **MaxHP is not re-derived when CON changes.** MaxHP is stored and adjusted by ad hoc deltas: CON damage and drain do not change HP, and a level-up CON increase gives no retroactive HP. Rage, GrantInherentBonus and the generic CON buff scale by total Level, while Bear's Endurance uses Hit Dice. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2119-2133, 2385-2404; Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterSheet/LevelUpUI.cs:627-645. *Fix:* compute CON HP as a derived TotalMaxHP term, (CONMod - baseCONMod) * HitDice, instead of mutating MaxHP.
- **CHR-013** · **Low** · Player-visible — **AlignmentHelper.IsWithinOneStep accepts diagonal steps.** It returns diffLC <= 1 && diffGE <= 1, so LG counts as one step from TN (also for cleric summon limits), and DeityAlignmentRestrictionTests enshrines this. SummonMonsterAlignmentRulesTests expects orthogonal steps and fails 2 assertions: a lawful neutral or chaotic neutral cleric sees 7 Summon Monster II options, not 3 (2026-10-07). Creation enforces deity alignment for every class, not only clerics. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/Alignment.cs:128-146; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:1942-1953. *Fix:* use (diffLC + diffGE) <= 1, update the TN test, and restrict deity enforcement to clerics.
- **CHR-014** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Rolled skill checks omit the familiar bonus; no synergies, Craft or Profession.** CharacterStats.RollSkillCheck skips GetFamiliarSkillModifier, which GetSkillBonus includes, so the character-sheet roll can differ from the displayed bonus. Skill synergies are not implemented, and ClassSkillDefinitions has no Craft or Profession. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:5941-5954, 5994-6045. *Fix:* make RollSkillCheck use d20 + GetSkillBonus(skillName).
- **CHR-015** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Level-up save preview is wrong.** LevelUpCalculator.EstimateProjectedBestSave takes the highest class base save instead of the sum, and New*Save omits luck, resistance and familiar bonuses that Old*Save includes. LevelUpUI shows a save line only when New > Old, so increases are often hidden. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Progression/LevelUpCalculator.cs:121-163; Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterSheet/LevelUpUI.cs:233-240. *Fix:* projected save = current save - current class base + projected class base, reusing GetTotalClassSaveBonus.
- **CHR-016** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Feat ability prerequisites read buffed scores.** FeatPrerequisite.IsMet reads live ability scores that Rage, Enlarge, Bull's Strength-style buffs and others modify directly, so a buffed character can qualify for a feat its base score would not allow (RAW: temporary bonuses do not count). This matters only when choosing feats while buffed. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/Feat.cs:140-152. *Fix:* check prerequisites against Base* scores plus permanent modifiers.
- **CHR-017** · **Low** — **Ranger Endurance uses total character level.** RangerClass.InitFeats grants Endurance when stats.Level is at least 3, so a Fighter 2/Ranger 1 gets it early (RAW: ranger level 3). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/Ranger/RangerClass.cs:79-97. *Fix:* use stats.GetClassLevel("Ranger") >= 3.
- **CHR-056** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Bards are not restricted to nonlawful alignments.** AlignmentHelper.IsAlignmentValidForClass and GetClassRestrictionText have no Bard case, so character creation and level-up accept a lawful Bard (PHB ch.3 requires any nonlawful). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/Alignment.cs:168-196, 201-211. *Fix:* Add a Bard case returning !IsLawful(alignment) and the matching restriction text.
- **CHR-057** · **Low** — **Stunning Fist daily uses are never refreshed by rest.** StunningFistUsesRemaining starts at -1 and ResetStunningFistUses runs only while it is negative (once, on first check); RestorePartyAfterCombat resets rage, turn undead, bardic music and domain uses but not Stunning Fist. Latent until CMB-022 makes Stunning Fist usable, after which uses run out for the whole session. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:506-508; Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2213-2218; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:957-981. *Fix:* Call stats.ResetStunningFistUses() in RestorePartyAfterCombat alongside the other per-day resets.
- **CHR-058** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Multiclass XP penalty logic is wrong and never stacks per class.** RecalculateXPPenaltyStatus drops all classes at the highest level, then the favored class, and penalizes only if the remaining classes differ among themselves by more than 1, so two-class characters (e.g. gnome Fighter 5/Wizard 1) and classes equally far below the highest are never penalized; favored class 'Any' skips the check entirely. GetEffectiveXpGain applies a flat 20% instead of 20% per offending class. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3618-3651; Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3445-3456. *Fix:* Ignore only the favored class (highest class for 'Any'), compare each remaining class to the highest remaining, and apply -20% per class two or more levels below; add tests.
- **CHR-059** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Default Bard loses Read Magic to an unregistered spell ID.** BardClass lists the cantrip "read_magic_wiz", which is neither registered nor aliased. The GetSpell(id) != null filter drops it, logs a miss warning, and the default Bard ends up with 5 of its 6 cantrips. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/Bard/BardClass.cs:127-132; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Database/SpellDatabase_R.cs:478. *Fix:* Use SpellNames.READ_MAGIC and SpellNames constants for the other literal IDs.
- **CHR-062** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Elemental domain turning targets the wrong subtype and shares Turn Undead uses.** ActivateElementalTurning uses the domain name as the subtype, so an Air cleric affects air creatures, and it turns or rebukes by alignment instead of PHB's fixed mapping (Air turns earth and rebukes air, and so on); it also spends Turn Undead attempts instead of a separate 3 + CHA pool. - **CHR-063** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Luck domain reroll is pre-armed and keeps the better roll.** The Luck power is armed before the roll and fires on the next attack, save or skill d20, even a natural 20, and ApplyLuckReroll keeps Max(original, reroll). - **CHR-064** · **Low** · Player-visible — **War domain feats are granted only at initial creation.** GrantWarDomainFeats runs only in GameManager's creation path; the level-up domain step in CharacterCreationManager sets ChosenDomains and refreshes slots but never grants Weapon Focus or favored-weapon proficiency. - **CHR-065** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Strength domain bonus is untyped and stacks with enhancement.** ActivateStrengthDomain adds cleric level to the untyped TemporarySTRBonus, which EffectiveStrengthScore adds on top of Bull's Strength and item enhancement (WondrousEnhancementSTR). - **CHR-069** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Inspire Courage picks recipients from the PCs list, and rage and bardic-music durations tick only on the PC turn path.** `ApplyInspireCourageToParty` and `RemoveInspireCourageFromParty` loop over `PCs`. - **CHR-070** · **Med** · Player-visible — **NPC casters cannot have class choices: no cleric domains, domain slots or spontaneous conversion, and no wizard specialization or familiar.** `NPCDefinition` has no deity, domain, spontaneous-casting, specialization or familiar fields, and `InitializeNPCFromDefinition` sets none of them. - **CHR-071** · **High** · Player-visible — **PC Hit Dice freeze at creation level, while HD-gated rules that read Stats.Level miscount templated and class-levelled monsters.** `CharacterStats.HitDice` is set once in the constructor (`HitDice = Max(1, level)`) and `ApplyPendingLevelUp` never updates it. Not verified in Play mode. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3366, :3566-3628; Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/TeamUtility.cs:77-81; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:1904-1911; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:72-73; ZombieTemplate.cs:62-63; Assets/Scripts/Character/CreatureClass/CreatureClassEngine.cs:147-159; TurnUndeadSystem.cs:491, :539. *Fix:* derive HD once (racial HD from the definition plus the sum of ClassLevels) as `CharacterStats.GetHitDice()`. Point `TeamUtility.GetHitDice` and every HD-gated Stats.Level reader at it. Keep Level for XP, feats and skill caps. Found by the PC/NPC parity audit (docs/systems/PC_NPC_PARITY.md); not verified in Play mode.
- **CHR-072** · **High** · Player-visible — **NPC classes and monster creature types grant no weapon or armor proficiency, so armed NPCs attack at -4 plus their armor and shield ACP.** The proficiency lists in `CharacterStats` name only PHB classes, and NPCs have no race. Per MM p.310-311, creature types are proficient with simple weapons, the weapons in their entry and the armor they wear. `WarriorClass` declares its proficiencies but nothing reads them. CHR-022 (no proficiency feats) is related. Not verified in Play mode. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:5268-5308, :5313-5358, :5395-5441; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:5041-5042; Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/NPC/WarriorClass.cs:62-66; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_G.cs:325-353. *Fix:* give ICharacterClass proficiency data and read it instead of name lists. In `InitializeNPCFromDefinition`, add every listed weapon to `ExtraWeaponProficiencies` and the worn armor category (and lighter) plus shields to a new extra-armor-proficiency field, so MM creatures are proficient with what they carry. Found by the PC/NPC parity audit (docs/systems/PC_NPC_PARITY.md); not verified in Play mode.
- **CHR-073** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Weapon Finesse replaces Strength even when Strength is higher.** `FeatManager.ShouldUseWeaponFinesse` returns true for any light weapon, a rapier or no weapon (natural attacks and unarmed strikes), and `AttackCalculator.GetAttackAbilityModifier` then uses the Dexterity modifier without comparing it with Strength. ## Development traps

- **CHR-068** · **Med** — **Assigning `CharacterStats.BaseAttackBonus` is silently ignored for any character with class levels.** The setter writes `_baseAttackBonus`, but the getter returns `BaseAttackBonusOverride`, the creature-type progression or the sum over `ClassLevels` first, and reads the backing field only when none of those apply. The positional constructor's `bab` argument and test writes such as `Stats.BaseAttackBonus = 20` therefore do nothing for a classed character; the GrappleDamageRulesTests helpers relied on it, which made several of their checks dice-dependent until they switched to `BaseAttackBonusOverride` on 2026-10-07 (TST-027). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2431-2461 (getter and setter), 3418 (constructor). *Fix:* have tests set `BaseAttackBonusOverride` or build a character of the right level, and make the setter warn when `ClassLevels` exist. Do not simply redirect the setter to the override: the constructor's `bab` would then replace class BAB for every PC.
- **CHR-029** · **Med** — **FeatBenefit values look authoritative but are mostly unread.** FeatDefinitions fills about 55 FeatBenefit fields, but production code reads only SkillBonuses, RequiresWeaponChoice and Description. Feat effects are hard-coded HasFeat("<name>") checks, so editing a value such as Weapon Focus's AttackBonus changes nothing. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/Feat.cs:158-230; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:372. *Fix:* drive feat effects from FeatBenefit, or strip the unused fields.
- **CHR-030** · **Med** — **HasFeat is a case-sensitive magic-string lookup.** There are no FeatNames or ClassNames constants and about 150 HasFeat("literal") calls (142 outside tests); HasFeat is Feats.Contains on a `HashSet<string>` with the default comparer, so a mis-cased or misspelled name silently returns false. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:1875-1878. *Fix:* add FeatNames (and ClassNames) constants and construct Feats with StringComparer.OrdinalIgnoreCase.
- **CHR-031** · **Med** — **Two IsDead/IsUnconscious APIs with different meanings.** The IsDead pair (HP <= -10 vs HPState.Dead) agrees unless HP is written without SyncHPStateFromCurrentHP. CharacterStats.IsUnconscious (0 >= HP > -10) counts Disabled and Diehard characters and misses nonlethal and Unconscious-condition cases; it has 5 references. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3298-3314; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:4602-4648. *Fix:* use the CharacterController properties for gameplay, and fix or delegate Stats.IsUnconscious.
- **CHR-032** · **Med** — **The positional CharacterStats constructor puts WIS before INT.** Its order is (name, level, characterClass, str, dex, con, wis, intelligence, cha, ...), unlike the usual STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, so positional calls easily swap the two. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3345-3350. *Fix:* use named arguments (wis:, intelligence:) or TestHelpers.CreateStats.
- **CHR-033** · **Low** — **TryShiftCurrentSize reports success without committing a partial shift.** When a multi-step shift hits Fine or Colossal, it returns from inside the loop (true once a step was taken) without assigning CurrentSizeCategory, so the size stays unchanged. All current callers shift one step, so this is latent. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:5614-5642. *Fix:* commit the partial shift before returning (or return false) and report the delta.

## Performance

- **CHR-034** · **Med** — **EnsureMulticlassDataInitialized defeats the class-level cache and rewrites Level.** It nulls _classLevelCache on every call and runs from hot getters (BaseAttackBonus, GetClassLevel, saves; 16 call sites in CharacterStats, 12 elsewhere), so each GetClassLevel rebuilds its dictionary. It also resets Level to sum(ClassLevels) + PendingLevelUps, silently undoing writes to Stats.Level. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:355-401 (cache nulled at 358, Level at 383). *Fix:* invalidate the cache only when ClassLevels changes, and treat Level as derived.
