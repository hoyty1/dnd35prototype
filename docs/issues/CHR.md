# Known issues: Characters, feats and classes (CHR-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CHR-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Level-up feat selection drops Weapon Focus weapon choices.** CharacterCreationManager.ApplySelectedFeats (also used by LevelUpUI) adds only feat names and never copies FeatSelectionUI.GetAllSelectedWeapons() into stats.WeaponFocusWeapons, so a first Weapon Focus taken at level-up gives +0 and a second one for a new weapon is lost. Creation copies the weapons correctly. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterCreation/CharacterCreationManager.cs:221-237; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:168-171. *Fix:* add each selected weapon to stats.WeaponFocusWeapons in the onConfirmed callbacks.
- **CHR-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Class auto-granted feats are applied only at construction.** CharacterStats.InitFeats (which starts with Feats.Clear()) runs only from the constructor (CharacterStats.cs:4579) and test configs, so multiclassing into Monk or Wizard grants no Improved Unarmed Strike or Scribe Scroll. Only the ranger's Track and Endurance are granted by class level at level-up (`RangerRules.GrantAutomaticFeats`, called from ApplyPendingLevelUp, CHR-020). Calling InitFeats later would wipe selected feats. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2745-2762, 4579, 4750. *Fix:* add an additive per-class-level hook (for example ICharacterClass.GrantLevelFeatures) called from ApplyPendingLevelUp for Monk and Wizard as well.
- **CHR-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Feats are bare names: no per-weapon choice, no stacking, inert NPC parameters.** Feats is a `HashSet<string>`, so Toughness and Extra Turning cannot stack, Weapon Specialization and the Greater feats cover every Weapon Focus weapon, and Improved Critical all weapons. NPC entries such as 'Weapon Focus (longsword)' or 3.0 'Expertise' are copied verbatim and do nothing (Multiattack is read by its bare name since 2026-10-07: `CharacterStats.GetNaturalAttackSequencePenalty`, CMB-102; the animal companion's `AnimalCompanionData.HasMultiattack` (effective druid level 9+) is set but read by nothing and never becomes that feat, beyond the level 1-8 scope). Weapon Focus never applies to a natural attack: the natural sequence in `CharacterController.FullAttack` builds its modifier with no weapon, so `FeatManager.GetWeaponFocusBonus(stats, null)` looks up "Unarmed Strike", and the wolf's bare 'Weapon Focus' with `WeaponFocusChoice` "Bite" does nothing: in Play mode (scenario `rules/maneuver-freetrip`, 2026-10-07) the MM wolf bites at +2, not the MM's +3. The CRE-045 audit (2026-10-08) wrote the MM's natural-weapon Weapon Focus and Improved Natural Attack entries in the 'Name (param)' form, inert until this is fixed. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:136-160, 326-335; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:730-736. *Fix:* model feats as instances with a parameter and count, parse 'Name (param)' for NPCs, and tie weapon feats to one weapon.
- **CHR-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Augment Summoning boosts fields nothing reads.** FeatManager.ApplyAugmentSummoningBonuses adds +4 to BaseSTR and BaseCON, pre-racial creation inputs that STRMod and CONMod ignore, so summons get only the +2 HP per HD. Phase2SpecializedTacticsTests asserts on BaseSTR/BaseCON and enshrines the bug. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:866-876; Assets/Scripts/Tests/Feats/Phase2SpecializedTacticsTests.cs:251-270. *Fix:* apply the +4 to the effective STR and CON and assert STRMod/CONMod in the test.
- **CHR-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Some bonuses still bypass the shared stacking code.** Since 2026-10-09 (owner directive: strict RAW) every spell effect registers its attack, weapon damage, save, skill, speed and ability bonuses with its type in `CharacterStats.Bonuses`, totals follow `BonusStacking` (PHB p.171-172), item and Strength domain enhancement to an ability score count only beyond a spell enhancement bonus (`EnhancementBeyondSpells`), and luck, morale and speed enhancement bonuses no longer stack. Left: Death Knell (+2 Strength, PHB p.217) and Divine Power (+6 Strength) add to the STR field directly, so they stack with Bull's Strength and an item; dodge bonuses (Dodge feat, Haste, racial dodge) are summed by hand (the Dodge feat and Haste AC are dropped when DEX is denied, by a condition or by the attack itself, since 2026-10-14, `CharacterStats.DodgeACBonus`, CMB-028; fighting defensively's dodge bonus, added per attack, is not); touch AC omits WondrousInsightACBonus; ring, wondrous and class skill bonuses are summed by hand (`GetSpellSkillModifier`, `GetWondrousSkillModifier`) outside the ledger. ## Development traps

- **CHR-068** · **Med** — **Assigning `CharacterStats.BaseAttackBonus` is silently ignored for any character with class levels.** The setter writes `_baseAttackBonus`, but the getter returns `BaseAttackBonusOverride` or the racial-HD progression plus the sum over `ClassLevels` first, and reads the backing field only when none of those apply. The positional constructor's `bab` argument and test writes such as `Stats.BaseAttackBonus = 20` therefore do nothing for a classed character; the GrappleDamageRulesTests helpers relied on it, which made several of their checks dice-dependent until they switched to `BaseAttackBonusOverride` on 2026-10-07 (TST-027). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3249-3277 (getter and setter), 4544 (constructor). *Fix:* have tests set `BaseAttackBonusOverride` or build a character of the right level, and make the setter warn when `ClassLevels` exist. Do not simply redirect the setter to the override: the constructor's `bab` would then replace class BAB for every PC.
- **CHR-029** · **Med** — **FeatBenefit values look authoritative but are mostly unread.** FeatDefinitions fills about 55 FeatBenefit fields, but production code reads only SkillBonuses, RequiresWeaponChoice and Description. Feat effects are hard-coded HasFeat("<name>") checks, so editing a value such as Weapon Focus's AttackBonus changes nothing. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/Feat.cs:158-230; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:372. *Fix:* drive feat effects from FeatBenefit, or strip the unused fields.
- **CHR-030** · **Med** — **HasFeat is a case-sensitive magic-string lookup.** There are no FeatNames or ClassNames constants and about 200 HasFeat("literal") calls (146 outside tests); HasFeat is `Feats.Contains` on a `HashSet<string>` with the default comparer (plus the ranger combat-style checks), so a mis-cased or misspelled name silently returns false. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2448 (Feats), 2457-2462 (HasFeat). *Fix:* add FeatNames (and ClassNames) constants and construct Feats with StringComparer.OrdinalIgnoreCase.
- **CHR-031** · **Med** — **Two IsDead/IsUnconscious APIs with different meanings.** The IsDead pair (HP <= -10 vs HPState.Dead) agrees unless HP is written without SyncHPStateFromCurrentHP. CharacterStats.IsUnconscious (0 >= HP > -10) counts Disabled and Diehard characters and misses nonlethal and Unconscious-condition cases; it has 5 non-test references (3 gameplay: WallOfFireAreaEffect twice and GameManager.LootCollection; 2 debug logs). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:4436-4442; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:2256, 2308. *Fix:* use the CharacterController properties for gameplay, and fix or delegate Stats.IsUnconscious.
- **CHR-032** · **Med** — **The positional CharacterStats constructor puts WIS before INT.** Its order is (name, level, characterClass, str, dex, con, wis, intelligence, cha, ...), unlike the usual STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, so positional calls easily swap the two. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:4483-4488. *Fix:* use named arguments (wis:, intelligence:) or TestHelpers.CreateStats.
- **CHR-033** · **Low** — **TryShiftCurrentSize reports success without committing a partial shift.** When a multi-step shift hits Fine or Colossal, it returns from inside the loop (true once a step was taken) without assigning CurrentSizeCategory, so the size stays unchanged. All current callers shift one step, so this is latent. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:6779-6807. *Fix:* commit the partial shift before returning (or return false) and report the delta.

## Performance

- **CHR-034** · **Med** — **EnsureMulticlassDataInitialized defeats the class-level cache and rewrites Level.** It nulls _classLevelCache on every call and runs from hot getters (BaseAttackBonus, GetClassLevel, saves; 18 call sites in CharacterStats, 53 elsewhere, 50 of them in tests), so each GetClassLevel rebuilds its dictionary. It also resets Level to sum(ClassLevels) + PendingLevelUps, silently undoing writes to Stats.Level. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:460-513 (cache nulled at 463, Level at 495). *Fix:* invalidate the cache only when ClassLevels changes, and treat Level as derived.
