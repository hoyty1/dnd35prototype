# Known issues: Characters, feats and classes (CHR-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CHR-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Level-up feat selection drops Weapon Focus weapon choices.** CharacterCreationManager.ApplySelectedFeats (also used by LevelUpUI) adds only feat names and never copies FeatSelectionUI.GetAllSelectedWeapons() into stats.WeaponFocusWeapons, so a first Weapon Focus taken at level-up gives +0 and a second one for a new weapon is lost. Creation copies the weapons correctly. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterCreation/CharacterCreationManager.cs:221-237; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:168-171. *Fix:* add each selected weapon to stats.WeaponFocusWeapons in the onConfirmed callbacks.
- **CHR-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Class auto-granted feats are applied only at construction.** CharacterStats.InitFeats (which starts with Feats.Clear()) runs only from the constructor and test configs, so multiclassing into Monk or Wizard grants no Improved Unarmed Strike or Scribe Scroll, and a Ranger reaching level 3 by level-up never gets Endurance. Calling it later would wipe selected feats. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2084-2100, 3442. *Fix:* add an additive per-class-level hook (for example ICharacterClass.GrantLevelFeatures) called from ApplyPendingLevelUp.
- **CHR-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Feats are bare names: no per-weapon choice, no stacking, inert NPC parameters.** Feats is a `HashSet<string>`, so Toughness and Extra Turning cannot stack, Weapon Specialization and the Greater feats cover every Weapon Focus weapon, and Improved Critical all weapons. NPC entries such as 'Weapon Focus (longsword)' or 3.0 'Expertise' are copied verbatim and do nothing (Multiattack is read by its bare name since 2026-10-07: `CharacterStats.GetNaturalAttackSequencePenalty`, CMB-102; the animal companion's `AnimalCompanionData.HasMultiattack` (effective druid level 9+) is set but read by nothing and never becomes that feat, beyond the level 1-8 scope). Weapon Focus never applies to a natural attack: the natural sequence in `CharacterController.FullAttack` builds its modifier with no weapon, so `FeatManager.GetWeaponFocusBonus(stats, null)` looks up "Unarmed Strike", and the wolf's bare 'Weapon Focus' with `WeaponFocusChoice` "Bite" does nothing: in Play mode (scenario `rules/maneuver-freetrip`, 2026-10-07) the MM wolf bites at +2, not the MM's +3. The CRE-045 audit (2026-10-08) wrote the MM's natural-weapon Weapon Focus and Improved Natural Attack entries in the 'Name (param)' form, inert until this is fixed. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:136-160, 326-335; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:730-736. *Fix:* model feats as instances with a parameter and count, parse 'Name (param)' for NPCs, and tie weapon feats to one weapon.
- **CHR-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Augment Summoning boosts fields nothing reads.** FeatManager.ApplyAugmentSummoningBonuses adds +4 to BaseSTR and BaseCON, pre-racial creation inputs that STRMod and CONMod ignore, so summons get only the +2 HP per HD. Phase2SpecializedTacticsTests asserts on BaseSTR/BaseCON and enshrines the bug. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:866-876; Assets/Scripts/Tests/Feats/Phase2SpecializedTacticsTests.cs:251-270. *Fix:* apply the +4 to the effective STR and CON and assert STRMod/CONMod in the test.
- **CHR-010** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Some bonuses still bypass the shared stacking code.** Since 2026-10-09 (owner directive: strict RAW) every spell effect registers its attack, weapon damage, save, skill, speed and ability bonuses with its type in `CharacterStats.Bonuses`, totals follow `BonusStacking` (PHB p.171-172), item and Strength domain enhancement to an ability score count only beyond a spell enhancement bonus (`EnhancementBeyondSpells`), and luck, morale and speed enhancement bonuses no longer stack. Left: Death Knell (+2 Strength, PHB p.217) and Divine Power (+6 Strength) add to the STR field directly, so they stack with Bull's Strength and an item; dodge bonuses (Dodge feat, Haste, racial dodge) are summed by hand (the Dodge feat and Haste AC are dropped when DEX is denied, by a condition or by the attack itself, since 2026-10-14, `CharacterStats.DodgeACBonus`, CMB-028; fighting defensively's dodge bonus, added per attack, is not); touch AC omits WondrousInsightACBonus; ring, wondrous and class skill bonuses are summed by hand (`GetSpellSkillModifier`, `GetWondrousSkillModifier`) outside the ledger. The Rage spell was a third case (its +2 Strength and Constitution went straight to the score fields, were never taken back when it ended, and a second Rage added +2 more); it is fixed (2026-10-09): `ApplyRageSpellBuff` goes through `StatusEffectManager.AddEffect`, which registers the morale bonuses to Strength and Constitution and the +1 morale on Will, removes them with the effect and applies the same-spell rule (`BonusStackingRulesTests.TestRageSpell`). The barbarian's rage gives an untyped +4 to Strength and Constitution (only its Will bonus is morale, PHB p.25), so it stacks with the spell's morale bonuses to those scores, as RAW allows. Not verified in Play mode. *Evidence:* `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Effects/EffectService.cs` (`ApplyDeathKnellBonus`), `Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager_Spells_D.cs` (Divine Power), `Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs` (`ArmorClass`, `TouchArmorClass`, `GetSkillBonus`). *Fix:* register these as ledger entries (abilities through `StatusEffectManager` or `SyncAbilityScoreWithBonuses`, dodge AC as `BonusTarget.ArmorClass` dodge entries dropped when DEX is denied) and read item skill bonuses through `BonusStacking`.
- **CHR-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Healing goes to nonlethal damage first.** CharacterStats.HealDamage (16 call sites) removes nonlethal damage first and heals lethal damage only with the remainder (RAW: both by the full amount, PHB p.146). The stabilization half of this entry was fixed on 2026-10-14 (with CORE-038): a dying character now has a 10% chance on d% to become stable each round, else it loses 1 hit point (PHB p.145; `CharacterController.ProcessEndOfTurnHPState`, checked by `rules/combat-end-tie-victory` and `rules/combat-end-tie-defeat`). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs (HealDamage); Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/Paladin/LayOnHandsRules.cs (`HealingNeeded` sums lethal and nonlethal damage to match `HealDamage`, so Lay on Hands spends 10 points on 5 lethal plus 5 nonlethal damage). *Fix:* heal nonlethal and lethal damage independently by the full amount (no house rules, owner directive 2026-10-09), and make `LayOnHandsRules.HealingNeeded` max(lethal, nonlethal) in the same change.
- **CHR-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **MaxHP is not re-derived when CON changes.** MaxHP is stored and adjusted by ad hoc deltas: CON damage and drain do not change HP, and a level-up CON increase gives no retroactive HP. Rage, GrantInherentBonus, the Amulet of Health, the generic CON buff and Bear's Endurance scale by Hit Dice (`GetHitDice`, since CHR-071 on 2026-10-08) (their bonuses are even, so bonus/2 is the modifier change); what remains is that the HP are mutated rather than derived. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2119-2133, 2385-2404; Assets/Scripts/UI/CharacterSheet/LevelUpUI.cs:627-645. *Fix:* compute CON HP as a derived TotalMaxHP term, (CONMod - baseCONMod) * HitDice, instead of mutating MaxHP.
- **CHR-013** · **Low** · Player-visible — **AlignmentHelper.IsWithinOneStep accepts diagonal steps.** It returns diffLC <= 1 && diffGE <= 1, so LG counts as one step from TN (also for cleric summon limits), and DeityAlignmentRestrictionTests enshrines this. - **CHR-014** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Rolled skill checks omit the familiar bonus; no synergies, Craft or Profession.** CharacterStats.RollSkillCheck skips GetFamiliarSkillModifier, which GetSkillBonus includes, so the character-sheet roll can differ from the displayed bonus. - **CHR-015** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Level-up save preview is wrong.** LevelUpCalculator.EstimateProjectedBestSave takes the highest class base save instead of the sum, and New*Save omits the luck, resistance, competence and familiar bonuses, Divine Grace and the racial bonus on all saves that Old*Save (the shared `CharacterStats.GetSaveTotal`, CHR-018) includes. ## Development traps

- **CHR-068** · **Med** — **Assigning `CharacterStats.BaseAttackBonus` is silently ignored for any character with class levels.** The setter writes `_baseAttackBonus`, but the getter returns `BaseAttackBonusOverride` or the racial-HD progression plus the sum over `ClassLevels` first, and reads the backing field only when none of those apply. The positional constructor's `bab` argument and test writes such as `Stats.BaseAttackBonus = 20` therefore do nothing for a classed character; the GrappleDamageRulesTests helpers relied on it, which made several of their checks dice-dependent until they switched to `BaseAttackBonusOverride` on 2026-10-07 (TST-027). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:2608-2635 (getter and setter), 3610 (constructor). *Fix:* have tests set `BaseAttackBonusOverride` or build a character of the right level, and make the setter warn when `ClassLevels` exist. Do not simply redirect the setter to the override: the constructor's `bab` would then replace class BAB for every PC.
- **CHR-029** · **Med** — **FeatBenefit values look authoritative but are mostly unread.** FeatDefinitions fills about 55 FeatBenefit fields, but production code reads only SkillBonuses, RequiresWeaponChoice and Description. Feat effects are hard-coded HasFeat("<name>") checks, so editing a value such as Weapon Focus's AttackBonus changes nothing. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/Feat.cs:158-230; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:372. *Fix:* drive feat effects from FeatBenefit, or strip the unused fields.
- **CHR-030** · **Med** — **HasFeat is a case-sensitive magic-string lookup.** There are no FeatNames or ClassNames constants and about 150 HasFeat("literal") calls (142 outside tests); HasFeat is Feats.Contains on a `HashSet<string>` with the default comparer, so a mis-cased or misspelled name silently returns false. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:1875-1878. *Fix:* add FeatNames (and ClassNames) constants and construct Feats with StringComparer.OrdinalIgnoreCase.
- **CHR-031** · **Med** — **Two IsDead/IsUnconscious APIs with different meanings.** The IsDead pair (HP <= -10 vs HPState.Dead) agrees unless HP is written without SyncHPStateFromCurrentHP. CharacterStats.IsUnconscious (0 >= HP > -10) counts Disabled and Diehard characters and misses nonlethal and Unconscious-condition cases; it has 5 references. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3298-3314; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:4602-4648. *Fix:* use the CharacterController properties for gameplay, and fix or delegate Stats.IsUnconscious.
- **CHR-032** · **Med** — **The positional CharacterStats constructor puts WIS before INT.** Its order is (name, level, characterClass, str, dex, con, wis, intelligence, cha, ...), unlike the usual STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, so positional calls easily swap the two. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:3345-3350. *Fix:* use named arguments (wis:, intelligence:) or TestHelpers.CreateStats.
- **CHR-033** · **Low** — **TryShiftCurrentSize reports success without committing a partial shift.** When a multi-step shift hits Fine or Colossal, it returns from inside the loop (true once a step was taken) without assigning CurrentSizeCategory, so the size stays unchanged. All current callers shift one step, so this is latent. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:5614-5642. *Fix:* commit the partial shift before returning (or return false) and report the delta.
