# Known issues: Creatures, templates and summoning (CRE-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CRE-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Every swarm deals a flat 6 damage.** `AIService.RollSwarmDamage` returns `SwarmDamage` whenever it is above 0 and all six swarms set it to 6, so bat, rat and spider swarms always deal their 1d6 maximum and centipede, hellwasp and locust swarms (meant as 2d6/3d6) deal 6. RAW swarm damage is rolled (1d6 to 3d6), and `SwarmTraits.SwarmDamageCount` is never read. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1956-1960; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_B.cs:98-99 (bat), NPCDatabase_R.cs:46-47, NPCDatabase_S.cs:151-152, NPCDatabase_C.cs:376, NPCDatabase_H.cs:413, NPCDatabase_L.cs:539. *Fix:* set `SwarmDamage = 0` with the right `SwarmDamageDice`, and drop or wire up SwarmDamageCount.
- **CRE-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Celestial and fiendish templates change ability scores and gate SR by HD.** `OutsiderTemplateBase.ApplyStatAdjustments` raises STR, CON, CHA and WIS (celestial) or DEX (fiendish) but floors INT at 3 only for animals and magical beasts, and `ApplyMitigation` grants SR only at 8+ HD. RAW (MM 3.5): abilities unchanged except Int at least 3, SR HD+5 (max 25) at any HD; DR and energy resistance are correct. Flooring Int is not enough for a mindless base such as a vermin: MM p.31 and p.108 also make an animal or vermin with the template a magical beast (augmented), and a creature with an Int score is not mindless. The template copies `IsMindless` and the `ImmunityPresets.MindlessImmunities` preset unchanged and sets no `CreatureType`, so at spawn `GameManager.InitializeNPCFromDefinition` calls `CharacterStats.ApplyMindlessTrait(def.IsMindless)`, which sets INT to no score and immunity to mind-affecting effects, and `CharacterStats.IsImmuneToMindAffecting` grants the same immunity to the `vermin` type. A celestial or fiendish vermin summon (the giant fire beetle, monstrous centipedes and spiders, bombardier beetle, praying mantis and wasp, and since CRE-003 (2026-10-14) the Summon Monster II celestial giant bee, which until then was a dire bat clone with Int 2) is therefore immune to Sleep, Command, charms and the other mind-affecting spells, for PCs' and NPCs' summons alike, where RAW gives it Int 3 and no such immunity. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/CreatureTemplateFramework.cs:195-253 (`ApplyStatAdjustments`, `ApplyMitigation`); Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:933 (`stats.ApplyMindlessTrait(def.IsMindless)`); Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs (`ApplyMindlessTrait`, `IsImmuneToMindAffecting`). *Fix:* drop the ability bonuses, floor Int at 3 for every creature and grant SR HD+5 (cap 25) at every HD. When the floor gives a creature an Int score, clear `IsMindless` and the mindless immunity preset on the clone (keeping the vermin's other immunities only where the MM keeps them), and set the type of an animal or vermin base to Magical Beast with the augmented subtype, so neither `ApplyMindlessTrait` nor the vermin type check undoes it. Test with the celestial giant bee and a fiendish monstrous spider (Int 3, not mindless, affected by Sleep).

- **CRE-031** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Stench auras and grapple blood drain are configured but never used.** `ConfigureStenchAura` stores a DC and range (set for creatures in NPCDatabase_G, _N and _T) and `ConfigureBloodDrain` stores a `BloodDrainDefinition`, but `HasStenchAura` and `GetBloodDrainDefinition` have no readers. ## Development traps

- **CRE-022** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Creature IDs silently overwrite each other.** `NPCDatabase.Register` has no duplicate check and `Init` registers files in a hand-kept, non-alphabetical order, so the later entry wins in 5 collisions (dretch, skeleton_archer and 3 more). Two creatures also exist under two IDs used by different consumers (large_viper/viper_large, huge_monstrous_centipede/monstrous_centipede_huge). The `dretch` that wins (`RegisterDretch`) has Str 14 where MM p.42 gives 12 (the losing `RegisterSummonDretch` has 12), so every dretch, summoned or encountered, gets +1 to melee attack and damage rolls; SummonMonster3CreaturesTests fails on it (2026-10-07). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:18-62, 212-226; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:200, 542. *Fix:* warn on duplicate IDs, delete or rename the losers, and merge each double-ID creature into one ID or an explicit alias.
- **CRE-023** · **Med** — **Template adapters and `NPCDefinition.Clone` copy hand-picked fields.** The skeleton, zombie and lycanthrope adapters copy back only about 60 fields, losing the factories' clearing of auras and regeneration (latent today; since batch 12 step 2, 2026-10-28, they copy the breath weapons, frightful presence and engulf, which the factories clear). Clone is partly shallow and its SwarmTraits copy drops `SwarmDamageCount`; a new NPCDefinition field must be added to Clone, three `CopyDefinitionFields` methods and the factories. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonCreatureTemplate.cs:41-126; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:910-1034. *Fix:* have the adapters replace the definition wholesale and make Clone deep-copy reference fields.
- **CRE-024** · **Med** — **Monsters carry placeholder class levels.** 250 database entries use `CharacterClass = "Warrior"` for racial HD, which becomes a real Warrior level, so `IsWarrior` and `HasClass("Warrior")` are true for monsters (no production reader yet). Weapon and armor proficiency already tells the two apart (CHR-072, 2026-10-08): `NPCDefinition.ClassLevelsAreRacialHitDice` (derived: an empty class, or Warrior on a non-humanoid; set explicitly for the gnoll, bugbear, lizardfolk and troglodyte, humanoids with more than 1 racial HD, MM p.310) becomes `CharacterStats.RacialHitDiceStandInClass`, which the proficiency tables ignore; that marker could serve the rest of this fix. Skeletons and zombies get Fighter 1 from `EnsureMulticlassDataInitialized`, so `IsFighter`, `AISpellcastingStrategist.IsFrontliner` and WishExecutor treat them as fighters. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:468-476, 2322-2330, 2665, 6386-6391; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:90. Since CRE-004 (2026-10-08) BAB and base saves also skip the stand-in class (`CharacterStats.RacialHitDice` takes its levels with the type's progressions). *Fix:* use an explicit racial-HD marker for the class checks too, or skip the Fighter fallback when `RacialHitDiceStandInClass` is set.
- **CRE-025** · **Low** — **The creature count is only known at runtime.** `NPCDatabase.Init` registers about 30 partial files, generates dragons in a loop and adds aliases, so the authoritative number is the "[NPCDatabase] Initialized with N NPC types." log line. A static count (about 317 `Register(` calls, up to 60 dragons, plus aliases) suggests about 380-390 (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:20-61. *Fix:* quote the runtime log line instead of hard-coding a count in docs or tests.
- **CRE-033** · **Low** — **Creature type and tag spell "Magical Beast" two ways.** 26 definitions use `CreatureType = "Magical Beast"` and 4 use `"MagicalBeast"` (plus `BaseCreatureDefinitions`); `CreatureTags` mix both too. `CreatureTypeProgression` accepts both, and so does the ranger's favored enemy matching since 2026-10-14 (`RangerRules.MatchesFavoredEnemy` ignores spacing and case), but other exact-string checks and tag matching miss the other spelling. Runtime count via `NPCDatabase.AllNPCs` on 2026-10-03. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_C.cs:614, NPCDatabase_H.cs:766/820/874; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/BaseCreatureDefinitions.cs:30; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/CreatureTypeProgression.cs:111-112. *Fix:* normalise all data to "Magical Beast" (or compare via `CreatureTypeProgression`'s alias map).
