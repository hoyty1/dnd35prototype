# Known issues: Creatures, templates and summoning (CRE-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CRE-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The Greater Lion's Shield lion uses a summon tracker nothing ticks.** `LionsShieldBehavior.ActivateSummon` registers the dire lion only with `SummoningService`, whose `TickDurations` has no callers, not with `GameManager._activeSummons`. Since 2026-10-08 (ENC-001) the next encounter setup removes it: `GameManager.EnsureNPCPoolSize` takes every `NPCs` entry that is not an enemy pool slot off the grid and out of both summon trackers and destroys it, so the lion can no longer be taken over as an enemy of a 16+ creature encounter; it still lasts the whole fight it was called in. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Armor/LionsShieldBehavior.cs:241-253 (`ActivateSummon`); Assets/Scripts/Services/SummoningService.cs:257. *Fix:* register it through GameManager's own summon path (including initiative), then delete or adopt SummoningService. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: LionsShieldBehavior.ActivateSummon builds its stats in `LionsShieldBehavior.BuildSummonStats`, a third stat path. - **CRE-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Celestial and fiendish templates change ability scores and gate SR by HD.** `OutsiderTemplateBase.ApplyStatAdjustments` raises STR, CON, CHA and WIS (celestial) or DEX (fiendish) but floors INT at 3 only for animals and magical beasts, and `ApplyMitigation` grants SR only at 8+ HD. ## Development traps

- **CRE-022** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Creature IDs silently overwrite each other.** `NPCDatabase.Register` has no duplicate check and `Init` registers files in a hand-kept, non-alphabetical order, so the later entry wins in 5 collisions (dretch, skeleton_archer and 3 more).
