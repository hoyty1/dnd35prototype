# Known issues: Creatures, templates and summoning (CRE-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CRE-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **Templates are applied twice at encounter spawn.** Skeleton, zombie and lycanthrope factories record their own template in `AppliedTemplateIds`, and `GameManager.BuildEncounterDefinitionForSpawn` reapplies that list with no already-applied check. Skeletons get DEX +2 twice, zombies double HD again, most were-creatures re-add animal stats and dungeon celestial/fiendish entries stack their bonuses (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:2828-2859; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:246. *Fix:* split pending and applied template lists, or skip templates already applied.
- **CRE-002** · **High** · Player-visible — **Spawned NPCs have no alignment.** `GameManager.InitializeNPCFromDefinition` never copies `NPCDefinition.CharacterAlignment`, so about 160 aligned database entries spawn as `Alignment.None` (only some test encounters and Summon Monster set it by hand). Template smite, Holy Avenger, Protection from/Magic Circle against alignment and alignment-targeted spells never see ordinary monsters as evil or good. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:445-560, 320-442; Assets/Scripts/Combat/Special/TemplateSmiteSystem.cs:108. *Fix:* copy the alignment in InitializeNPCFromDefinition, then drop the redundant overrides.
- **CRE-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Summon aliases overwrite real Monster Manual creatures.** `NPCDatabase.RegisterSummonCreatureAliases` runs last in `Init` and replaces badger, riding_dog, owl, raven and giant_bee with clones of dire_badger, dog, eagle and the Large dire_bat. Summon Monster I-II, the creature_showcase preset and figurine/bag wondrous items get the wrong stat block. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:59, 238-269; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:135-161. *Fix:* delete those five aliases (keep wolf, the only new ID) and make `RegisterSummonAlias` skip existing IDs.
- **CRE-004** · **High** · Player-visible — **Spawned NPCs ignore class BAB and saves.** `InitializeNPCFromDefinition` always sets `UseCreatureTypeProgression`, so BAB and saves follow creature-type progressions (or overrides) and `NPCDefinition.BAB` is never read (human_paladin: BAB 5 in data, +3 spawned). `CreatureClassEngine.ApplyClassToDefinition` adds class HD, HP and CR but no effective BAB, saves, feats or skills, and replaces racial HP when `BaseHitDieHP = 0`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:482-488; Assets/Scripts/Character/CreatureClass/CreatureClassEngine.cs:140-175. *Fix:* apply class BAB/save progressions to class HD (e.g. via the overrides).
- **CRE-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The Greater Lion's Shield lion uses a summon tracker nothing ticks.** `LionsShieldBehavior.ActivateSummon` registers the dire lion only with `SummoningService`, whose `TickDurations` has no callers, not with `GameManager._activeSummons`. So it likely never expires or despawns after combat, never joins initiative and has no AI behavior entry (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Armor/LionsShieldBehavior.cs:286-293; Assets/Scripts/Services/SummoningService.cs:257. *Fix:* register it through GameManager's own summon path (including initiative), then delete or adopt SummoningService.
- **CRE-006** · **Med** — **Despawned summons pile up in `GameManager.NPCs`.** `RestorePartyAfterCombat` destroys the `_activeSummons` GameObjects but leaves them in `NPCs` and `_npcAIBehaviors`, a pool SceneBootstrap builds once. Destroyed entries accumulate across encounters behind Unity fake-null checks, and NPC indices drift out of step with `_npcAIBehaviors`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:868-889; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:364-369. *Fix:* remove each despawned summon from both lists, or prune null entries at encounter setup.
- **CRE-007** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Every swarm deals a flat 6 damage.** `AIService.RollSwarmDamage` returns `SwarmDamage` whenever it is above 0 and all six swarms set it to 6, so bat, rat and spider swarms always deal their 1d6 maximum and centipede, hellwasp and locust swarms (meant as 2d6/3d6) deal 6. RAW swarm damage is rolled (1d6 to 3d6), and `SwarmTraits.SwarmDamageCount` is never read. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1388-1392; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_B.cs:94-95. *Fix:* set `SwarmDamage = 0` with the right `SwarmDamageDice`, and drop or wire up SwarmDamageCount.
- **CRE-008** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Celestial and fiendish templates change ability scores and gate SR by HD.** `OutsiderTemplateBase.ApplyStatAdjustments` raises STR, CON, CHA and WIS (celestial) or DEX (fiendish) but floors INT at 3 only for animals and magical beasts, and `ApplyMitigation` grants SR only at 8+ HD. RAW (MM 3.5): abilities unchanged except Int at least 3, SR HD+5 (max 25) at any HD; DR and energy resistance are correct. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/CreatureTemplateFramework.cs:106-162, 196-203. *Fix:* drop the ability bonuses, floor Int for every creature and grant SR HD+5 (cap 25) at every HD.
- **CRE-009** · **Med** — **Undead use 3/4 BAB.** `CreatureTypeProgression` gives Undead `BABProgression.Medium` and only the skeleton and zombie templates override it, so allip, wight, lich, ghoul and other MM undead attack with too high a BAB (RAW: 1/2, poor). Their explicit BAB fields (wight BAB=2) are ignored at spawn. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/CreatureTypeProgression.cs:95; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:90-93. *Fix:* change Undead to `BABProgression.Poor`.
- **CRE-010** · **Low** — **noble_djinni has an unregistered class.** It sets `CharacterClass = "Outsider"`, so every `ClassRegistry.GetClass` lookup for it logs "[ClassRegistry] Class not found: Outsider"; it is reachable through the dungeon encounter tables and the Ring of Djinni Calling. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_G.cs:56-61; Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/ClassRegistry.cs:70-76. *Fix:* set `CharacterClass = "Warrior"` like the other MM monsters.
- **CRE-034** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Summon Monster I-IV lists deviate from the PHB.** SM II offers a fiendish Large monstrous scorpion where the PHB table (p.287) lists a Medium one (`monstrous_scorpion_medium` exists), and SM I-IV lack all PHB aquatic entries: celestial porpoise and fiendish octopus (I; `octopus` exists), fiendish squid and fiendish Medium shark (II), fiendish Large shark (IV; `large_shark` exists). The Summon Nature's Ally side of the aquatic gap is CRE-021. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:154-177 (`GetSummonMonsterIIOptions`, line 168), 129-153, 219-275. *Fix:* point SM II at `monstrous_scorpion_medium`, add the entries whose NPC ids exist, and add the missing aquatic NPCs (with an underwater/no-water placement rule).
- **CRE-035** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Eight creatures have no Challenge Rating.** A read-only runtime pass over `NPCDatabase.AllNPCs` on 2026-10-04 found a blank `ChallengeRating` on monstrous_centipede_medium, skeleton_warrior, zombie_shambler, dire_bear, brown_bear, wolf, octopus and tiger; the static definitions of all but wolf (an alias clone of wolf_pack_hunter) set no CR. Several spawn from the DMG dungeon tables and the summon lists. How XP, EL and random-encounter math treat a blank CR was not checked (CR parsing: CRE-027). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabaseCustom.cs:180, 262, 635, 717, 752; NPCDatabase_M.cs:364; NPCDatabase_O.cs:112; NPCDatabase.cs:240. *Fix:* add the MM CRs (and an `Init` warning for any blank CR).
- **CRE-036** · **Low** · Player-visible — **The MM blue slaad has pounce; the red slaad should.** `blue_slaad` sets `HasPounce = true`, but in the MM pounce belongs to the red slaad (MM p.229), not the blue (MM p.230). `red_slaad` also has it, which is correct. Found by the 2026-10-07 tactics inventory verification. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_B.cs:615-640; NPCDatabase_R.cs:108-130. *Fix:* remove `HasPounce` from `blue_slaad`.

## Unreachable or stubbed features

- **CRE-011** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Lycanthropes are hybrid-only and the curse does nothing.** Form shifting and curse progression are not implemented, and natural lycanthrope bites carry `DiseaseType.Lycanthropy`, which has no `DiseaseDatabase` entry, so `CharacterController.ExposeToDisease` silently skips the DC 15 save that the SpecialAbilities text advertises. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/LycanthropeTemplate.cs:285-292; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3682-3686. *Fix:* add a Lycanthropy DiseaseData entry (DC 15 Fort) or a curse handler, and log unknown on-hit disease types.
- **CRE-012** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Several Monster Manual abilities are placeholders or text only.** The Beholder's "Antimagic Cone" is a Frightened aura (SaveDC 0, 0 rounds) that suppresses no magic, the Vargouille's Shriek frightens instead of paralyzing, and the juvenile Arrowhawk has no electricity ray; orc and kobold light sensitivity and the summoned Unicorn's SLAs and magic circle are text only. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_B.cs:796-830, NPCDatabase_V.cs:322-345, NPCDatabase_U.cs:63-108. *Fix:* implement an antimagic zone or remove the aura, give the Vargouille a Paralyzed aura, and implement or relabel the Unicorn's abilities.
- **CRE-013** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Gibbering Mouther Ground Manipulation does nothing.** `AIService.ActivateTerrainManipulation` only logs and never marks difficult terrain, and it runs after every AI advance move, so the "activates ..." message repeats each turn; the CharacterController duration tick and active check are never called. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:547-549, 3238-3255; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3795-3826. *Fix:* mark cells within `RadiusFeet` with `GameManager.SetAreaDifficultTerrain` (clearing them on expiry or move), activate once and tick at turn start.
- **CRE-014** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Swarm poison is never applied.** The swarm-damage poison rider in `AIService` only logs "X is exposed to Y's poison!" and never calls the existing `CharacterController.ApplyPoison(poisonId)`; in current data only the spider swarm (`medium_spider_poison`) is affected. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:1374-1379; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_S.cs:151-152. *Fix:* call `victim.ApplyPoison(traits.PoisonId)`, respecting poison immunity.
- **CRE-015** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Dragon spell-like abilities are never used.** `DragonData.PopulateSpellcastingData` fills per-age `SpellLikeAbilityIds`, but `NPCDatabase_Dragons` reads only `SorcererSpellIds`, so spawned dragons have no SLAs; the `DragonTypeTemplate` lists are never read either. Several SLAs are stand-ins anyway (Suggestion as charm_person, Corrupt Water as create_water). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/DragonData.cs:104-148, 1519-1563; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_Dragons.cs:103-107. *Fix:* map SpellLikeAbilityIds into uses-per-day SLAs and delete the template-level lists.
- **CRE-016** · **Low** — **Dragon spellcasting and age range are approximate.** `GetSpellsForCasterLevel` takes the first 6/9/13/16 entries of a flat list that starts with six cantrips, ignoring the sorcerer spells-known table (CL1 knows only cantrips), and `DragonAgeCategory` ends at Adult. The added Sorcerer level makes `stats.Level` HD + CL, and the CL is found by a nested scan over all dragons. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/DragonData.cs:33-41, 1274-1345; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:681-710. *Fix:* store spells per level, pick them by the sorcerer table, and store the CL on NPCDefinition.
- **CRE-017** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Mephit breath weapons and most creature SLAs are text only.** `MephitBase` sets no `BreathWeapon` or spells and models fast healing 2 as `RegenerationAmount = 2` (that code acts like fast healing), and about 30-35 "spell-like" mentions across 13 creature files (hags, slaadi, yuan-ti and others) are description only. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_M.cs:87-148; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3844-3864. *Fix:* give mephits `BreathWeaponDefinition` entries (already used by Hell Hounds and dragons) and add a FastHealing field.
- **CRE-018** · **Low** — **Creature trample is not implemented.** `NPCDefinition.HasTrample` is set only for the Treant and never read, and creature trample is SpecialAbilities text only (Treant, giant beetles, Gorgon, wild-shape forms); `FeatManager.HasTrample` and `MountedCombatSystem` implement the unrelated mounted Trample feat. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:523; Assets/Scripts/Character/Feats/FeatManager.cs:945. *Fix:* implement a creature trample action that uses the field, or remove it.
- **CRE-019** · **Low** — **Summon tables stop at level IV.** `SummonMonsterLists` has Summon Monster and Summon Nature's Ally tables for I-IV only (SM V is empty); harmless today because only levels 1-4 of both spells exist, and the stub staff spell `summon_monster_ix` would not match the `summon_monster_(\d+)` regex anyway. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:96-124; Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Staves/StaffDatabase.cs:657. *Fix:* add SM and SNA V+ tables before adding those spells, and use a numeric id for any staff that casts SM IX.
- **CRE-020** · **Low** — **The DMG NPC-by-level system has no entry point.** `QuickSpawnSystem`, `NPCTemplateDatabase`, `TemplateData` (70 templates) and 2 more classes are called only from NPCTemplateSystemTests. Only Wizard 5 fills `SpellsPrepared`, and `NPCTemplateAIConfigurator.ConfigureConsumables` maps potions, scrolls and wands but never weapons or armor. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Utilities/QuickSpawnSystem.cs:18-131; Assets/Scripts/AI/NPCTemplateAIConfigurator.cs:40-43, 124-145. *Fix:* wire QuickSpawnSystem into encounter generation with weapon and armor mapping, or mark it experimental.
- **CRE-021** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Summon Nature's Ally lists lack 6 aquatic creatures (45/51 entries).** SNA I-II lack Porpoise, Medium Shark and Squid, and SNA IV lacks Sea Cat, Huge Shark and Juvenile Tojanida, all for want of an NPCDatabase entry. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:494, 517, 519, 568, 569, 572. *Fix:* add the NPCDatabase entries.
- **CRE-030** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Vampire spell list is unusable because it is a Fighter.** The vampire definition is `CharacterClass = "Fighter"`, so `stats.IsSpellcaster` is false and `InitializeNPCFromDefinition` never adds a `SpellcastingComponent` despite its `KnownSpellIds`. Re-routing it to a casting routine (AI-002) alone will not make it cast. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_V.cs:246-248; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCSetup.cs:716-718. *Fix:* Give the vampire its MM spell-like abilities (or a caster class) instead of a Fighter spell list, then fix routing with AI-002.
- **CRE-031** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Stench auras and grapple blood drain are configured but never used.** `ConfigureStenchAura` stores a DC and range (set for creatures in NPCDatabase_G, _N and _T) and `ConfigureBloodDrain` stores a `BloodDrainDefinition`, but `HasStenchAura` and `GetBloodDrainDefinition` have no readers. No stench save or grapple CON drain ever happens; only the separate natural-attack blood drain works. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:508-511, 525-528, 3769-3792; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_G.cs:521. *Fix:* Process stench as an aura at turn start and apply blood drain while grappling, or remove the fields.
- **CRE-032** · **Low** · Player-visible — **Summon command menu has no effect; ProtectCaster is unreachable.** The command menu (AttackNearest/ProtectCaster) is offered only for controllable summons, which take player turns, while `CurrentCommand` is read only by the AI summon turn. AI summons always keep the default AttackNearest, so ProtectCaster never applies. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:2492-2507, 3792-3801; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:305-306; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:91-97, 183-189. *Fix:* Either offer the menu for AI-controlled player summons or remove it for controllable ones.
- **CRE-037** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Energy riders on natural attacks are data only.** Seven creature files set `BonusElementalDamageDice/Count/Type` on `NaturalAttackDefinition` entries (for example the hell hound's fiery bite), but no damage code reads them; the only other writer is `UndeadTemplateUtils`, which zeroes them. So these attacks deal physical damage only. Noted in `docs/systems/CREATURES.md` without an ID until now. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:74-76, 119-120; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/UndeadTemplateUtils.cs:27-28; `grep -rn BonusElementalDamage Assets/Scripts`. *Fix:* add the rider as a typed damage packet in the natural-attack damage path (through `ApplyIncomingDamage` so resistances apply).

## Development traps

- **CRE-022** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Creature IDs silently overwrite each other.** `NPCDatabase.Register` has no duplicate check and `Init` registers files in a hand-kept, non-alphabetical order, so the later entry wins in 5 collisions (dretch, skeleton_archer and 3 more). Two creatures also exist under two IDs used by different consumers (large_viper/viper_large, huge_monstrous_centipede/monstrous_centipede_huge). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:18-62, 212-226; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:200, 542. *Fix:* warn on duplicate IDs, delete or rename the losers, and merge each double-ID creature into one ID or an explicit alias.
- **CRE-023** · **Med** — **Template adapters and `NPCDefinition.Clone` copy hand-picked fields.** The skeleton, zombie and lycanthrope adapters copy back only about 33-37 fields, losing the factories' clearing of breath weapons, auras and regeneration (latent today). Clone is partly shallow and its SwarmTraits copy drops `SwarmDamageCount`; a new NPCDefinition field must be added to Clone, three `CopyDefinitionFields` methods and the factories. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonCreatureTemplate.cs:41-93; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:641-739. *Fix:* have the adapters replace the definition wholesale and make Clone deep-copy reference fields.
- **CRE-024** · **Med** — **Monsters carry placeholder class levels.** 250 database entries use `CharacterClass = "Warrior"` for racial HD, which becomes a real Warrior level, so `IsWarrior` and `HasClass("Warrior")` are true for monsters (no production reader yet). Skeletons and zombies get Fighter 1 from `EnsureMulticlassDataInitialized`, so `IsFighter`, `AISpellcastingStrategist.IsFrontliner` and WishExecutor treat them as fighters. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:355-387, 3345-3356; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:73-74. *Fix:* use an explicit racial-HD marker, or skip the Fighter fallback under `UseCreatureTypeProgression`.
- **CRE-025** · **Low** — **The creature count is only known at runtime.** `NPCDatabase.Init` registers about 30 partial files, generates dragons in a loop and adds aliases, so the authoritative number is the "[NPCDatabase] Initialized with N NPC types." log line. A static count (about 317 `Register(` calls, up to 60 dragons, plus aliases) suggests about 380-390 (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:20-61. *Fix:* quote the runtime log line instead of hard-coding a count in docs or tests.
- **CRE-033** · **Low** — **Creature type and tag spell "Magical Beast" two ways.** 26 definitions use `CreatureType = "Magical Beast"` and 4 use `"MagicalBeast"` (plus `BaseCreatureDefinitions`); `CreatureTags` mix both too. `CreatureTypeProgression` accepts both, but exact-string checks such as ranger favored enemies (`FavoredEnemyData`, "Magical Beast") and tag matching miss the other spelling. Runtime count via `NPCDatabase.AllNPCs` on 2026-10-03. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_C.cs:594, NPCDatabase_H.cs:733/785/837; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/BaseCreatureDefinitions.cs:29; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/CreatureTypeProgression.cs:109-110; Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/Ranger/FavoredEnemyData.cs:37. *Fix:* normalise all data to "Magical Beast" (or compare via `CreatureTypeProgression`'s alias map).

## Dead code, duplication and tech debt

- **CRE-026** · **Low** — **Creature data is about 14.6k lines of hand-written C# with no validator.** There are no data assets and no database validator (largest: NPCDatabaseCustom.cs 1612, DragonData.cs 1569, NPCDatabase_G.cs 1327 lines), so stat errors surface only at runtime or in a few hand-run tests. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase*.cs. *Fix:* add a static validator over all NPCDefinitions (HD vs HP, BAB vs progression, CR parse, attacks) or migrate to data assets.
- **CRE-027** · **Low** — **Four CR-string parsers.** `CRCalculator.CRToFloat` (culture-sensitive; `FloatToCR` has no 1/10), `ChallengeRatingUtils.TryParse`, `CustomEncounterBuilderUI.ParseCRToFloat` and a private `LycanthropeTemplate.ParseCR`. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/CreatureClass/CRCalculator.cs:23-50; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:839-910. *Fix:* keep ChallengeRatingUtils and delete the others.
- **CRE-028** · **Low** — **`StatArrayApplier` is test-only.** Its elite/nonelite arrays, priority stats, racial modifiers and ability increases are referenced only by NPCTemplateSystemTests. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/CreatureClass/StatArrayApplier.cs:20-145. *Fix:* wire it into NPC generation or delete it with its tests.
- **CRE-029** · **Low** — **Unused CR and class-engine methods.** `CRCalculator.CalculateNPCCR`, `GetStandardCR`, `FloatToCR` and five `CreatureClassEngine` methods (`CalculateClassSave`, `CalculateTotalBAB` and 3 more) are test-only; `CalculateTotalCR` and `CalculateTotalSave` have no callers. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/CreatureClass/CRCalculator.cs:23-122; Assets/Scripts/Character/CreatureClass/CreatureClassEngine.cs:16-121. *Fix:* delete them or use them for class-leveled NPCs.
