# Known issues: Creatures, templates and summoning (CRE-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **CRE-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **Templates are applied twice at encounter spawn.** Skeleton, zombie and lycanthrope factories record their own template in `AppliedTemplateIds`, and `GameManager.BuildEncounterDefinitionForSpawn` reapplies that list with no already-applied check. Skeletons get DEX +2 twice, zombies double HD again, most were-creatures re-add animal stats and dungeon celestial/fiendish entries stack their bonuses (not verified in Play mode). Since CHR-071 (2026-10-08) HD-gated rules read the definition's HitDice, so the doubled HD also reach Turn Undead, Sleep and the other HD limits: a spawned bugbear zombie counts 12 HD instead of 6, which only an 8th-level cleric with the best turning check (level + 4, PHB p.159) can reach (the XFail in `rules/class-progression`). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:2830-2861; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:246. *Fix:* split pending and applied template lists, or skip templates already applied.
- **CRE-003** · **High** · Player-visible — **Summon aliases overwrite real Monster Manual creatures.** `NPCDatabase.RegisterSummonCreatureAliases` runs last in `Init` and replaces badger, riding_dog, owl, raven and giant_bee with clones of dire_badger, dog, eagle and the Large dire_bat. Summon Monster I-II, the creature_showcase preset and figurine/bag wondrous items get the wrong stat block. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:59, 238-269; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:135-161. *Fix:* delete those five aliases (keep wolf, the only new ID) and make `RegisterSummonAlias` skip existing IDs.
- **CRE-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **The Greater Lion's Shield lion uses a summon tracker nothing ticks.** `LionsShieldBehavior.ActivateSummon` registers the dire lion only with `SummoningService`, whose `TickDurations` has no callers, not with `GameManager._activeSummons`. So it likely never expires or despawns after combat, never joins initiative and has no AI behavior entry (not verified in Play mode). Since 2026-10-08 (ENC-001) the next encounter setup removes it: `GameManager.EnsureNPCPoolSize` takes every `NPCs` entry that is not an enemy pool slot off the grid and out of both summon trackers and destroys it, so the lion can no longer be taken over as an enemy of a 16+ creature encounter; it still lasts the whole fight it was called in. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Equipment/Armor/LionsShieldBehavior.cs:241-253 (`ActivateSummon`); Assets/Scripts/Services/SummoningService.cs:257. *Fix:* register it through GameManager's own summon path (including initiative), then delete or adopt SummoningService. *Parity audit 2026-10-07:* Parity audit 2026-10-07: LionsShieldBehavior.ActivateSummon builds its stats in `LionsShieldBehavior.BuildSummonStats`, a third stat path. Since CRE-004 (2026-10-08) it sets BAB and saves through the same `CreatureTypeProgressionDatabase.ApplyToStats` as the spawn (racial HD by the animal type), but it skips feats, DR, ER, SR, immunities and equipment, unlike InitializeNPCFromDefinition used for spell summons.

Noted in `docs/systems/CREATURES.md` without an ID until now. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:74-76, 119-120; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/UndeadTemplateUtils.cs:27-28; `grep -rn BonusElementalDamage Assets/Scripts`. *Fix:* add the rider as a typed damage packet in the natural-attack damage path (through `ApplyIncomingDamage` so resistances apply).
- **CRE-039** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Monster special abilities have AI-only executors, so a controllable creature with the same data cannot use them.** Breath weapons (Dragon profile only), auras and gazes (`ProcessAuraAbility`), free-action spittle and ranged specials, engulf, bombardier acid spray and frightful presence run only inside `AIService.ExecuteNPCTurn` and `NPCPerformAttack`. PC-cast summons are controllable and take the PC UI, which has no action for any of them. So a summoned hell hound (SM III) or celestial bombardier beetle (SM II) never breathes or sprays, and controllable creatures never project auras. These executors also resolve saves and attack rolls outside the shared pipelines (AI-040, AI-041, AI-006); their damage goes through the shared `GameManager.DealDamage` since 2026-10-09 (SPL-004). The Gibbering aura spares the mouther's allies, which MM p.126 does not allow. RAW: PHB p.285 summoned creatures use their abilities; MM p.307-309. Not verified in Play mode. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/AIService.cs:170-192, :539, :600, :629, :735, :780, :3173-3398; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.NPCTurns.cs:1028-1116, :1134-1137, :1485-1696; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Resolution/GameManager.SpellCasting.cs:311. *Fix:* a monster-ability service with actor-agnostic executors (action cost, SavingThrowResolver, `ApplyIncomingDamage`). The PC panel lists each ability the creature has, the AI selects the same executors, and passive auras run from the shared turn-start hook. Found by the PC/NPC parity audit (docs/systems/PC_NPC_PARITY.md); not verified in Play mode.

## Development traps

- **CRE-022** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Creature IDs silently overwrite each other.** `NPCDatabase.Register` has no duplicate check and `Init` registers files in a hand-kept, non-alphabetical order, so the later entry wins in 5 collisions (dretch, skeleton_archer and 3 more). Two creatures also exist under two IDs used by different consumers (large_viper/viper_large, huge_monstrous_centipede/monstrous_centipede_huge). The `dretch` that wins (`RegisterDretch`) has Str 14 where MM p.42 gives 12 (the losing `RegisterSummonDretch` has 12), so every dretch, summoned or encountered, gets +1 to melee attack and damage rolls; SummonMonster3CreaturesTests fails on it (2026-10-07). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:18-62, 212-226; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Components/SummonMonsterLists.cs:200, 542. *Fix:* warn on duplicate IDs, delete or rename the losers, and merge each double-ID creature into one ID or an explicit alias.
- **CRE-023** · **Med** — **Template adapters and `NPCDefinition.Clone` copy hand-picked fields.** The skeleton, zombie and lycanthrope adapters copy back only about 33-37 fields, losing the factories' clearing of breath weapons, auras and regeneration (latent today). Clone is partly shallow and its SwarmTraits copy drops `SwarmDamageCount`; a new NPCDefinition field must be added to Clone, three `CopyDefinitionFields` methods and the factories. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonCreatureTemplate.cs:41-93; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:641-739. *Fix:* have the adapters replace the definition wholesale and make Clone deep-copy reference fields.
- **CRE-024** · **Med** — **Monsters carry placeholder class levels.** 250 database entries use `CharacterClass = "Warrior"` for racial HD, which becomes a real Warrior level, so `IsWarrior` and `HasClass("Warrior")` are true for monsters (no production reader yet). Weapon and armor proficiency already tells the two apart (CHR-072, 2026-10-08): `NPCDefinition.ClassLevelsAreRacialHitDice` (derived: an empty class, or Warrior on a non-humanoid; set explicitly for the gnoll, bugbear, lizardfolk and troglodyte, humanoids with more than 1 racial HD, MM p.310) becomes `CharacterStats.RacialHitDiceStandInClass`, which the proficiency tables ignore; that marker could serve the rest of this fix. Skeletons and zombies get Fighter 1 from `EnsureMulticlassDataInitialized`, so `IsFighter`, `AISpellcastingStrategist.IsFrontliner` and WishExecutor treat them as fighters. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Stats/CharacterStats.cs:355-387, 3345-3356; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/SkeletonTemplate.cs:73-74. Since CRE-004 (2026-10-08) BAB and base saves also skip the stand-in class (`CharacterStats.RacialHitDice` takes its levels with the type's progressions). *Fix:* use an explicit racial-HD marker for the class checks too, or skip the Fighter fallback when `RacialHitDiceStandInClass` is set.
- **CRE-025** · **Low** — **The creature count is only known at runtime.** `NPCDatabase.Init` registers about 30 partial files, generates dragons in a loop and adds aliases, so the authoritative number is the "[NPCDatabase] Initialized with N NPC types." log line. A static count (about 317 `Register(` calls, up to 60 dragons, plus aliases) suggests about 380-390 (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:20-61. *Fix:* quote the runtime log line instead of hard-coding a count in docs or tests.
- **CRE-033** · **Low** — **Creature type and tag spell "Magical Beast" two ways.** 26 definitions use `CreatureType = "Magical Beast"` and 4 use `"MagicalBeast"` (plus `BaseCreatureDefinitions`); `CreatureTags` mix both too. `CreatureTypeProgression` accepts both, but exact-string checks such as ranger favored enemies (`FavoredEnemyData`, "Magical Beast") and tag matching miss the other spelling. Runtime count via `NPCDatabase.AllNPCs` on 2026-10-03. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase_C.cs:594, NPCDatabase_H.cs:733/785/837; Assets/Scripts/Character/Templates/BaseCreatureDefinitions.cs:29; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/CreatureTypeProgression.cs:109-110; Assets/Scripts/Character/Classes/Ranger/FavoredEnemyData.cs:37. *Fix:* normalise all data to "Magical Beast" (or compare via `CreatureTypeProgression`'s alias map).
