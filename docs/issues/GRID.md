# Known issues: Grid and movement (GRID-)

## Bugs and rules deviations

- **GRID-001** · **High** · Player-visible — **Player Withdraw ignores double-move range and first-square AoO exemption.** The `avoidThreats` branch of `MovementService.FindPath` drops the range override, `suppressFirstSquareAoO` and other flags and calls `SquareGrid.FindSafePath`, which uses `Stats.MoveRange`. Withdraw is cut to single-move range despite a full-range preview and leaving the first square provokes AoOs; AI withdraw works. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:184-195; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:545-575. *Fix:* call `FindPathAoOAware` with the range override, size and flags, then `AnalyzePathForAoOs` with suppression; retire `FindSafePath`.
- **GRID-002** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Difficult terrain does not slow movement.** Cells marked by `GameManager.SetAreaDifficultTerrain` (Grease, Web and 4 more) are ignored by A*, the highlight and the `ExecuteMovement` budget, since only the uncalled `MovementService.GetMovementCost` doubles cost; the set just forbids 5-ft steps and blocks charge lanes. Grease's +1 per step is charged at execution only, so a previewed path can end early. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:147-162; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.CombatActions.cs:788-798. *Fix:* one step-cost function, including terrain and Grease, for A*, trimming, highlight, preview and execution.
- **GRID-003** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Movement highlight includes squares that cannot be reached.** `MovementService.CalculateMovementRange` offers every cell within straight-line distance that passes `CanPlaceCreature` and is not intact Wall of Ice, ignoring intervening walls, Wall of Force, spheres and detours. On click, `FindPathAoOAware` trims or cuts the path, so the character silently stops short. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:117-145; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:380-445. *Fix:* compute the highlight with a Dijkstra flood using the A* rules and costs, and reject unreachable clicks.
- **GRID-004** · **Med** · Player-visible — **5-ft step validation and the step itself disagree.** `MovementService.IsValidAdjacentStepDestination` checks only Wall of Ice (not Wall of Force, corners or spheres), while `CharacterController.FiveFootStep` rejects any `IsOccupied` cell, counting corpses and the mover itself. Corpse squares are offered and then fail, and Large or bigger creatures cannot step +x, +y or +x+y. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:339-375; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:11935-11977. *Fix:* use `CanPlaceCreature(target, size, this)` in `FiveFootStep` and add the missing wall and sphere checks to the validator.
- **GRID-005** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Tiny and smaller creatures take a full square and have 5-ft reach.** `SizeCategory.GetSpaceWidthSquares` returns 1 and `GetNaturalReachFeet` 5 ft for Fine, Diminutive and Tiny, and only swarms may share squares, so they attack from adjacent squares. RAW they have 0-ft reach and must enter the target's square, provoking an AoO. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/SizeCategory.cs:80-90, 120-128; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:488-500. *Fix:* allow several Tiny-or-smaller occupants per cell (already supported for swarms) and set reach 0 with attack-in-square and an AoO on entry.
- **GRID-006** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Overlapping difficult-terrain spells clear each other's cells.** `MovementService` stores difficult terrain as a plain `HashSet<Vector2Int>` without reference counting, so when one of two overlapping effects ends it removes cells the other still covers. No map terrain feeds the set, and `ClearDifficultTerrain` has no callers. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:15, 377-395; Assets/Scripts/Spell/Special/GameManager_Grease.cs:395-402. *Fix:* reference-count cells per source and add a data-driven map terrain source.
- **GRID-007** · **Low** · Player-visible — **A* misjudges threats and start squares for multi-square or overlapping movers.** The +200 `THREAT_COST` applies only when the base square is threatened while `AnalyzePathForAoOs` checks the whole footprint, so Large+ creatures can take unweighed AoOs; `FindPathAoOAware` returns an empty path when `CanPlaceCreature(start)` fails, which can strand a swarm or grappler (not verified in Play mode). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:241-243, 357-363; Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:225. *Fix:* penalise any threatened footprint square and ignore the mover and its overlapping occupants in the start check.
- **GRID-008** · **Low** — **Reach of 15 ft or more over-threatens diagonals.** `ThreatSystem` measures threat with Chebyshev distance (diagonal = 1), so a (3, 3) offset counts as 3 squares; RAW uses the 1-2-1 count (20 ft) except for 10-ft reach. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs:115-133. *Fix:* use `SquareGridUtils.GetDistance` for reach of 3+ squares and keep the 10-ft special case.

## Unreachable or stubbed features

- **GRID-009** · **Med** · Player-visible — **No line of sight; line of effect applies only to area spells.** `CharacterController.CanSee` checks only total concealment and `LineOfEffectService` is used only by `AoESystem`, so Wall of Ice and Wall of Force do not block attacks or single-target spells and Wall of Fire never blocks sight or targeting. Only Resilient Sphere blocks attacks and AoOs. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:8785-8794; Assets/Scripts/Spell/AreaEffects/AoESystem.cs:790-907. *Fix:* add a `LineOfEffectService` check to attack and single-target spell validation, highlighting and AI targeting.

## Development traps

- **GRID-010** · **Low** — **`SquareGrid.Instance` is overwritten by every `Awake` and never cleared.** Six test suites (e.g. OverrunRulesTests, GustOfWindRulesTests) create and `DestroyImmediate` extra grids, leaving `Instance` on a destroyed grid in Play mode; impact is limited because it is only a fallback when `GameManager.Instance` or `GameManager.Grid` is null. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:12, 25-28; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3126. *Fix:* assign only if null and clear in `OnDestroy`, or save and restore it in the tests.

## Performance

- **GRID-011** · **Low** — **Debug.Log runs on per-frame movement-preview hot paths.** During movement selection `Update` calls `UpdatePathPreview` every frame without a hovered-cell cache, which logs, runs A* (logging each result) and gathers threatened squares (`ThreatSystem` logs per character), flooding the console and costing frame time. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:2212-2213, 11137-11238; Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/ThreatSystem.cs:97, 110. *Fix:* gate verbose logs behind a debug flag or `[Conditional]` wrapper and recompute the preview only when the hovered cell changes.
- **GRID-012** · **Low** — **`SquareGrid.ClearCreatureOccupancy` scans every cell on each `RefreshGridOccupancy`.** That runs on every movement step and size change; 400 cells on the default 20x20 grid is negligible but grows with map size. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:166-178; Assets/Scripts/Character/Controller/CharacterController.cs:3290-3296. *Fix:* track each creature's occupied cells and clear only those.

## Dead code, duplication and tech debt

- **GRID-013** · **Med** · Player-visible — **Movement legality and cost are implemented in several disagreeing places.** A* checks spheres, Wall of Ice/Force and diagonal corners, `MovementService` only Wall of Ice, and `FiveFootStep` and `ExecuteMovement` no walls; cost is computed three ways (A* 2/3, `CalculatePathCost` 1-2-1, `ExecuteMovement` 1 plus Grease). This drift causes GRID-002, GRID-003 and GRID-004. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:332-354, 397-434; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGridUtils.cs:201-229. *Fix:* one `IsStepLegal(from, to, mover)` and one step-cost function shared by A*, highlighting, the 5-ft step and `ExecuteMovement`.
- **GRID-014** · **Low** — **Unused grid helpers and a duplicate Chebyshev name.** `SquareGrid.HighlightSpellRange`, `ClearSpellRangeHighlight` (clears all highlights), `SquareGridUtils.GetDirection` and `DirectionNames` are unused; `ChebyshevDistance` and `GetChebyshevDistance` are aliases, both in use (about 14 and 11 call sites). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGrid.cs:593-624; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareGridUtils.cs:36, 151, 171-176. *Fix:* delete the unused helpers and settle on one name.
- **GRID-015** · **Low** — **Unused `MovementService` members.** `GetMovementCost`, `GetSquaresInRange`, `IsPositionValid` and `ClearDifficultTerrain`, plus the `GameManager.GetSquaresInRange`/`GetMovementCost` wrappers. *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Services/MovementService.cs:37, 94, 147, 393; Assets/Scripts/_Core/GameManager.cs:10827-10828. *Fix:* delete, or route range and cost queries through them.
- **GRID-016** · **Low** — **Unused hex-era aliases in `RangeCalculator`** (`FeetPerHex`, `HexesToFeet`, `RangeInfo.HexDistance` and 3 more). *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/Combat/Core/RangeCalculator.cs:176-190, 210-211. *Fix:* delete.

## Misleading code comments and strings

- **GRID-017** · **Low** — **Hex-grid wording survives in comments on the square grid.** The SceneBootstrap layout diagram says "HEX GRID AREA", the `HighlightType.SpellRange` comment mentions hexes, and the NPCDatabase header says "hex RPG prototype". *Evidence:* Assets/Scripts/_Core/SceneBootstrap.cs:16; Assets/Scripts/Grid/SquareCell.cs:400; Assets/Scripts/Character/Creatures/NPCDatabase.cs:7. *Fix:* change to "square grid".
